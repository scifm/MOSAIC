**Coverage, family `orbit-amp` (closed; follow-up moved to `joint-cma`).** This family was amplitude (α) on orientation-resolved joint (±G,±R) C6 orbits, plus the first α + β-odd combinations.

Findings:
1. **Full-shell α hides large first-order slopes.** The self-mirror (SM) and mirror-pair (PM) orbits of one (S,T) can have slopes of opposite sign. On S1T1, SM− gains while PM+ gains, so the full-shell product (≈ SM + PM) cancels them.
2. **Best α-only results.** Best single direction: S4T1-SM −0.05, at s21 1.368 / r46 1.517. A 7-orbit first-order combination at 1.5× reaches 1.703 / 2.065, and the 9-orbit slope-weighted combination at 0.6× reaches 1.760 / 2.208. All are PASS, and M2^(k) is unchanged to < 0.5 %.
3. **These gains are beyond M2.** The matched-density controls give R = 0.9995 / 0.9976 (s21 and r46 agree).
4. **α combined with swarm-e's β-odd O1T1− breaks the s21 ≈ 1.73 ceiling.** The best was 87e3 (b = 0.102 per entry plus combo7 at 1.0×) at 2.915 / 3.179. This led directly to swarm-a's 2fc4 and to the `joint-cma` lineage (429f, 3b76, 0bd5).
5. **The Laughlin basin is narrow on outer orbits.** At 0.05 per entry, every S9 direction and every S4/S3 PM direction FAILs.
