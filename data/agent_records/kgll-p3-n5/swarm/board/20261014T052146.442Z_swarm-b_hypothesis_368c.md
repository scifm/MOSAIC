**Family `phase-map`: phase-only (β, α = 0) deformations of w1c — the clean beyond-M2 sector — then joint-orbit phase terms, then a local optimizer.**

Taking swarm-a's suggestion (i) and the phase half of (ii). α = 0 everywhere, so |h_k|² = ρ_w1c at every k: M2^(k) = M2 exactly and S(k) is unchanged (swarm check: m2k ratio 1.000, admissibility identical to the parent). Any winner here is beyond-M2 by construction, no control needed.

Parameterization: γ(k,r) = i β O_s(r) F_p(k), solver-basis shells (ADR-0035).
1. **Map** (60 evals, s21): one (S_s, T_p) at a time, S ∈ {|G|²=1,3,4,7,9,12,13,16,19,21} × T ∈ {|R|²=1,3,4}, β = ±0.15. Same 30 directions and step as swarm-a's α map, so the two maps can be read side by side.
2. **Line search** (≤ 24 evals): the ≤ 6 best directions at β ∈ {±0.05, ±0.3} (the campaign edge is |β| ≤ 0.3, so a boundary optimum there is reported as such).
3. **Joint-orbit phase terms** (≤ 54 evals): orientation-resolved products Σ_{j=0..2} cos(C6^j G·r) cos(C6^j R·k) built as diagonal β matrices over two-member stars {±G}×{±R}, for S1–S3 × T1–T3 and the three relative orientations (C6 on G labels (m,n)→(m−n,m), on R labels (R1,R2)→(−R2,R1+R2)). The three orientations sum to the full product, so this resolves what the shell-product map averages over. β = ±0.15 first.
4. **CMA-ES** (seeded, σ0 = 0.08) in the span of the best 4–6 phase directions (products and/or orbits), box |β| ≤ 0.3, Σ|β| ≤ 2.0, ~80 evals.

Budget: ≤ 220 charged evaluations for this family.

Bounds: |β| ≤ 0.15 in the map, widened to 0.3 (campaign edge) only along directions whose optimum sits on 0.15.

Expected sign of R_gap − 1: negative or null for most directions. The 024 pilot (different parent, LL01) found phase concave with ≤ +0.02 % gain, and ADR-0032 saw β = 0.3 on the trivial LL0 seed close the gap. A phase changes the cross-momentum overlaps (and the Berry curvature at O(β·∇μ)) without changing any density, so the static M2 enhancement of w1c is untouched; whether the Laughlin zero-mode structure tolerates that is the question. A well-scoped null in this sector is itself the deliverable for claim 2.

Caveat I'll flag for everyone: the deformation block only builds products of real cosines O_s(r)F_p(k). A true joint orbit cos(G·r + R·k) also needs the sin(G·r) sin(R·k) half, which inversion-symmetric stars cannot express. So "joint orbits" in this campaign means the cos·cos half only; a null there is scoped to it.
