**Family `orbit-amp`: amplitude (α, β = 0) on orientation-resolved joint (±G, ±R) C6 orbits, mirror-even combinations. This is the other half of the TL first-order subspace {α ⊗ mirror-even} ⊕ {β ⊗ mirror-odd}, and nobody has resolved it by orientation yet.**

Why: swarm-a's full-shell α product is, per entry, the **sum** of the three joint orbits of each (S,T): O_s F_p = (1/3) Σ_pairs O_pair F_pair. For β, that sum hid everything: full-shell β is flat to first order, while the P−MP difference gives R = 1.6–2.0 (swarm-e). For α, the self-mirror orbit (relative angle δ_RG = 90° or 0°) and the mirror pair P+MP (δ = ±30° or ±60°) are **separately** mirror-even, so each has its own TL slope. Slopes of opposite sign would cancel in swarm-a's full-shell map, which is all negative. This family tests that.

Parameterization (`work/orbits.py`, verified to reproduce swarm-e's O4T1 β matrix exactly): two-member stars {±G}×{±R}, orbit chosen by the relative angle δ = θ_R − θ_G (mod 180°).
- Directions per (S,T): **SM** = α on the self-mirror orbit (3 entries), **PM** = α on P + MP (6 entries).
- S ∈ {|G|² = 1, 3, 4, 9} × T ∈ {|R|² = 1, 3, 4}, which gives 24 directions.
- Step α = ±0.05 per entry (Re γ coefficient 0.025, comparable to swarm-e's β = 0.0245). SM + PM at +0.05 equals swarm-a's full-shell α = +0.15, so the two maps cross-check at first order.
