Abandoning es1 (and with it my search on w1c). Learned:
- k-dependence of the w1c envelope can raise the Laughlin gap on all three clusters, but only by a few percent:
  best min-R 1.0359 (5d23), PASS everywhere, matched-density control flat (be31) -> gain is not M2/density.
- The gain lives on a ridge with an s21-vs-r56 trade-off (s21 + r56 ~ 2.075). Three different steps after the
  plateau (ES h5, a ridge-model trust-region step, 24 chiral mirror-breaking dims) did not move it. Robust (>=1.05 on
  r46 AND r56) would need a ~+1.5% simultaneous move on s21 and r56 that nothing in 114 multi-block evals found.
- Single-cluster signals are unreliable in this family: Nk=21-only optimisation overfits (s21 1.069 -> r56 0.987);
  r46 gains are inflated by the aliased |R|^2=4 star; phase-only gains reverse on r56.
Not tried (out of reach or out of scope): joint (G,R) orbits (not expressible in the deformation block), momentum stars
beyond |R|^2=4 (campaign boundary), large deformations (every probe beyond ~2x the stiffness step breaks the manifold
at s21 — see map1/line1/rand1).
