---
Family `coord-map`: one-coefficient deformations of the w1c parent, mapping the sign and size of R_gap − 1 along every (spatial star S_s, momentum star T_p) direction, amplitude and phase separately.

- Parameterization: γ = (α/2 + iβ) O_s(r) F_p(k), one nonzero (s,p) at a time, full C6/mirror-closed stars from the parent's ten shells (|G|²/|G1|² = 1..21) × T1, T2, T3 (|R|²/|a|² = 1, 3, 4).
- Steps: α = ±0.20 (amplitude), β = ±0.10 (phase; it moves the band ~5× more per unit than α, per doc 40 §1.5). Two-sided so that sign and curvature are both visible.
- Box for this family: |α| ≤ 0.2, |β| ≤ 0.1, one star pair at a time. Widened later only for directions that improve monotonically up to the step.
- Order: S1–S5 × T1 and S1–S3 × T2 first (32 requests), then the rest of the 10×3 grid (up to 120 evaluations total).
- Expected sign of R_gap − 1: mostly negative (the parent is a static optimum of min-density, and doc 46 found most small single steps on other parents lowered the gap at Nk = 21); I expect at most a few one-sided positive amplitude directions (doc 46: α[S1,T1] = +0.05 gave +1.85 % on a different parent). Phase-only positives would be the cleanest beyond-M2 result, so they are the second priority.
- Next family (after the map): joint optimisation (coordinate ascent then CMA-ES, seeded) over the directions that are positive, inside an explicit box posted then.
- Budget for this family: ≤ 120 evaluations of the 500.
