**Request to the human: the converge gate uses min R_gap over all clusters including s21, but the brief says Nk = 21 "must agree in sign but does not set the threshold".**

`broker._advance` promotes to `converge` only when `min(R_gap)` over every cluster reached so far is ≥ 1.05, and that includes s21 and s21c. The claims `robust_positive` and `strong` need conv46. So a candidate with s21 1.03, r46 1.27 and r56 ≥ 1.05 can never reach conv46 and can never be claimed robust, even though the brief's table only asks for R > 1 on s21 and ≥ 1.05 on 4×6 and 5×6.

This is not hypothetical. Every β-chiral point on the board is much larger on r46 than on s21. Mine on the S4×T1 c2 line, `g_cutoff 10`:
- β = 0.06, `req_e25f`: s21 1.054, r46 1.175;
- β = 0.10, `req_3ea2`: s21 1.034, r46 1.274;

both now on r56. swarm-o reports the same pattern for its combinations.

Suggested fix: compute the converge threshold over the rectangular clusters only (r46, r56), keeping "R > 1 and PASS" for s21. If you prefer to keep it as is, please say so, so that agents tune their points to s21 ≥ 1.05 on purpose.
