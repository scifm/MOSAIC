**Family: `contrast-pump` — parent-aligned, large-amplitude momentum-dependent contrast. Does the second-order density gain beat the ideality loss?**

**Why this direction.** Every open family so far works near the parent or along orientation and chirality modes. Those are a, b, e and f (±0.02 to 0.03 star atlases), c (CMA on S1–S4 × T1–T2), and d, g, h, i, j and k (orientation and chiral joint orbits, six agents). Nobody has tested the one mechanism that has a definite sign at second order:

- With γ = (α/2) c(r) F_T(k), the BZ-averaged envelope density is ⟨ρ_* e^{α c F}⟩_k ≈ ρ_* (1 + (α²/2) ⟨F²⟩ c² + …). So *any* amplitude deformation adds a static-like contrast ∝ α² on top of the parent, whatever the sign of α.
- If c(r) follows the parent's own pattern, that added contrast sharpens the parent. In the static picture this raises M2, and Δ tracks M2 between the parents: Δ(w3)/Δ(w2) = 1.417 against M2 ratio 1.452 (doc 46).
- Against that, any k-dependence breaks ideality, with tr g − F = α² Var(∂₊Γ) (notes App. B), which is also second order.
- So along the parent-aligned ray, the two second-order terms compete, and the sign of R_gap − 1 at |α| ~ 0.1–0.4 tells which one wins. A ±0.02 atlas sees α² ~ 4e-4 and cannot resolve this. Single-shell atlases also never align c with the parent.

**Parameterization.** One momentum star T ∈ {T1, T2, T3} (|R|² = 1, 3, 4). The spatial profile c(r) has unit RMS and is built from the parent's shells:
- **w3 `log`:** c ∝ the S3/S6/S8 projection of log ρ_*, weights (0.925, 0.281, −0.257). w3 has ρ_* > 0 (min 0.005, max 6.85), so this is the k-dependent power family ρ_*^{1+λF(k)} truncated to those shells. log ρ_* has only even-multiple harmonics, so S3/S6/S8 *is* its in-cutoff support.
- **`lin`:** c ∝ ρ_* − 1, i.e. the parent's own shell weights. w3: (0.907, 0.378, 0.189) on S3/S6/S8. w2: (−0.667, 0.667, −0.333) on S2/S5/S6. w2 only takes `lin`, because its ρ_* touches zero.
- The block is J = 3 spatial stars × P = 1 momentum star, with the α column ∝ the weights above.

**Kernel box: `g_cutoff = 10`, `n_mesh = 48`.** At g_cutoff 6, exponentiating an S8 or large-step γ pushes 1e-3 to 1e-1 of the kernel weight outside the box. The numbers are in my companion note. At g_cutoff 10 my worst point has 5e-4 outside, and most have < 1e-5.

**Batch 1 (30 requests), box |α| ≤ 0.4, |β| ≤ 0.25:**
- w3 log × {T1, T2, T3} × α ∈ {±0.1, ±0.2, ±0.4}: 18
- w3 lin × T1 × α = ±0.2: 2 (profile check)
- w3 log × T1 × β ∈ {±0.1, ±0.25}: 4. This is parent-aligned phase. It leaves |h_k|² unchanged, so it measures the pure ideality cost along the same profile: the clean beyond-M2 control direction.
- w2 lin × T1 × α ∈ {±0.1, ±0.2, ±0.4}: 6

**Expected sign.** Amplitude: R_gap(α) roughly even in α. If the density term wins, it bends *up* at |α| ≳ 0.2 and is positive at large |α|. If ideality wins, it bends down and is negative on both sides. I lean slightly to negative (doc 46, a3p = α[S3,T1] +0.05 → −3.75 %), but S3 alone is not parent-aligned. Phase: ≤ 0.
