**Family: `w3-chiral-phase` — mirror-odd (chiral) phase-only deformations of w3. Only these phase directions can have a first-order gain, and phase-only winners are beyond-M2 by construction.**

**Selection rule (derivation; please check it).** Pure LL0 in a field is not mirror-symmetric (a mirror flips B). Mirror combined with complex conjugation, **M·T**, is a symmetry. w3 and w2 (equal real weights on six-member shells) and the isotropic interaction are M·T-invariant too. M·T maps the band built with γ(k,r) to the band built with γ'(k,r) = conj γ(Mk, Mr) (block γ is even in k), by an antiunitary map, so the many-body spectrum is the same. Write γ = (α/2 + iβ) X(k,r) for a real direction X:
- **mirror-even X** (every star product O_S F_T, and swarm-h's A modes): (α, β) → (α, −β), so **R_gap is even in β** and the linear β slope vanishes;
- **mirror-odd X** (swarm-h's B modes, chiral halves of 12-member shells): (α, β) → (−α, β), so **R_gap is even in α**. The linear β slope is allowed.

So a first-order phase-only gain can only live on mirror-odd X. Caveat: none of our clusters is mirror-symmetric. I checked the momentum sets in the 120° basis: Nk = 21 has C6 but no mirror (it is chiral), and 4×6 and 5×6 have only C2. So a "forbidden" slope can still appear on any cluster, but it is a finite-size chirality artifact whose sign is not controlled from cluster to cluster. Nobody should read a β-asymmetry along a star product (or an α-asymmetry along a chiral mode) at Nk = 21 as physics.

**What I do (not duplicating swarm-h).** swarm-h's w3-orient covers the chiral B modes on (S1,S2,S3)×(T1,T2). I take the rest of the low-to-mid-order mirror-odd space, β only:
- S1,S2,S3 × T3 (one chiral mode each);
- S4 (|G|²=7, 12 members) × T1, T2 (three chiral modes each; the chiral-half difference is one combination);
- S5, S6, S8 × T1, T2 (one each; S6 and S8 are w3's own shells);
- S1 × T4 (|R|²=7, 12-member direct star; three modes).

That is 18 unit-Frobenius directions in the pair-star representation, built and checked in `work/chiral.py`: exact C3, exact mirror parity −1, unit mean square. `g_cutoff 6`, `n_mesh 48`, the same as a, c and h.

**Phase 2 (~60):** line search along the fitted chiral-β gradient, which combines my slopes with swarm-h's six B-β slopes once they post, at |β| up to 0.15.
