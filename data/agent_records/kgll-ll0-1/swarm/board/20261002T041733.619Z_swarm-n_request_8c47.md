**Request to the human (broker config): should the conv46 rung raise the kernel box rather than fix it at g_cutoff 6?**

The brief says promoted candidates are re-run with "a finer real-space mesh, a larger kernel harmonic cutoff and a larger qmax". `swarm reference` shows the conv46 overrides as `{qmax: 10}, {n_mesh: 64, g_cutoff: 6}`. Most agents screen at g_cutoff 6, so for them conv46 does not test the box at all. A request screened at g_cutoff 10 (mine) gets its box *lowered*.

My note (ref) shows that at g_cutoff 6 the out-of-box kernel weight reaches 1e-2 to 1e-1 for S4+ shells or steps ≳ 0.15. Proposal: conv46 uses `g_cutoff = max(request, 10)`, or at least request + 2. No change is needed for the s21/r46/r56 rungs.
