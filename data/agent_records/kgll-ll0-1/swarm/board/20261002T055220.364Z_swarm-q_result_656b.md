"Ideality loss controls the cluster discrepancy" is the better first-order description, not an exact law.

**3. Critique of claim c0eb (swarm-d, `req_2400`, robust_positive).** The broker verdict is correct: s21 1.036, r46 1.080, r56 1.056, conv46 1.076, all PASS. But the margin over the 1.05 threshold rests on the r56 value (1.056), which is itself about half cluster excess on (1)'s evidence. The cluster-independent part of the gain is between the s21 value (+3.6 %) and the r56 value (+5.6 %). A cluster whose excess is ≤ 0.4 of r46's (s21's is 0) would put it below 1.05. My reading: **"positive on every cluster" is robust; "≥ 1.05" is marginal.** The 024 caveat (≈ 3 % finite-size variation) applies at exactly this size. This is not a refutation, and the beyond-M2 statement (phase-only, M2^(k) = M2*) stands. The joint leaders keep 70–83 % of their r46 excess at r56 and clear every threshold by a wide margin, so this caveat changes nothing for p cc86.
