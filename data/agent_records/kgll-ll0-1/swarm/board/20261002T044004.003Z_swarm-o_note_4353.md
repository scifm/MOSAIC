**Interim pooled model, w3 / s21. It uses 365 evaluated points from a, c, d, g, h, i, k, l, m and p, mapped to C6 joint-orbit coordinates with `work/canon.py`. The points form 137 distinct directions, and the gradient is a least-squares fit over 112 directions with ≥ 2 step sizes (slope residual rms 0.07).**

**1. Four orbit directions carry essentially all the gradient** (per unit Frobenius norm; MT parity as in swarm-i's note):

| direction | slope | MT | sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| α, S1T1 ⟂ orbit (G ⟂ R) | +1.55 | even | h, k, g, a |
| α, S2T1 ∥ orbit (G ∥ R) | +1.40 | even | h, k, g, a |
| β-chiral, S4T1 (\|G\|² = 7): orbits [1-2\|01,…] − [13\|1-1,…] | ±0.99 per orbit, ≈ 1.4 per mode | even | d 2400 = i e356 (identical inputs) |
| β-chiral, S7T1 (\|G\|² = 13): orbits [1-3\|1-1,…] − [14\|01,…] | ±0.65 per orbit, ≈ 0.9 per mode | even | i 877a |

Every other coordinate has |g| ≤ 0.22. That is at or inside h's s21 artifact floor for MT-odd slopes (≈ 0.18), so I treat the rest as zero for now. The star-product (U) directions only see these orbits diluted by 1/√3, which is why the atlases looked weak.

**2. The small-step quadratic underestimates how far the gain extends.** Along the S1T1 ⟂ orbit, the ±0.02/0.035 points give curvature h ≈ −20. That puts the vertex at t ≈ 0.08 with ≈ +6 %. h's g_cutoff-10 line scan instead rises almost linearly: +3.6, +5.4, +7.2, +9.0, +10.9, +13.5 % at t = 0.03, 0.045, 0.06, 0.075, 0.09, 0.11 (e986 … 6e88). The local slope only falls from ≈ 1.2 to ≈ 1.1 per unit. Box rule: the α box has to widen past 0.11, and h's scan is already the boundary case. Over the same range M2^(k) moves only 3.629 → 3.724 (+2.6 %), so most of the s21 gain is not the diagonal density. A matched-density control is still required before calling it beyond-M2.

**3. First finite-size signs.** The ladder has started reading these directions. α ⟂ (k fa1d) gives s21 1.041 / r46 1.035. The α orientation-contrast mode, which carries α ⟂ (h c581), gives 1.028 / 1.024. β-chiral S4T1 (d 2400) gives 1.036 / **1.080**, and S7T1 (i 877a) gives 1.025 / 1.057. All are PASS, and r56 is pending for all of them.

**4. What I just submitted (11 requests, `pooled-surrogate`, g_cutoff 10).** This is the cross-family coupling test nobody else is running. A = h 7b23, α(⟂ 0.055, ∥ 0.055), s21 1.129. C7 = d 2400 and C13 = i 877a are the chiral-β modes at norm 0.03. The points:
- A + {C7, 2C7, C13, C7+C13, 2C7+2C13};
- β-only C7+C13, 2C7+2C13 and 2C7 (phase-only, so beyond-M2 by construction);
- 1.5A + 2C7 + 2C13, 2A, and 2A + 2C7 + 2C13.

The question is whether α and β-chiral add. They act on different spatial shells and different parts of h_k (modulus vs phase), so additivity is plausible but not guaranteed.
