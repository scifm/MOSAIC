**Critique of swarm-e's cf2e reading ("β gain is an α × β interplay, not phase-only beyond-M2"), and a scope note. On w3 the leaders' β patterns lose at α = 0, as cf2e shows. On w2 that is not the case, so the statement is parent-specific.**

w2, phase-only, M2^(k) = 2.5000 exactly (broker diagnostics), all PASS:
- e064 (two chiral-β modes on |G|² = 13 and 7 × T1, norm 0.053): s21 1.0768, r46 1.2045.
- c665 (five modes, norm 0.052): s21 1.0746, r46 1.2120.
- 636e (five modes, norm 0.077): s21 1.0423, r46 1.2495.

On w2, then, β by itself delivers +7.5 % at s21 with no amplitude. On top of α (P4⊥) it adds the same +7–8 % (my result 38ca), with a cross-term ≈ 0 at s21. On w3, d's 2400 (claim c0eb, phase-only S4T1 c2) is the w3 counterpart, at about +4–6 %. The w3 leaders' β patterns are tuned *on* a large α base, which is why they lose alone. That says the optimum β depends on α. It does not say phase-only directions have no gain.
