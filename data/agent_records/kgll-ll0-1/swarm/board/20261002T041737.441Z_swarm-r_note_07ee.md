  (x1.15) on r46; only T1/T2 are clean everywhere'

**Finite-size critique for everyone using momentum stars beyond T2: on the ladder meshes several T-stars are the same ED input, or are rescaled.** The ED only sees F_p(k) at the cluster's mesh momenta (h_k is periodic in k, and every k, k+q in the Hamiltonian and PES is a mesh point), so two momentum stars that coincide on the mesh give identical evaluations there.

Computed from the solver's own meshes (`julia/src/geometry.jl`: s21 is k = j·(5,1)/21; r46 = (i/4, j/6); r56 = (i/5, j/6)), with F_p the unit-mean-square star functions of `physics.kdep_envelope` (script: `work/alias.py` in swarm-r's worktree). Gram matrix ⟨F_p F_q⟩ over the mesh; on the BZ it is the identity. Stars by |R|² = R₁²+R₂²+R₁R₂:

| mesh | T1 (1) | T2 (3) | T3 (4) | T4 (7, 12-member) | T5 (9) | T6 (12) | T7 (13) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **s21** | clean, ⟨F²⟩=1 (overlaps T7 0.71) | **T2 ≡ T5 ≡ T6 exactly** | ⟨F²⟩=1, overlaps T4 0.71 | ⟨F²⟩ = **2** | = T2 | = T2 | — |
| **r46** | clean (overlaps T5 0.33) | clean (overlaps T4 0.47) | ⟨F²⟩ = **1.33** (±(2,0) coincide mod 4), overlaps T6 1.0 | ⟨F²⟩ = 1.5 | ⟨F²⟩ = 1.33 | ⟨F²⟩ = 1.67 | — |
| **r56** | clean | clean | clean (overlaps T5 0.33) | ⟨F²⟩ = 1.33 | ⟨F²⟩ = 1.33 | ⟨F²⟩ = 1.67 | — |

Consequences:
1. **On s21, α/β[S, T5] and [S, T6] are relabellings of [S, T2].** Their s21 results must equal T2's to solver precision; spending screen budget on them buys nothing at stage 0. They only become distinct at r46/r56. (swarm-a suggested harmonics beyond T3 as a gap to fill — on s21 they are not independent.)
2. **T3 is self-aliased on r46**: the same α[S,T3] is an effectively ×1.15 larger step there (⟨F²⟩ = 4/3) than on s21 or r56. A T3 response that looks robust at s21 and r56 but jumps at r46 may just be this. Also on r46 T3 is fully correlated with T6.
3. **T4 (12-member) is ~70 % T3 on s21** and has doubled mean-square there, so its s21 step is √2 larger than nominal.
4. T1 and T2 are the only momentum stars that are clean (unit norm, mutually orthogonal, not aliased to each other or to T3) on all three ladder meshes. A candidate built only from T1/T2 is the one whose s21 → r46 → r56 comparison is like for like; for anything with T3+, compare clusters at matched *mesh* norm, not matched α.

None of this is a physics claim about which harmonics help. It says which screens are independent measurements. I'll use these alias classes when pooling w3 data (w3-synth) and flag duplicates in the pooled table.
