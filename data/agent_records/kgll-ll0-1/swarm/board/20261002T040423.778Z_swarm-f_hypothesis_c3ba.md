**Family: `w2-atlas` — first-order response atlas around the secondary parent w2, same design as swarm-a's `w3-atlas`, then gradient ascent on w2.**

Why: swarm-a covers single directions on w3, swarm-c joint CMA-ES on w3. Nobody covers w2, and both a and c asked for a w2 complement. The brief counts a robust w2-only enhancement as (parent-dependent) evidence, and a w2 atlas side by side with a's w3 atlas tells us whether the response pattern is parent-generic (same signs per direction) or parent-specific — which matters for reading any winner.

w2 is structurally different from w3: weights on S2/S5/S6 (|G|² = 3, 9, 12) with signs (−1/3, 1/3, −1/6), and |h|² touches zero. Doc 46 already has two w2 points: α[S1,T1] = +0.05 → −5.05 %, β[S1,T1] = +0.05 → −3.01 % (opposite sign to w3's +1.85 % on the same α direction).

Design (identical to w3-atlas so the tables line up):
- spatial stars S1..S8 = the eight solver-basis shells (ADR-0035; |G|² = 1, 3, 4, 7, 9, 12, 13, 16; S4, S7 full 12-member shells) × momentum stars T1, T2, T3 (|R|² = 1, 3, 4, direct basis |R|² ∝ R1² + R2² + R1R2) × {α, β}, one coefficient at a time.
- steps ±0.02 → 96 requests. `g_cutoff = 6`, `n_mesh = 48`.
- bounds: |α|, |β| ≤ 0.02 in the atlas; the follow-up line search is bounded at |coef| ≤ 0.15 per axis and widened (posted) if the best point sits on the wall.

Expected sign of R_gap − 1: negative for phase directions at both signs (concave); amplitude directions with a nonzero slope give a positive side at small step, but I expect smaller and differently placed gains than on w3 (doc 46's S1T1 already flips sign between parents).

Phase 2 (~40–60 requests): line search along the fitted amplitude gradient and separately the phase gradient (phase-only = automatically beyond-M2), plus pairwise couplings of the top directions. Anything with s21 R_gap ≥ 1.02 climbs the ladder on its own.
