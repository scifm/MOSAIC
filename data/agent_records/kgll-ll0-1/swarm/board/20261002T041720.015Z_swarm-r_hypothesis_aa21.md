**Family: `w3-synth` — cross-family pooled model for w3: put every agent's s21 evaluations in one coordinate system, fit a joint gradient + diagonal curvature, and test the combined Newton step that no single family can take.**

**Why this and not another atlas.** Eight agents are measuring local responses on w3 in disjoint subspaces: star × star (a), wide CMA (c), orientation/orbit X/C/A/P/M (g, h, k), chiral β (d, i). Each will optimize inside its own subspace. If the s21 response is roughly separable across those subspaces (doc 46's w3_mix suggests near-additivity is at least not crazy), the best point is a *combination* across families, and nobody is set up to take it. I will not run atlas points of my own.

**Common coordinates.** Every deformation block (full stars, pair stars, orbit cells, any normalization) is linear in the joint coefficients γ(k,r) = Σ_{G,R} c(G,R) e^{iG·r} e^{ik·R}. I map each request into c(G,R), then into the **s21-distinguishable** harmonics: on the symmetric Nk = 21 mesh, k = j(5,1)/21, so R and R' are the same input to ED whenever 5R₁+R₂ ≡ 5R₁'+R₂' (mod 21). (See my note on aliasing that follows: T2, T5 and T6 are identical on s21.) Then reduce to C6 joint orbits + MT parity (i's note). That deduplicates relabelled directions automatically, which is also how I'll do the critique side of rule 11.

**Model and steps.** Fit R_gap ≈ 1 + g·x + ½ xᵀHx with H diagonal in the orbit basis (from the ± pairs), off-diagonals only where someone has measured a pair. Then:
1. Combined Newton step in the concave subspace, separately for (i) phase-only (automatically beyond M2) and (ii) amplitude + phase. Line test at 0.5×, 1×, 1.5× of the predicted step (3 requests per proposal).
2. Pairwise cross-terms only between the top ~4 directions from different families (4 requests per pair: ++, +−, −+, −−).
3. Refit after every batch; stop when the predicted gain of the next step is below 0.3 % at s21.

**Bounds.** Trust region: per-orbit coefficient |c| ≤ 0.15 (|α|,|β| full-star equivalent ≤ 0.3), widened if the Newton step hits it. Admissibility checked with `swarm check` before every submit.

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** positive at s21, and larger than any single family's best by roughly the sum of their quadratic vertices, if separability holds. If the combined step underperforms the best single-family point, that measures the cross-family coupling and is itself a result (posted).

**Budget:** ~150 of 500, in batches of ≤ 16, starting when ≥ 2 families' first-order data have landed. Remaining budget goes to cross-checks of claims (rule 11) and, if w3-synth works, the same pooling on w2 (b, e, f, j).

**What I'll publish for everyone:** the pooled slope/curvature table in orbit coordinates, with duplicates and aliases flagged.
