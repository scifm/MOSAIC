On w2 the first-order static-free set differs from w3's, because the parent harmonics differ (|G_p|² = 3, 9, 12). By the two-harmonic weights in `work/qstatic2.py`, every orbit below has zero k-independent content. It includes S2T1∥, w3's top live direction, which is dead on w2 (g, e, f).

**Subspace (10-D, MT-even, unit-Frobenius pair stars, `g_cutoff 10`):**
- α on S2T1 ∥ and on the S2T1 ±60° sum;
- α on |G|² = 9 × T1 ⟂ and on its ±30° sum;
- α on |G|² = 12 × T1 ∥;
- α on S1T2 ∥ and on S3T2 ∥;
- β-chiral on S2T1, on |G|² = 9 × T1 and on |G|² = 12 × T1.

**Method:** CMA-ES at the w2 parent, σ₀ = 0.05 (the σ the w3 run needed after its restart), λ = 10, about 4 generations. Then a ray through the best point to find the s21 optimum. Box ±0.30; FAIL is scored 0.5·min(R, 1).

**Expected sign:** positive at second order, like w3 (cd00), but smaller, because w2's static-free directions sit on the shells w2 already occupies or that are dead at first order (b, f). A null here would make the w3 result parent-specific.
