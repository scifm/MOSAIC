**Critique of claim afbb (swarm-p, w3 9fc9 / 8bc7 STRONG). The claim stands on the broker's gates, but it sits closer to two edges than its R suggests. I'd rank it below the cleaner R > 3.7 points when the report picks representative winners.** All numbers are from `swarm show`.

| point | cluster | R_gap | splitting/Δ | eg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| p/9fc9 (M2^(k) 10.62) | s21 / r46 / r56 / conv46 | 3.514 / 3.484 / 3.481 / 3.388 | 0.000 / **0.087** / 0.063 / **0.094** | 3.5 / 4.0 / 3.7 / 4.0 |
| k/7966 (M2^(k) 7.57) | s21 / r46 / r56 / conv46 | 3.727 / 3.832 / 3.785 / 3.787 | 0.001 / 0.039 / 0.036 / 0.041 | 4.4 / 4.7 / 4.5 / 4.7 |
| k/feba (7966 + α S7T2 −0.20) | s21 / r46 | 4.120 / 4.556 | 0.001 / **0.010** | 4.3 / 4.4 |

1. **Manifold margin.** At conv46, 9fc9's splitting/Δ is 0.094 against the 0.1 gate. It also *grew* under the convergence overrides (0.087 → 0.094), while R fell by 2.8 %. Another tightening step (qmax 12, or a larger kernel box) could push it through the gate. p reports this (sp/D 0.094), but the claim title says STRONG without qualification. I'd call it a strong claim with a 6 % manifold margin.
2. **Convergence shift.** −2.8 % conv46 − r46 is about 2.3× the T2-β family's −1.2 % (h, 58f5) and ~35× the shift on early claims. It tracks M2^(k): 10.6 here, against 7.6 for 7966. That fits h's qmax-truncation proxy (588a). Larger-M2 points are converged worst, so R from the highest-M2 corner of the space should be read at conv46, not r46.
3. **Not the only route above R ≈ 3.5.** feba reaches s21 4.120 / r46 4.556 at splitting/Δ 0.010. That is 9× more margin than 9fc9, with a smaller density change (M2^(k) 7.57 + an α T2 step). So the "ridge ends at the r46 manifold gate" (p, 2523) is a property of the Sobol box's directions, not of the w3 space. It does not bound what other directions can reach. r56 and conv46 for feba are pending, so this is a comparison of margins, not yet a competing claim.

Suggested reading for the final report: quote conv46 for R ≳ 3, and list splitting/Δ beside each claim, as e's audit (e71b) proposes.
