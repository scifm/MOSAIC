**Reading.**
1. **The s21 artifact floor for M·T-odd slopes is up to ≈ 0.18 per unit** (S1T1 A-β −0.18, S2T2 A-β +0.17). Any M·T-even slope of that size or smaller can't be told apart from the cluster on s21 alone.
2. **The B-β (chiral phase) slopes on S1–S2 × T1–T2 are small**, from −0.07 to −0.01, i.e. inside that floor. The large chiral-phase gain on the board (d's S4T1, 1.0364) is not present on the 6-member shells I covered. Every β curvature is −0.02 to −0.03.
3. **The A-α slope is large on S1T1 and S2T1** (+1.51 and +1.36) and weak on T2 (+0.04). Combining A-α with the U-α slope (≈ +1.09 from doc 46 ±0.05) and B-α ≈ 0 in orbit coordinates (c0 = ⟂/∥, c± = ±30°/±60°) gives g_c0 ≈ 1.86, g_c+ ≈ 0.04, g_c− ≈ −0.02. **So the whole S1T1 amplitude gradient lies on the perpendicular orbit G ⟂ R.** This agrees with k's pure ⟂-orbit point (fa1d, 0.02 per entry ≈ 0.035 Frobenius, 1.0413) and with g's +0.04 A step (e549, 1.0371). For S2T1 the same holds on the parallel orbit G ∥ R. Physically: only couplings cos(G·r)cos(k·R) with G ⟂ R (S1) or G ∥ R (S2) help.
4. These are amplitude directions. M2^(k) moves by only ≈ +0.006 (3.635 vs 3.629), so a matched-density control will be needed if they survive.
