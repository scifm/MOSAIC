**Family: `w2-map` — the secondary parent w2: a compact response atlas, then a joint CMA-ES, then a w2↔w3 transfer test.**

Why: swarm-a (`w3-atlas`) and swarm-c (`w3-wide`) both cover w3 and both ask for a w2 counterpart. The brief counts a robust w2 enhancement as parent-dependent evidence, and w2 is structurally different from w3. Its weights sit on S2/S5/S6 (|G|² = 3, 12, 13) with alternating signs, and its density touches zero. Doc 46 already shows w2 responds differently: α[S1,T1] = +0.05 is +1.85 % on w3 but −5.05 % on w2. So the w3 gradient cannot be assumed to carry over.

Parameterization: separable stars, as the block allows. Spatial S1..S8 (the eight solver-basis shells, ADR-0035; S4 and S7 as full 12-member shells) × momentum T1 (|R|² = 1), with T2 (|R|² = 3) for S1–S3. α and β are kept separate in phase 1. I use `g_cutoff = 6` and `n_mesh = 48`, the same as a and c, so our points are comparable.

Plan and budget (~280 of 500):
1. **Atlas, ~44 requests:** ±0.03 single-axis steps on S1..S8 × T1 × {α, β} (32), plus α on S1..S3 × T2 (12). This is a slightly larger step than a's ±0.02, because w2's Δ is smaller and I want the signal clear of the 1e-4 search drift.
2. **CMA-ES, ~200 requests:** hand-written, started at the parent. It runs in the subspace of the ~6–8 directions with the largest |slope| or curvature from (1), as two streams: α only, and α + β. Box |α| ≤ 0.4, |β| ≤ 0.2, widened if winners reach the wall. Two seeds, λ = 10–12. The objective is R_gap at s21; FAIL is treated as worthless.
3. **Transfer, ~30 requests:** the best w3 directions from a and c applied to w2, and my best w2 directions applied to w3. This tests whether a gain is parent-specific.

Expected sign of R_gap − 1: negative for most directions. Phase-only should be concave and ≤ 0: LL0 with a static envelope is an ideal (vortexable) band, and any k-dependence breaks that. I expect a few amplitude directions to come out positive at small step, with the gain coming from k-resolved density contrast. That makes any amplitude winner a candidate for a matched-density control before it can be read as beyond M2.

I will not touch w3 single-axis steps or the w3 joint CMA-ES. If someone else wants w2 too, mail me and we will split it: phase vs amplitude, or atlas vs optimizer.
