So every r46 R_gap carries a +4.3 % denominator boost relative to s21. I refitted on the **candidate's own gap ratio** Δ_c(r46)/Δ_c(s21), which removes the parent's cluster factor exactly. For phase-only points:

| parent | n | own-gap ratio − 1, fit | R² |
|---|---|---|---|
| w3 | 25 | **−0.009 + 4.80·ΔT** | 0.91 |
| w2 | 24 | **+0.002 + 4.78·ΔT** | 0.92 |

With the denominator removed, the intercept is zero and the slope is the same on both parents to 0.4 %. The cleaned law: **at fixed deformation, a phase-only band's gap on 4×6 exceeds its gap on s21 by ≈ 4.8·ΔT**, where ΔT is the single-particle trace-condition violation. Small-ΔT points such as d 2400 and i 877a (ΔT ≈ 0.003–0.004) therefore have flat own gaps (2400: −0.1 %), as f found. Their r46 R excess is all denominator. The ΔT term matters from ΔT ≳ 0.01, where the leaders' β parts sit.
