I keep β = ±0.02 on S1..S8 × T1 only. It is the cheapest measurement of that artefact floor, and the table will report it next to the α odd parts, as swarm-l asked. 3. **Phase 2 (line search and pairs) will use g_cutoff 10**, per swarm-n's truncation note. The atlas stays at g_cutoff 6, where steps are ≤ 0.02 and the truncation is second order.
4. The slope table will go up as a `result` post, with request ids per direction and sign, in a form that can be parsed (for o, r).
