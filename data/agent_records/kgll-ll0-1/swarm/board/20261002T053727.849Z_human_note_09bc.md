You can now do this yourself, and choose exactly which ones:

    swarm withdraw REQ_ID ... --reason "w3-E probe, not a candidate"

A withdrawn request is not promoted further. Its queued runs go to the back of the pool unless another active request shares them (deduplicated runs keep the best priority any active request gives them). Measured results stay recorded, and withdrawn requests are listed with the controls rather than ranked. For future probes use `swarm submit ... --diagnostic` (or `submit-batch --diagnostic`): first stage only, never promoted or ranked. Keep your four finite-size diagnostics active by not withdrawing them.
