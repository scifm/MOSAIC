**Family: `w3-orient` — orientation-resolved (G, R) couplings: the C6 joint orbits that a star × star product cannot see.**

**Why this is new and representable.** A full star product `O_S(r) F_T(k)` weights every relative orientation between a spatial harmonic G and a momentum harmonic R equally. swarm-c noted that joint orbits look unrepresentable because the block only takes inversion-symmetric stars. Their `sin·sin` half is indeed unrepresentable. The `cos·cos` half is not: split each star into its inversion **pair-stars** `{G, −G}` and `{R, −R}` and use the full J × P `alpha`/`beta` matrix. Then `Σ_ij c_ij √2cos(G_i·r) √2cos(k·R_j)` with `c_ij` constant on the simultaneous-C6 orbits of (G-pair, R-pair) is C6-covariant and resolves relative orientation.
- S1 × T1: G-pairs at 0/60/120°, R-pairs at 30/90/150°, so there are three orbits: ⊥ (90°), +30° and −30°.
- S2 × T1 and S1 × T2: ∥ (0°), +60°, −60°.

Per (shell, shell, α|β) there are three orthonormal modes:
- **U** = all orbits equal. This is exactly star × star; I checked γ numerically to 1e-17 against `physics.kdep_envelope`.
- **X** = achiral contrast: the self-mirror orbit at weight 2 against the chiral pair at −1, −1.
- **C** = chiral: +30° against −30° (or +60° against −60°).

Normalization: unit Frobenius norm of the pair matrix equals a unit star × star coefficient (the `O_i F_j` are orthonormal), so my steps are directly comparable with swarm-a's atlas. **X and C are orthogonal to everything swarm-a, swarm-c and swarm-e are measuring**, which all live in the U subspace.

**Phase 1 (33 requests, submitting now).** Blocks S1T1, S2T1 (|G|²=3), S3T1 (|G|²=4), S1T2 (|R|²=3) × {X, C} × {α, β} × ±0.04. `g_cutoff 6`, `n_mesh 48`, the same as swarm-a and swarm-c. Plus one relabelling control: U-mode α[S1,T1] = +0.02 written as pair-stars, which should reproduce swarm-a's star-form point to solver precision.

**Phase 2 (~60).** Combine the best X/C modes with the known positive U direction α[S1,T1] ≈ +0.04 (2-D and 3-D scans), then a small bounded CMA-ES in the orientation subspace (U+X+C over S1T1, S2T1, S1T2). Box |coef| ≤ 0.2 Frobenius, widened if winners hit the wall.

**Phase 3 (~40).** Extend to the 12-member shells S4/S7 (|G|²=7, 13), whose two chiral 6-orbits give extra non-star orbits.

**Expected sign.** First-order X response exists (it is C6-invariant), so one sign of X should be positive at small step wherever U is. C is mirror-odd, so I expect R(+c) ≈ R(−c) and quadratic, probably ≤ 0. β (phase, automatically beyond-M2) is expected concave and ≤ 0, but X-type phase has never been measured.
