**Kernel-box truncation at `g_cutoff = 6` is not negligible for outer shells or large steps, and the conv46 rung cannot detect it. It runs with g_cutoff 6, the same box most of us screen with.**

Exponentiation creates harmonics beyond γ's own support. The parent density already reaches |m|,|n| = 4, and γ on S4 or S8 has |m| up to 4, so `ρ_* e^{γ*+γ'}` has weight well outside the 13×13 box. The solver drops it, so the evaluated kernel is no longer exactly F_G[h_k^* h_k'].

Below is the fraction of Σ_G|W_G(k,k')|² outside the box, the worst case over 25 (k, k') pairs (Γ, K, M, generic). It was computed with `physics.kdep_envelope.kernel_coefficients` on a 96 mesh; the script is `work/trunc_table.py` in the swarm-n worktree. One star at a time, T1:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| w3 | S1T1 α 0.6 / β 0.3 | 4.7e-4 / 3.6e-4 | 1.5e-6 / 7e-7 | ~1e-9 |
| w3 | S3T1 α 0.15 / 0.6 | 1.1e-4 / 1.1e-2 | 8.8e-7 / 9.8e-4 | 4e-9 / 5.7e-5 |
| w3 | S4T1 α 0.15 / 0.6 | 6.3e-3 / **1.3e-1** | 2.8e-4 / 3.7e-2 | 1.2e-5 / 8.6e-3 |

Reading:
- **Atlases at ±0.02–0.04 (a, b, e, f, g, h):** S1–S3 are clean. S4–S8 sit around 1e-5 to 1e-4 at cut 6 (quadratic in the step). That is probably harmless next to a % level response, but worth recording with the slopes.
- **Wide boxes (swarm-c: |α| ≤ 0.6, |β| ≤ 0.3 on S1–S4).** S4 at the wall loses ~13 % of the kernel weight at cut 6. A CMA run can then climb a truncation artifact. Suggestion: g_cutoff 10 for anything with |coef| ≳ 0.15 on S3 and above (`swarm check` accepts it), or at least re-run any winner at g_cutoff 10 as a control.
- **Ladder:** the conv46 rung overrides to `{n_mesh 64, g_cutoff 6}`. For requests screened at g_cutoff 6 it does not test the box at all. For requests screened at g_cutoff > 6 it *lowers* the box. I will ask the human whether conv should use max(request, 10).
