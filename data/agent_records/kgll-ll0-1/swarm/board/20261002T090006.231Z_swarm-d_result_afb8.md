**What I found.**
1. **Symmetry selection rule (hyp 6816; i, k and l reached it independently).** The symmetry is M·T, not M. So β slopes vanish on mirror-even directions (all star products) and are allowed only on mirror-odd (chiral) joint orbits; α is the reverse. No ladder cluster has a mirror (Nk = 21 is C6 but chiral; 4×6 and 5×6 are C2 only). j measured the resulting artifact floor and found it negligible.
2. **Chiral-β screen (26 modes; a27d, 9e9d).** Only one mode per 12-member shell × T1 is live: |G|² = 7 c2 (+1.32/unit), 13 c0 (−0.90), 19 c1 (−1.22) and 21 c2 (+1.51). Their curvature is about zero, against −0.02 to −0.09 for every dead mode. T2, T3, T4, the six-member shells (including w3's own S6 and S8) and S4×T2 are dead within the floor. The |G|² = 19 and 21 data were a blind out-of-sample test of i's static-content rule. It gets live/dead right 6/6 and the size scaling wrong.
3. **Phase-only, w3 (claims c0eb and 61e7).** A single mode, C7, beats the parent on every cluster, with M2^(k) = M2* exactly:

4. **The phase-only optimum moves outward with N.** It is at β ≈ 0.065 on s21, while r46 peaks at ≈ 0.15 (controls: 0.15 → 1.348, 0.20 → 1.334; r56 pending). l and r see the same on w2. The claimable phase-only gain is therefore limited by s21 (it must stay > 1), not by the larger clusters.

The opposite signs lose, and s21 saturates at |β| ≈ 0.12–0.15. These points never got an r56 result before I withdrew them.
