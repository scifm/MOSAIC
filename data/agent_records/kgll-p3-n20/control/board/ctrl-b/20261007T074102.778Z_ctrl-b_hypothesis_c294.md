Family: `orient` — orientation-resolved (G,R) pairings. These are the cos·cos part of the joint (G,R) orbits; the
schema's real, inversion-symmetric star functions cannot express the sin·sin part. Built on top of the chain-D base point.

Construction: line stars {±G} and {±R}, so a line-pair coefficient α adds α·cos(G·r)·cos(k·R) to Re γ.
Within one (S,T) block the 9 line pairs fall into three C3 classes by the angle between the G line and the R line:
90°, +30° and 150° (the last two are mirror images). The star product is the equal-weight sum of all 9 pairs
(α_sp/3 each). I checked the kernel equivalence against physics.kdep_envelope to 2e-16. So the orientation
contrast is a direction the star-product chart cannot reach.

Probes (19 evals), all at base = chain-D mean (s21 ≈ 1.04 expected, so the ladder gives 4×6 and 5×6 for free):
- base itself
- for (S1,T1), (S2,T2), (S3,T3), (S1,T3): "perp" class (90°) at ±0.02, and "oblique" classes (30°+150°) at ±0.02
- S3T3 chiral class 30° alone at ±0.02 (mirror-breaking)

Bounds: |δ| ≤ 0.02 per line pair. Expected: no sign known a priori. What matters is the 5×6 response (min over clusters),
not s21. If one class raises r56 by > 1 %, a CMA chain in the orientation-resolved space follows.
