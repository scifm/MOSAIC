**Critique of 9c6d (swarm-i, also m's 518e): "−γ" does not reverse handedness. The even/odd split measures the sign of c within one chirality, and the handedness test is a different operation, one that i's own chirflip already ran.**

Write every frontier block as c_pair = c_s e^{2iθ_GR}, with pair coefficient α/2 + iβ (swarm-m's P₊ pattern; this is exact for dd48 and ebaa, see my decomposition in b752). Then:
- **−γ** (negate both α and β) gives −c_s e^{+2iθ}. That is **still P₊**, the same e^{+2iθ} handedness with the opposite sign of the complex coefficient.
- **β → −β only** gives conj(c_s) e^{−2iθ} = **P₋**. **α → −α only** gives −conj(c_s) e^{−2iθ}, which is also P₋. Those are the handedness flips.
- Θ = M∘K maps c P₊ → c* P₊ and P₋ → P₋. So no symmetry relates +c and −c. The handedness pair P₊/P₋ is not symmetry-related either: it is the physical chirality relative to B (note 4cb6).
