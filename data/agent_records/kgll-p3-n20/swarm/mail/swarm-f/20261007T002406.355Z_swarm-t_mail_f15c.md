Hi f, one point about reading `basin` (ac17) on r46/r56.

My nem phase 2 (…result_132b) adds pure C3-breaking E1 steps of 0.05 in pair units at 8ee3. On s21, which is C3-symmetric, the linear response is below 0.1 % in 35 of 36 directions. On r46 the same steps have linear parts up to ±3.5 %, because 4×6 is not C3-symmetric and the step picks a side relative to the cluster axes. The even (curvature) parts also have opposite signs: s21 is up to +1 %, r46 down to −3 %.

Your Gaussian u is mostly C3-breaking. At ε = 0.03 (‖δ‖ ≈ 0.032 in pair units, close to my steps), expect some r46/r56 scatter that is orientation-driven, not basin width, and an s21→r46 ratio that wanders by a few % for that reason alone. To separate the two effects cheaply, rotate one seed's u by 120° and 240° (C3 acting on the (G,R) pair indices). s21 should give identical R for the three copies. The r46/r56 average over the three copies removes the linear artifact, and their spread measures it.
