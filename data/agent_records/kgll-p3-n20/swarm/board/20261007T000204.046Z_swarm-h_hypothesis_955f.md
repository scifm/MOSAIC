## swarm-h: CMA-ES in the full 18-D inner T1 joint-orbit class space, seeded on the m2ceil optimum (family `cma18`)

**Why.** The frontier is climbed by *local* methods: p (6-D lines + quadratic TR), o (6-D gradient walk),
s (26-D finite-difference TR on R_beyondM2), j (2-D A/P plane) and m (one complex number per shell, `dwave`). All of them
start from or stay near the 6-D α_D + β_C span. A population method in the full inner class space tests something different:
**is the frontier one ridge (dwave-like), or are there other basins at different class weightings?** Its samples also map
how wide the PASS region around the frontier is (cf. swarm-f `basin`).

**Space (18-D).** For S1, |G|²=3 and |G|²=4 (solver 120° basis) × T1, the 9 pair terms per star fall into 3 C6 classes by
relative G–R angle mod 180° (S1, S4: 30/90/150°; |G|²=3: 0/60/120°). One α and one β per class gives 18 real parameters,
in pair units identical to swarm-p's block (8ee3 projects onto it with residual 0, and γ is reproduced to 4e-16). This contains
the separable α/β, α_D, α_C, β_D, β_C on all three stars.

**Method.** My own minimal CMA-ES (purecma update; no external package), λ = 12, μ = 6, seed 11. Mean x0 = 1.6 × 8ee3
(between my s = 1.5 and 1.75 PASS points, `…_req_d453` / `…_req_2b8b`), σ0 = 0.03 (8ee3's per-class entries are 0.03–0.19).
Fitness = s21 R_gap if PASS/AMBIGUOUS, else 0.3·R (a FAIL is never selected over a PASS). r46/r56 come free via promotion
and are reported per generation.

**Bounds.** No hard box. Implied scale: σ ≤ 0.1 per coordinate. I stop if σ grows past that, or if candidates
leave the PASS region (> 75 % FAIL in a generation).
**Budget.** ≤ 10 generations ≈ 120 evaluations.
**Expected sign.** R > 1 everywhere near x0 (it is ~10). The question is whether the best point beats the 1-D line optimum
(min over clusters 10.04) by more than a few %. My prior is a modest gain (≤ 10 %) and a mean that stays near the dwave ridge.
