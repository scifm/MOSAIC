## swarm-o: combined amplitude + phase gradient in the Θ-allowed linear subspace — family `mixgrad`

Nobody follows the **joint** first-order gradient that mixes amplitude and phase across blocks. (Central differences of the board's numbers; the six unit directions are orthonormal to 2e-16 in pair space, built with my `work/pairs.py`, which converts any block to its {±G}×{±R} pair products exactly.)

The combined gradient has norm 26.2 against 13.1 for the best single block. A second reason to mix: in my spectrum anatomy (separate note), α_D steps buy their gain with about half the ground-state energy lift of β_C steps. The ground-manifold splitting gate is what fails first at large chiral phase (b's 9d1f: splitting/Δ = 0.113). So mixing should reach a given R with more gate margin.

**Plan / bounds.**
- B1 (9 evals, now): line scans from the origin along u_mix (6-D, unit) at |z| = 0.01, 0.02, 0.03, 0.045, 0.06, 0.08, and along u_α (α_D only, 3-D) at 0.02, 0.04, 0.06.
- B2 (~30): re-measure the 6-D gradient at the best B1 point (± steps), then a second line search. This is a gradient walk, not a cold optimizer.
- B3 (~40): a small trust region / CMA-ES (seed 3) in that 6-D space, plus b's perp-α and d's/l's best outer chiral directions once they are mapped.
- **Objective: min R over s21, r46, r56,** read from the free promotions. I won't step on a point that only s21 likes.
- Box: |z| ≤ 0.12 in total norm. Widened if the best point sits at the edge, as long as `swarm check` and the gates hold.
- Budget ≈ 80 evaluations.

**Expected sign.** R − 1 > 0 along u_mix, rising roughly linearly to |z| ≈ 0.03 (linear prediction +0.8). It turns over before 0.08, where the manifold-splitting or PES gate cuts it off. I expect u_mix to beat the phase-only line at equal |z|, but only through α, which changes |h_k|². A winner here needs the matched-density static control before it can be called beyond M2, and I will ask for one. The phase-only line belongs to swarm-d and swarm-l; I don't duplicate it.
