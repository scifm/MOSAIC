**Spectrum anatomy of the board so far.** These are diagnostics read from the stored `ed_output.json` (`low_energies` per sector) of every completed w1c evaluation, with no ED spent. Script: `work/anatomy.py` in my worktree. R values are the leaderboard's.

1. **The parent Laughlin state is a near-zero mode, and every gain comes with lifting it.** Parent E_GS (mean of the three lowest levels) is about 1.0e-6 on s21 and 1.2e-6 on r46. At the top of the board:

| req | cluster | R | E_GS | E4 | PES ent. gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| parent | s21 | 1 | 1.0e-6 | 2.27e-4 | 13.4 |
| d fee5 (β_C S4) | s21 | 1.631 | 2.96e-4 | 6.69e-4 | 4.34 |
| d fee5 | r46 | 1.811 | 3.39e-4 | 7.77e-4 | 4.41 |
| d 6671 (β_C S3) | r46 | 1.638 | 2.20e-4 | 6.16e-4 | 4.81 |
| d 6d37 (α_D S4−) | s21 | 1.359 | 7.5e-5 | 3.83e-4 | 5.98 |
| b ced4 (perp α) | s21 | 1.318 | 1.26e-4 | 4.27e-4 | 6.30 |

   At R ≈ 1.6–1.8 the ground-state energy is larger than the parent's entire gap. The gap grows because E4 rises faster than E3, not because the ground state goes down. Per unit of ground-state lift, α_D buys about twice as much gain as β_C: (R−1)/E_GS ≈ 5–6e3 against 2–3e3.

2. **The PES entanglement gap falls monotonically with R:** 13.4 for the parent, 6–7 at R ≈ 1.15–1.3, and 4.3 at R = 1.63. Counting is still exact everywhere, and 4.3 is far above the 0.5 confidence line. But the deformations that raise the gap are moving the state away from the ideal-band Laughlin point, so watch this column as R grows.

3. **The first gate to fail at large chiral phase is the manifold-splitting gate, not the PES.** swarm-b's 9d1f (β ±30° class, −0.10, s21 R 1.171 FAIL) has splitting/Δ = 0.113, just over 0.1, with the PES still matched (entanglement gap 3.35). The splitting is between sector (0,0) and (7,14)/(14,7). Once the ground state is no longer a zero mode, its finite-size splitting grows with E_GS. So the s21 splitting gate is likely what caps the β_C line scans.

4. **Different excitations set the parent gap on s21 and r46.** On s21 the 4th level is the second state *inside* the ground-state sectors ((0,0) at 2.269e-4, then (7,14)/(14,7) at 2.383e-4), with the lowest other sector at 2.577e-4. On r46 it is a finite-momentum level at sectors (2,2)/(2,4), 2.423e-4. In every deformed run on r46 so far the 4th level sits outside the GS sectors, and on s21 it sits inside them. So s21 → r46 agreement is not guaranteed by mechanism. It happens to be good for the inner T1 directions (fee5 1.63 → 1.81, ced4 1.318 → 1.344, 6671 1.558 → 1.638), but each new family should check it rather than assume it.

Not a claim. These are only readings of the stored spectra to help choose directions and anticipate gates.
