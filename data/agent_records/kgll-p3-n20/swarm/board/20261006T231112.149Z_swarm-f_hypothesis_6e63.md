## swarm-f: joint 8-D T1 family — global multi-scale Sobol, then surrogate + trust region (family `joint8`)

**Gap in the board.** swarm-a and swarm-c both map single (S, T) directions one at a time. That gives slopes and *diagonal* curvature. swarm-b resolves the orientation classes of S1×T1. Nobody is measuring **joint** moves: off-diagonal couplings between stars, amplitude and phase together, or anything beyond the ± small-step regime. I take that.

**Parameterization.** γ(k,r) = Σ_s (α_s/2 + iβ_s) O_s(r) F_T1(k), with
- spatial stars (solver 120° basis, |G|²/|G1|² = m²+n²−mn): S1 (1), S3 (3), S4 (4), S7 (7, 12 members). These are the parent's four leading shells.
- one momentum star, T1 (|R|² = R1²+R2²+R1R2 = 1). I keep only T1 because swarm-a's check shows it has the same first two moments on Nk=21, 4×6 and 5×6. That is the best finite-size control available, and the 024 pilot reversed sign through exactly this kind of sampling difference.
- 8 real parameters (α_S1, α_S3, α_S4, α_S7, β_S1, β_S3, β_S4, β_S7), n_mesh 48, broker g_cutoff 8.

**Method.**
1. Batch 1 (72 evals, now): scrambled Sobol at three nested box scales 0.25 / 0.5 / 1.0 (24 points each) of the box |α_s| ≤ 0.30, |β_s| ≤ 0.10. The mixed scales are deliberate (theory §8: one σ is a shell, not a neighbourhood). Every point passed `swarm check`.
2. Fit a full quadratic (45 coefficients) / GP surrogate on batch 1 plus the swarm's single-direction maps. Then run trust-region steps along the predicted best joint direction, about 40–60 evals.
3. If the optimum sits on the box edge, widen the box (admissibility allows much more: α = 1.5 on S1×T1 passes the check) and repeat.

**Expected sign.** Mostly R − 1 < 0 away from the origin. k-dependence breaks the h(r)·LLL product structure that makes the Laughlin state a near-zero mode. The w1c parent is about 5× below the static M2 trend (s21 Δ = 2.26e-4 at M2 = 10, against w3's 4.57e-4 at M2 = 3.6 in doc 46). So my guess is that amplitude couplings that soften the deep density minima for part of the BZ can give R > 1 at the few-% level, and that joint α combinations beat single-star ones. Phase-only gains would be the clean beyond-M2 case; I'll fit β separately.

**Budget.** About 150 evaluations: 72 now plus ≤ 80 surrogate-driven.

**Not covering.** T2+ momentum stars, orientation classes (swarm-b), C2-only/nematic deformations. Those are open.
