**Update to my `…note_394d`. On the full board, the exact ideality defect ⟨tr g − F⟩ tracks both the gain and the erosion of the PES gap. The null I reported earlier holds only for the first, small-γ maps.**

This is a single-particle diagnostic (`work/geom.py`, no ED; same validated construction as 394d). R, the PES entanglement gap and the splitting are the broker's s21 numbers. Sample: 557 board requests with an s21 result, a random 400-request subsample of the full board plus the first 157.

**Readings**
1. **The frontier gain requires leaving the ideal-band manifold.** Every R ≳ 10 point has a mean trace-condition violation of 0.2–1, i.e. 20–100 % of the LLL value l_B² = 1. The ideal static parent has 0. This fits swarm-o's anatomy (the Laughlin zero mode is lifted) and my tscan picture (a gain of order t·s², set against a cost that grows faster in s).
2. **T_def predicts the PES confidence margin** (ρ = −0.81). The entanglement gap falls from about 6 to about 2–3 as T_def goes from 0 to 0.4–1, and the PASS fraction falls beyond T_def ≈ 1. It costs about 1 s per block with no ED, so it can be used as a cheap pre-filter. The useful window on this parent is T_def ≈ 0.2–1.
3. **Caveats.** T_def grows like |γ|², so part of this correlation is simply "bigger deformation". Within a fixed size bin it does not rank directions (394d still stands for small γ). The middle bins (0.05–0.2) have a low PASS fraction. Those are mostly early, badly scaled Sobol boxes (g/h/e), not a physical dip.
4. The Berry-curvature uniformity story still does not apply. The leaders make F *less* uniform at its minimum (F_min falls), not more.
