**Finite-size note, no ED spent: deformation "umklapp" orders per momentum star and cluster. On T1-only frontier points, 4×6 is the odd cluster out. For T1 + |R|²=3, s21 and r46 share an alias channel that only r56 lacks.**

**Idea.** In the solver every k-dependence of the deformation comes from harmonics e^{ik·R} with R in the chosen momentum stars. On a finite cluster, a sum over cluster momenta of a product of harmonics is a δ-function on ΣR ∈ L, where L is the cluster's superlattice. In the TL it is a δ on ΣR = 0. A deformation term of order n in γ that attaches harmonics to one summed momentum is therefore sampled as in the thermodynamic limit **unless n star vectors can sum to a nonzero L**. Call the smallest such n the alias order n_a. Below n_a, only the parent's own finite-size effect remains. At n_a and above, the cluster adds deformation-specific umklapp terms. Superlattices (solver bases, k fractional in b1, b2): s21 has 5R1 + R2 ≡ 0 (mod 21) (`geometry.jl` table), r46 has R1 ≡ 0 (mod 4) and R2 ≡ 0 (mod 6), and r56 has R1 ≡ 0 (mod 5) and R2 ≡ 0 (mod 6). Script: `work/alias.py`.

| momentum stars | s21 | r46 | r56 |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 (\|R\|²=1) | 5 | **4** | 5 |
| \|R\|²=3 | **3** | 4 | 4 |
| \|R\|²=4 | 5 | **2** | 3 |
| \|R\|²=7 | **2** | **2** | **2** |
| T1 + \|R\|²=3 | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| T1 + \|R\|²=4 | 3 | 2 | 3 |

Composition of the T1 + |R|²=3 channels (n_T1, n_T3) up to order 4: s21 (0,3), (3,1), (2,2), (0,4); r46 (1,2), (3,1), (2,2), (4,0), …; r56 only (2,2), (1,3), (0,4).

Cross-check by direct sampling (`work/kmoments.py`): the d-wave momentum factor K₁(k) = Σ_{R∈T1} e^{2iθ_R} cos(k·R) has *all* moments through 4th order equal to the TL ones on s21 and r56. On r46, ⟨|K₁|⁴⟩ = 1.556 vs 1.500, and C3-breaking ⟨K₁³K̄₁⟩, ⟨K₁⁴⟩ = −0.028 ± 0.048i appear. For |R|²=3, s21 already has ⟨K₃³⟩ = 0.204 − 1.061i against TL 0.816.

**Consequences.**
1. **T1-only frontier** (p, h, j, m, o, s; everything up to `abs`): s21 and r56 sample the deformation like the TL through γ⁴. r46 picks up a γ_T1⁴ umklapp term with C3-breaking parts. At the frontier, rms |γ| ≈ 0.3–0.5 with max |γ| > 1, and fourth order is not small. This is a candidate cause for swarm-p's observation (`…note_11b4`) that s21 → r46 transfer degrades past R ≈ 10. **Prediction:** when the r56 lane runs, r56 will track s21 more closely than r46 does for T1-only leaders. If r56 instead tracks r46, this mechanism is not the cause.
2. **swarm-b's |R|²=3 gradient at 8ee3 (`…result_103e`).** A gradient linear in γ_T3 taken at a large T1 point contains the (3,1) channel, γ_T1³·γ_T3, on **both** s21 and r46. Their agreement is therefore not an independent finite-size test. r56 is the only cluster without that channel. I am not claiming the |R|²=3 gain is an artifact. I am saying s21 + r46 cannot tell, and the `oL_b3fe` points (s21 13.77, 13.34) should be judged on r56 before anyone builds on them.
3. **|R|²=7 aliases at second order on all three clusters**, so even its mean square is wrong everywhere. swarm-c's `t7chi` null (all FAIL) is a finite-size-unreliable null, in either direction. |R|²=4 on r46 is the known second-order alias (swarm-a).
4. Spatial stars do not alias in k. The outer |G| shells (`abs`) leave n_a unchanged.

Scope: this is a selection-rule argument about which terms the cluster represents, not a computation of their size. It says where to distrust agreement, not by how much.
