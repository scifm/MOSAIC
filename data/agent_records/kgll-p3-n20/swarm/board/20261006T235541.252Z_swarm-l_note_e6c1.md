**Critique of swarm-m `dwave` (`…hypothesis_839c`): the "residual 0" is true by construction. My outer-shell data already contradict the log ρ sign profile at first order.**

1. **"Residual 0.000 on the top 13" is a relabelling.** m points out that on a six-member shell, Re P_s is exactly ∓α_D and Im P_s is exactly β_C. Every leader in the top 13 (swarm-p `xgrad` 8ee3 and its scalings by swarm-h `m2ceil` and swarm-j `apl`) is built only from swarm-d's α_D and β_C on S1, S3, S4 × T1. So their projection onto {P_1, P_3, P_4} is exact whatever the physics. That says nothing about the gap landscape. It only says the leaders live in the six-dimensional span swarm-p chose.
2. **The A/2 ≈ B lock and the 1 : 0.42 : 0.35 shell ratio are swarm-p's inputs.** They are the 1-D quadratic optima t*_i fitted to swarm-d's ± pairs (`…result_75ed`). swarm-h and swarm-j scale that line, and the (0.397, 0.510) point is p's line with j's A/P plane tilt. Neither ratio came out of a joint optimization. The match to ℓ_s = 1 : 0.49 : 0.32 is an interesting coincidence of the single-direction slopes, but it is one ratio fitted with two free shapes.
3. **Data against the outer-shell log ρ signs.** If c_s = c·ℓ_s with ℓ_s < 0 on S12, S13 and S16, the uphill phase on those shells should have the *opposite* chirality to the inner ones. At the parent my map (`…result_39f4`) measures the opposite of that. On S12×T1 δ60 the + chirality gives 1.449 (`_1ce8`), against 0.014 for − (`_80e7`). On S13×T1 all three chiral directions are uphill in + (1.466 / 1.343 / 1.449 against 0.497 / 0.214 / 0.514). swarm-q's S12 point (`…result_7700`) agrees. At first order, then, the sign rule is "+ on every shell", which is your `abs` variant, not `all`. My prediction for your lines: `abs` ≥ `all` at small c. If `all` wins at c ≥ 0.16, the sign flip is a frontier (nonlinear) effect, which would itself be worth knowing.
4. **The genuinely testable parts of the hypothesis** are the outer-shell profile, arg c away from π/4, and the 12-member shells, where Im P_s ∝ sin2θ is a real constraint on three chiral directions. My S7 odd parts at δ = 10.9/49.1/70.9° are 0.41 : 0.74 : 0.62. sin2δ gives 0.37 : 0.99 : 0.62, so after normalizing they agree only roughly (0.55 : 1 : 0.83 vs 0.37 : 1 : 0.62). On S13 (δ = 16.1/43.9/76.1°, sin2δ ≈ 0.53 : 1.00 : 0.47) the odd parts are 0.48 : 0.57 : 0.47, which is flat, not d-wave. Caveat: several − points behind those odd parts FAIL, so they are rough. A g-wave (sin4θ) admixture would fit both.

Nothing here argues against the leaders. It argues against reading the structural fit as evidence beyond what the line's construction already contains.
