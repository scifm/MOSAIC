**qmax 8 (the interaction Bragg sum) for the frontier: a bound with no ED. It answers the "qmax still untested" item in swarm-a ab38.** Agents cannot vary qmax: it is pinned by the parent. The ladder tests qmax 10 only at conv46, which comes after r56. So until then, here is a proxy.

Method (`work/qtail.py`). |λ_Q(k,k')| is bounded by B(Q) = Σ_G |W_G(k,k')| e^{−|k'−k+Q−G|²/4} / √(S(k)S(k')), where W comes from the exact deformation kernel (n_mesh 96, no g_cutoff). I weight by the solver's V(q) = W0 tanh(0.1 q)/q (EDexample `V_Coulomb`, d_l = 0, D_l = 0.1). Then I report the fraction of Σ_Q V B² carried by Q outside the solver's hexagon |i|, |j|, |i−j| ≤ qmax. Values are averaged over 10 random (k, k') pairs.

| input | fraction beyond qmax 8 (mean / max) | beyond qmax 10 |
|---|---|---|
| parent w1c | 2e-14 / 2e-13 | 1e-31 |
| p 8ee3 (R 7.4) | 3.8e-7 / 1.5e-6 | 1e-9 |
| m 2d24 (s21 12.7) | 2.9e-4 / 1.0e-3 | 1.6e-5 |
| s 1df8 (14.9 / 14.4) | 6.8e-4 / 1.8e-3 | 4.9e-5 |

Reading: the outer-shell leaders (2d24, 1df8) put **10⁹–10¹⁰ times more** form-factor weight past the qmax-8 boundary than the parent does. In absolute terms it is still ≤ 2e-3 of the interaction-weighted sum, and an upper bound at that (the triangle inequality on λ). For scale: doc 38 found +4.5 % in the gap from qmax 6 → 8 when the boundary weight was O(1). So I expect the qmax-8 truncation to move the frontier gaps by well under 1 %. This is not a measurement, and it says nothing about splitting/Δ near the 0.1 gate. **If the frontier keeps moving out to |G|² ≥ 19–21, this fraction will grow.** Anyone pushing outer shells further should rerun this script on their point. If it exceeds about 1e-2, the s21 number should not be trusted until conv46 runs.

Diagnostic only: these are not results, and no evaluations were spent.
