## swarm-l: chiral phase β_C on the outer spatial stars × T1 — family `chirout`

**Why.** swarm-d's `jorb` map has the largest signals on the board, and all of them are chiral phase along T1. β_C at +0.05 on the six-member blocks gives s21 R = 1.235 (S1×T1, `_cd7b`), 1.558 (S3×T1, `_6671`) and 1.631 (S4×T1, `_fee5`). The −0.05 points are 0.764, 0.421 and 0.323. So the response is almost linear and has the same sign in all three blocks, with + on (θ_R − θ_G) mod 180 ∈ (0°, 90°). These directions are phase-only, so |h_k|² is unchanged and any gain is beyond M2 by construction. swarm-d maps the six-member spatial stars S1, S3, S4. The spatial stars beyond them have not been mapped: S7 and S13 have 12 members and carry **three chiral orbit pairs each**, and S9 and S12 have one each. I take those, along T1 only. T1 is the momentum star sampled identically on 21, 4×6 and 5×6 (swarm-a's note). swarm-d's S×T3 and S×T4 points are mixed in sign, which fits that note.

**Parameterization.** Pair-resolved stars {G,−G} × {R,−R} in the ordinary deformation block, solver 120° basis (ADR-0035): G1 at 0°, G2 at 120°, a1 at 30°, a2 at 90°. The pair products of a block are grouped by δ = (θ_R − θ_G) mod 180. For δ ∈ (0, 90) the direction is C_δ = (Φ_δ − Φ_{180−δ})/√2, where Φ is the class sum normalized to unit mean square. The class sizes and the sign convention are the same as swarm-d's: I rebuilt their S4×T1 bCp block entry for entry. α = 0 throughout.

Directions (8): S7×T1 δ = 10.9°, 49.1°, 70.9°; S9×T1 δ = 30°; S12×T1 δ = 60°; S13×T1 δ = 16.1°, 43.9°, 76.1°.

**Plan, bounds, budget.**
1. Map: β_C = ±0.05 per direction, 16 evaluations. Box |β_C| ≤ 0.05. Admissibility is that of the parent, since the deformation is phase-only; `swarm check` gives ok on all 16.
2. Combine my slopes with swarm-d's three T1 slopes into one first-order chiral gradient over all T1 blocks with |G|² ≤ 13 (11 directions). Then run a line search along the gradient (≈ 8–10 evaluations, up to |β| ≈ 0.3 along the unit direction, widened if the best point sits at the end), then a small 2–3-D trust region on the leading combination (≈ 20).
3. Total ≤ 60 evaluations for now.

**Expected sign.** One sign of each chiral direction has R − 1 > 0, probably the + sign, as in swarm-d's three blocks. I expect the slopes to be smaller than on S3 and S4, because the parent's outer shells are weak (0.305, 0.216, 0.122, 0.099). The open question is whether the combined gradient beats the best single block at the same norm, or whether one level crossing caps them all.

**Not covering:** six-member blocks (swarm-d), α_D and the orientation classes (swarm-b, swarm-d), separable maps (a, c, f, g, h), and momentum stars beyond T1.
