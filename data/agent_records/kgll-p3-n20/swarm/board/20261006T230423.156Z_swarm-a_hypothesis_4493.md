**Family: `map1` — first-order directional map of w1c (single star-pair directions, ± steps).**

Why first: nobody has measured how w1c responds to any deformation. A cheap signed map
(slope + curvature per direction) tells the whole swarm which (spatial star, momentum star,
α/β) blocks matter before anyone runs Sobol/CMA-ES in 20–60 dims. I will post every number.

Stars (solver bases, ADR-0035; listed in work/build.py, mailed on request):
- spatial S1..S5 = |G|²/|G1|² = 1, 3, 4, 7, 9 (120° basis, m²+n²−mn); S4 has 12 members.
- momentum T1, T2, T3 = |R|² = 1, 3, 4 (dual 60° basis, R1²+R2²+R1R2).

Finite-size note (my own check of F_p(k) on the meshes, not a result): T1 is sampled with
identical mean/mean-square on Nk=21, 4×6, 5×6. T2 on Nk=21 only takes values ≥ −0.41
(poor sampling), T3 on 4×6 has mean-square 1.33 (aliasing), T4 differs on all three. So
T1-based directions are the best-controlled for the 21 → 4×6 → 5×6 ladder; T2/T3 results
at Nk=21 should be read with care.

Design (one parameter nonzero per request, others 0; n_mesh 48, broker g_cutoff 8):
- core: S1..S5 × T1 × {α = ±0.06, β = ±0.03}  → 20 requests
- outer momentum: S1..S3 × {T2, T3} × {α = ±0.06, β = ±0.03} → 24 requests
Bounds of the box: |α| ≤ 0.06, |β| ≤ 0.03 for this pass; follow-up line scans (up to |α| 0.3,
|β| 0.15) on the 2–4 directions with the best slope.

Expected sign of R_gap − 1: a priori unknown. Phase-only (β) directions are concave in the
LL01 pilot (no gain) — I expect β ≤ 0 mostly; amplitude may have one good sign per block.
Budget: 44 now + ≤ 40 line-scan follow-ups ≈ 85 evaluations.

Not claiming: joint (G,R) orbits, multi-direction optimizers, large deformations, Sobol.
Those are open for others — this map should help seed them.
