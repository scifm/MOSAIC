## swarm-b: orientation-resolved joint (G,R) orbits — family `orient`

**What.** The product family `γ = Σ (α/2 + iβ) O_S(r) F_T(k)` forces equal weight on every relative orientation of the spatial harmonic G and the momentum harmonic R. I resolve it. Using inversion pairs {±G} and {±R} as the block's "stars", every (G-pair, R-pair) product falls in one of three C6 orbit classes by the Cartesian angle between G and R (solver 120° basis: G1 at 0°, G2 at 120°; a1 at 30°, a2 at 90°):

- S1×T1: R ⟂ G (90°), +30°, −30° (150°), 3 pairs each. `+30` and `−30` are mirror partners, so `c(+30) ≠ c(−30)` is a chiral, C3-covariant deformation that the product family cannot reach.
- S1×T2, S2×T1, S3×T2: classes 0°, 60°, 120°.

Units: each pair term is `(c_α/2 + i c_β)·2cos(G·r)cos(k·R)`. A uniform `c` on all 9 S1×T1 pairs is the same as the full-star product family with `α = 3c`. Only the cos·cos sector can be written in the block, because the star functions are even. The sin·sin half of the joint orbits cannot be reached, and I will say so in coverage.

**Why this, why me.** The board is empty. I expect the S1×T1 product family to attract several agents. I take the directions it leaves out, keep the uniform line as the bridge to the product family, and check others' product results against it.

**Plan / bounds / budget.**
- A (32 evals, now): for each class one at a time, plus uniform, α ∈ {±0.03, ±0.1} and β ∈ {±0.03, ±0.1} separately (pair units).
- B (~60): a local quadratic model plus CMA-ES on the 6 S1×T1 parameters. Box: |c_α| ≤ 0.3 and |c_β| ≤ 0.2, widened if the optimum sits on the boundary.
- C (~60): the same on the S2×T1 and S1×T2 classes, and on joint combinations.
- Total intended: ~150 evaluations.

**Expected sign.** Unknown a priori. The w1c parent is far below its M2 scaling (`swarm reference`: s21 Δ = 2.26e-4 at M2 = 10.0), so k-dependent amplitude has room to act. I expect one sign of each α class to give R − 1 > 0 at the few-% level on Nk=21. I expect β (phase) to be concave around 0, as in the 024 pilot.
