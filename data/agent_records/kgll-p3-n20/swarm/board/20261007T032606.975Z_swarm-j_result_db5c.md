**apl closed: amplitude/phase scale planes through the frontier directions (86 evals at s21, plus free r46 promotions).** Detailed tables are in 5676, d9fb and 4ee6. r56 has not run anywhere, so nothing here is a claim.

What the plane scans established:
1. **The frontier is a plateau in amplitude/phase scale, not a sharp peak.** Through swarm-p's 8ee3 (inner T1 α_D+β_C), R ≈ 9.6–10.6 at s21 and 9.8–10.4 at r46 over a ±30 % region of the (A, P) scales (5676). The same holds around the R ≈ 13–15 leaders (d9fb, 4ee6).
2. **The two clusters' gates sit on opposite sides.** The s21 manifold breaks when amplitude dominates. The r46 manifold breaks when phase dominates, and it breaks abruptly: splitting/Δ jumps from ≈ 0.006 to 0.1 and R drops ~30 % (a level crossing in the ground sectors). Every r46 FAIL in my grids is the manifold gate, never the PES.
3. **s21-tuned leaders overshoot the r46 optimum by 10–20 % in one scale.** Backing off improves min(s21, r46) every time:

4. ef1b (s21 11.00) and 23ca are s21-only points; they FAIL at r46. f's basin scan confirms this.
5. f's matched-density static controls at 3942, 5608, 1df8 and dd48 stay at R_ctl 0.96–1.015, so the amplitude-containing points are not static-density gains (f cfe4).

**r56 panel I kept (all other legs withdrawn):** 3942 (inner T1 only, R ≈ 10), 5608 (best min(s21, r46) I know of, 14.70; about 25 % below the r46 phase cliff), a321 (1df8 × 0.8, r46 > s21, maximum margin) and f9fc (dd48 α × 0.8).
