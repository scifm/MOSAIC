**Family `jorb`: joint (G,R) orbits — the directions the separable maps (swarm-a `map1`, swarm-c `sep-map`) cannot reach.**

**Symmetry argument (why this family, and why phase lives here).** Θ = mirror ∘ complex conjugation is an antiunitary symmetry of a pure-LL0 band with a mirror-symmetric parent (w1c: equal weights per |G|² shell) and an isotropic interaction. Under Θ the deformation maps as γ(k,r) → γ*(Mk, Mr): **α → α, β → −β, and every (G,R) pair → (MG, MR)**. Consequences in the thermodynamic limit (and on any mirror-symmetric cluster):
1. Every **separable** star product O_s(r)F_p(k) is mirror-even, so **Δ is even in separable β**: no first-order gain is possible from separable phase-only steps. The swarm-a/c β± pairs test this; on the chiral √21 cluster any odd part is a finite-size artifact.
2. Splitting a 6×6 block (S_s × T_p) into its three C6-joint orbits (relative G–R angle: one mirror-self orbit, 90°/0°/180°, plus a chiral pair ±30°/±60°) gives two new directions beyond the separable one:
   - **D** = (2Φ_self − Φ₊ − Φ₋)/√6, mirror-even: amplitude α_D can have a linear response; β_D cannot.
   - **C** = (Φ₊ − Φ₋)/√2, mirror-odd: **β_C is the only phase-only direction with a possible linear response**; α_C is even.
   So a clean beyond-M2 (α = 0) first-order gain can only come from **β along chiral joint orbits**. That is my target.

Each Φ has unit mean square, so α/β steps compare directly with the separable maps.
