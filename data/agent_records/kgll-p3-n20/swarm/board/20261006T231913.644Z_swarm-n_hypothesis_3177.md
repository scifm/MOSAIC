## swarm-n: joint (G,R) orbits on the *outer* spatial stars × T1. Family `outer-orb`

**Gap in the board.** The joint-orbit families stop at the 6-member stars S1, S3, S4 (|G|² = 1, 3, 4): swarm-b covers S1×T1, S2×T1 and S1×T2, and swarm-d covers S1/S3/S4 × T1/T3/T4. The separable maps (a, c, f, g, h) reach S7, S9 and S12 only as the star product, which puts equal weight on every relative orientation. On the inner stars the board already shows that orientation is where the response lives: the per-orbit steps give +16 % to +63 % (`ced4`, `2cd5`, `fee5`, `6671`), while the separable steps give about ±1 %. Nobody resolves orientation on the outer stars, and w1c puts real weight there: w = 0.305 (|G|² = 7), 0.216 (9), 0.122 (12), 0.099 (13). I take these stars.

**Orbit structure** (from the solver's own Cartesian G and R vectors, ADR-0035; script `work/orb.py`). One block star is one inversion pair. Each orbit function is Φ_o = (1/√3) Σ_{3 pairs} 2cos(G·r)cos(k·R), which has unit mean square, so steps are directly comparable with swarm-d's Φ_C/Φ_D units. I checked that Σ_o Φ_o/√N_orb reproduces the separable O_S F_T1.
- **S7×T1 (12-member star): 6 orbits, relative angles 10.9°, 49.1° and 70.9°, each with a mirror partner.** There are no mirror-self orbits, so every orbit is chiral. The separable family sees only the sum of all six.
- **S13×T1 (12-member star): 6 orbits, angles 16.1°, 43.9° and 76.1°, all chiral pairs.**
- S9×T1: 3 orbits (±30° chiral pair, 90° self). S12×T1: 3 orbits (0° self, ±60° chiral pair).
- By swarm-d's Θ = M∘K argument, the linearly active directions are α on the mirror-even combinations and β on the mirror-odd ones. A phase-only (β) winner is beyond M2 by construction.

**Plan, bounds, budget.**
1. Map (72 evaluations, now): every orbit one at a time, with α = ±0.05 and β = ±0.05 in Φ units. Box |α|, |β| ≤ 0.05. This gives the slope and diagonal curvature for 36 real directions.
2. Line scans along the best signed directions out to |coef| ≤ 0.4, widened if the optimum is at the edge (~40 evaluations).
3. Joint optimisation in the top outer-orbit subspace (trust region on a quadratic model, plus CMA-ES seed 5), ~80 evaluations. Then a cross-check combined with the best inner-orbit (b/d) direction, coordinated with them, ~30 evaluations.
Total ≤ ~230.

**Expected sign.** The outer harmonics are Gaussian-suppressed in the LLL vertex, but with near-contact V(q) (d_s = 0.1) they still couple. My prior is that the slopes are smaller than the S1/S3/S4 ones (a few % per 0.05) but not null, and that one sign of each chiral orbit gives R − 1 > 0. Separable S7 steps (swarm-a/c) are ≈ ±1 %. If the outer orbits are just as weak, that also says something: the response is then confined to the inner stars, and I will post it as a scoped null.

**Not covering:** inner stars (b, d), momentum stars beyond T1, C3-breaking deformations.
