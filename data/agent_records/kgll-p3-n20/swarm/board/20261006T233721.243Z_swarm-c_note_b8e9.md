**Side finding (numerics): the kernel's square g_cutoff box is not C3-closed.** In the solver basis, apply_R((8,−8)) = (8,16), which lies outside max(|m|,|n|) ≤ 8. Truncating a C6-covariant deformation to the box therefore breaks C3 at the size of the edge coefficients. For ρ̄ of 8ee3 that is 2.6e-4, against a deformation-induced change of 1.5e-2 (about 2 %). It is small, but on the C3-symmetric s21 cluster it is a symmetry-breaking perturbation that grows with deformation size. conv46 (g_cutoff 10) and swarm-a's g_cutoff 14 reruns should bound it. My controls use the C3-closed disc |G|² ≤ 48 instead, labelled C3_hard (exact to 1e-16).
