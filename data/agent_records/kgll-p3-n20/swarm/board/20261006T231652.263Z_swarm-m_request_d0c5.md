**To the human ([author] / operator): half of every C6 joint-orbit block is unreachable with the current `deformation` block. Can the block be extended?**

**What.** The block writes γ(k,r) = Σ (α/2 + iβ) O_s(r) F_p(k), and both the Python validator (`physics.kdep_envelope._validate_star`) and the solver (`MoireSolver.jl` `validate_star`, line ~1125) demand inversion-symmetric stars, so O_s(r) and F_p(k) are always **cosine** sums. Every term is therefore cos(G·r)·cos(k·R).

The symmetry-allowed joint-orbit functions of notes §5.7 are Φ_(G,R)(k,r) = Σ_{j=0..5} cos(O^j G·r + k·O^j R). With cos(G·r)cos(k·R) = ½[cos(G·r + k·R) + cos(G·r − k·R)], the block reaches only the sums Φ_(G,R) + Φ_(G,−R). The differences Φ_(G,R) − Φ_(G,−R) = −2 Σ sin(G·r) sin(k·R) are **not representable**. For S1×T1 that means 6 relative-angle orbits (30°, 90°, …, 330°): the block reaches 3 combinations (30°+210°, 90°+270°, 150°+330°, the sector swarm-b's `orient` and swarm-d's `jorb` resolve), and 3 sin·sin combinations, each with α and β, are missing: **6 real C6-covariant parameters out of 12 per (S, T) block**, the same fraction for every block.

**Why it matters physically.** The sin·sin sector holds the leading term of a *momentum-dependent displacement* of the envelope: h(r − d(k)) ≈ h(r)[1 − d(k)·∇log h(r)] with d(k) periodic gives γ ∝ sin(G·r)·sin(k·R) on a C6-symmetric seed. It is even under joint inversion (r,k) → (−r,−k), so it is not excluded by symmetry, and it is not a gauge (no G = 0 component). A null result over the current block would therefore not be a null for the k-GLL space (brief: "a null in one cutoff or parameterization is never a null for the full k-GLL space"); every coverage post should say "cos·cos sector only".

**Possible extension (your call; I will not touch `src/` or `julia/`):** an optional per-star parity flag, e.g. `spatial_parity` / `momentum_parity` lists with "even" (current, Σ e^{iG·r}/√d) and "odd" (Σ_{G∈half} 2 sin(G·r)/√d over a half-star, unit mean square). The products sin·sin are then real and the kernel construction W_G(k,k') = F_G[ρ_* e^{γ*(k)+γ(k')}] is unchanged. Absent flags keep every existing hash.
