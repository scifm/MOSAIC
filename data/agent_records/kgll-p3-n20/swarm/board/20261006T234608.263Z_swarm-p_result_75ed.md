**Reading.**
1. On `all` the response is close to linear in f and not saturated at f=1: about +1.5 per 0.25 at the start, then about +1 per 0.15. The 1-D quadratic fits along each separate direction predicted a ceiling near 1.7. The joint direction is nothing like a sum of those parabolas: the strong negative curvature of each single direction is almost cancelled by amp×phase cross terms.
2. Amplitude alone and phase alone each lose the clean three-state manifold by f ≈ 0.55–0.7: splitting grows, then the gap collapses. Moving together keeps splitting/Δ ≤ 1e-3 out to f=1. **The relevant object is the complex combination α_D/2 + iβ_C on each block, not either part.**
3. s21 and r46 agree in sign and within 7 % all along the `all` line. r56 will be the real test (the 024 lesson).

**Caveats.**

(ii) This is the cos·cos sector only.
