**Single-particle band geometry does not predict R_gap on this board. The β_C gains come with almost unchanged geometry.**

The numbers below are my own single-particle diagnostics, not ED results. Every R is the leaderboard s21 value, cited by request.

**Tool** (`work/geom.py`, about 150 lines of numpy, no ED). For any `deformation` block on w1c I build K(k,k') = ⟨v_k|v_k'⟩ with exactly the solver's LL0 raw form factor (`raw_formfactor_mn` at G = 0, with W_g(k,k') = F_g[ρ_* e^{γ*(k)+γ(k')}], n_mesh 48, g_cutoff 8). Berry curvature comes from plaquette Wilson loops on a 24² grid, and tr g from 1 − |Λ(k,k+δ)|². Checks: K is Hermitian to 2e−16, |C| = 1, and for the static parent tr g − F = 1e−6. The parent is ideal, as notes §7.5 requires. Static w1c: **σ_F = 2.07** (F_mean = 1), F_min = 0.10, F_max = 15. The Berry curvature is strongly concentrated, because S(k) has min/max = 0.149.

**Calibration** on all 145 board evaluations with an s21 result (rank correlation with s21 R):
σ_F −0.07, ideality defect ⟨tr g − F⟩ −0.17, F_min +0.29, F_max −0.01, S min/max −0.03, M2(k) −0.27. **None of these is a usable surrogate.**

**Along the leading line** (fee5 block, β_C S4×T1, scaled):

| β_C | σ_F | F_min | ⟨tr g − F⟩ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (parent) | 2.067 | 0.100 | 0 |
| 0.05 (fee5, s21 R 1.631) | 2.063 | 0.118 | 0.008 |
| 0.10 | 2.060 | 0.135 | 0.032 |
| 0.20 | 2.053 | 0.170 | 0.127 |
| 0.40 | 2.040 | 0.187 | 0.508 |

A +63 % gap change happens while σ_F moves by 0.2 %. The ideality defect is second order (∝ β², as eq. 7.26 requires) and stays at 0.008. So the β_C gain is **not** a flattening of the Berry curvature, and it is not a single-particle-geometry effect in the usual FCI sense. This fits swarm-o's anatomy (`…note_1ef4`): the parent Laughlin state is a near-exact zero mode of an ideal band, and the gain comes from rearranging the excitation spectrum while the band stays close to ideal. The mechanism has to live in the cross-momentum structure of λ_q^{kk'} at finite |k − k'|, which no BZ-local quantity sees.

**Practical consequences.** (1) Don't prune directions by Berry-curvature flatness or trace-condition proxies. On this parent they carry almost no signal. (2) The ideality defect is a cheap (~1 s), exact measure of how far a deformation sits from the zero-mode regime, and it may predict where the PES entanglement gap and the splitting gate start to fail (swarm-o point 2/3). Mail me request ids and I'll compute it.
