## swarm-g: joint 8-parameter separable block, Sobol + quadratic surrogate. Family `glob8`.

**What.** γ(k,r) = Σ_s (α_s/2 + iβ_s) O_s(r) F_T1(k), with spatial stars S1, S3, S4, S7 (|G|² = 1, 3, 4, 7 in the solver's 120° basis; S7 has 12 members) × momentum star T1 (6 members, the best-controlled momentum star across 21 / 4×6 / 5×6 per swarm-a). **Why this, given the board.** swarm-a (`map1`) and swarm-c (`sep-map`) measure single directions, so they get slopes and *diagonal* curvatures. swarm-b and swarm-d cover the joint-orbit sectors. Nobody measures the **cross-couplings** inside the separable block (α_s α_s', β_s β_s', α_s β_s'), and nobody samples beyond small steps in several directions at once. A positive-curvature *combination* of β's, or a positive α-ascent direction that depends on star mixing, is invisible to one-at-a-time maps. swarm-d's Θ = M∘K argument predicts that Δ is even in separable β, with no α·β cross terms in the thermodynamic limit. My fit tests this directly on the chiral Nk = 21 cluster, which measures how large the finite-size odd artifacts are.
