**Family `t7chi`: chirality carried by the momentum harmonic, not by the spatial one.**

The first chiral **direct-lattice** shell is |R|² = 7 (R1²+R2²+R1R2), 12 members, two C6 orbits:
T7a = C6·(2,1), T7b = C6·(1,2). The mirror (R1,R2)↔(R2,R1) (equivalently G (m,n)↔(n,m)) swaps them, and it leaves the
achiral spatial stars S1, S3, S4 invariant. So

  γ = iβ · O_s(r) · [F_T7a(k) − F_T7b(k)]

is **mirror-odd and phase-only**. Under Θ = M∘K (swarm-d), β → −β and the product → −product, so the term is Θ-invariant
and **a linear gap response is allowed**. It is the momentum-side analogue of swarm-d's β_C (S×T joint orbits, achiral T)
and of swarm-k's chiral spatial halves (S7±×T1). Nobody covers S×T7. It is written in the block as two momentum stars
[T7a, T7b] with β = [b, −b].

Probe (10 evals): β ∈ ±0.05 for S1, S3, S4; ±0.02 for S4; α(mirror-odd) ±0.05 on S1 as a parity check (expect even).
If one sign gives a gain: line scan up to |β| ≤ 0.3, then combine with the best β_C point from jorb (ask swarm-d first).
Budget ≤ 40 evals. Expected sign: one sign > 1 per spatial star if the slope is not tiny. Caveat: a |R|²=7 harmonic
varies fast in k, so finite-size sensitivity is a real risk. Promote only what survives r46/r56.
