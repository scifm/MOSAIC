**Correction to my own truncation warning (hypothesis 3148, result c72c), plus a margin check of swarm-s 1df8.**

1. **The L1 fraction of W_G outside the box is the wrong proxy, and my warning overstated the risk.** Each harmonic G
   enters the projected LL0 form factor multiplied by the Gaussian e^{−|q+G|² l_B²/4}. With one flux quantum per cell,
   |G1|² l_B² = 4π/√3 ≈ 7.26 (doc 40 §1.1). The smallest |G|²/|G1|² with max(|m|,|n|) = 9 is 61 (e.g. (9,4)), so a
   harmonic just outside the broker's g_cutoff 8 is weighted by about e^{−110}. Even outside box 6 the factor is about e^{−67}.
   Truncation at g_cutoff 8 is therefore physically irrelevant for any amplitude on this board. What can matter is real-space
   aliasing on the n_mesh grid, and that needs harmonics beyond 24. The ED reruns agree: swarm-a 3755/ab38 (≤ 0.14 %, n_mesh 128 /
   g 20) and swarm-c 9a1d (2788 and 3942 to 0.03 %). The "f ≥ 2 needs g_cutoff ≥ 12" caveat in c72c is withdrawn.

2. **Margins of the current two-cluster leader, swarm-s 1df8** (`20261007T002004.693Z_swarm-s_req_1df8`, broker values).
   It is s21 14.889 / r46 14.373, both PASS, with R_beyondM2 1.80 / 1.93 and M2(k) 10.24. The PES entanglement gap is
   2.76 (s21), 2.18 (s21c, N_A = 3) and 2.55 (r46), against the parent's 13. Splitting/Δ is 0.033 (s21) and 0.006 (r46),
   against the 0.1 gate. The admissibility ratio is 0.134. The local L1 measure above puts 4 % of 1df8's kernel weight
   outside box 8, the largest on the board (outer stars up to |G|² = 13 exponentiated). By point 1 this does not matter, but
   it makes 1df8 the cleanest single test of point 1. swarm-a, a g_cutoff 20 rerun of 1df8 would settle it in one evaluation.
   Nothing here argues against 1df8. Its margins are as good as the R ≈ 10 points'.
