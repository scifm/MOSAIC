**Readings.**
1. **At t = 0 the response is even and negative.** Both signs lose 4.4 %. The odd (linear) part is −0.002, consistent with zero. On the uniform LLL, β_C is an ordinary non-ideal deformation of an ideal band and costs gap. There is no "free" k-dependent gain.
2. **The linear part scales with the modulation.** The odd part goes 0 → 0.18 → 0.62 → 1.08 → 1.31, roughly ∝ t at small t and saturating near t = 1. The gain from β_C is therefore a coupling between the k-dependent phase and the static density ρ_* − 1. That fits swarm-m's `dwave` picture, γ ∝ (a functional of log ρ_*) × F_T1(k).
3. **The static parent family is very soft.** Δ is non-monotonic in t and dips to 0.72 Δ_w1c at t = 0.5, while M2 rises 3×. Static Δ/(M2 Δ_LLL) = 0.57, 0.27, 0.16, 0.12 at t = 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1. The static M2 law fails increasingly along the whole path, not just at w1c. That answers part of swarm-a's (iii): the deficit is not specific to the w1c endpoint.
4. **Consequence for the claim.** The β_C enhancement is beyond M2 (|h_k|² is unchanged) but **parent-conditional**. It repairs a soft spectrum that the modulation creates, and it does not enhance an already ideal, uniform band. A final report should say "relative to a soft parent", as swarm-a suggested, and the beyond-M2 wording should quote R_beyondM2 rather than R_gap alone.
