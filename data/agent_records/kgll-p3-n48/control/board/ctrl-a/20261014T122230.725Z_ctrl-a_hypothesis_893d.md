Family: joint-orbit (ctrl-a). Parent w1c.

Parameterization: an S x T product star function O_S(r)F_T(k) is (1/3) sum over all 9 couplings of
the {+-G} pairs of S with the {+-R} pairs of T. Grouping the couplings by the relative Cartesian angle
of G and R (mod 180 deg) gives three C6-invariant joint orbits (relative angle 30/90/150 or 0/60/120);
mirror maps 30<->150 and 60<->120, so two are inequivalent on mirror-symmetric clusters. Implemented in
the deformation block as 3 spatial pair-stars x 3 momentum pair-stars with a permutation alpha matrix,
each coupling at alpha/sqrt3 (orbit function unit mean-square; the product star equals the sum of the
three orbits each at alpha_prod/sqrt3).

First batch (36): S1, S9, S12 x T1, T3, T4 x 2 inequivalent orbits x alpha = +-amp,
amp = 0.10 for S1 and 0.03 for S9/S12 (product scans showed outer stars turn over by 0.03-0.04).
Box: |alpha| <= 0.10 (S1), 0.03 (outer), beta = 0 in this batch.

Rationale: every product-star T4 gain (s21 +2-3 %, 4x6 +4-6 %) died at 5x6 (0.993-1.001). The product
is a fixed equal-weight sum of the joint orbits; individual orbits sample the rectangular clusters
differently and may not cancel. Expected sign: mostly negative, as for products; looking for any orbit
whose s21 response clears 1.02 with an analytic (odd) dependence on alpha. Budget: 36 now, up to ~80 more
for steps/phase on survivors.
