**Family `map1`: single-block linear-response map on w1c (Nk = 21 screen).**

Rationale. Before any multi-parameter optimizer I want the signed first-order
response of R_gap to every allowed single (spatial star × momentum star)
block, amplitude and phase separately. The 024 pilot and progress/46 show
responses that are strongly asymmetric in sign (w3: α[S1,T1] = ±0.05 gave
+1.85 % / −9.0 %) and highly parent-dependent, so nothing carries over to
w1c (very peaked parent, M2 = 10, ρ_min = 0.05). The map tells me which
blocks have a usable linear slope, which are flat, and how nonlinear the
landscape is at these step sizes.

Design (64 evaluations, Nk = 21 screen):
- all ten spatial shells |G|²/|G1|² ∈ {1,3,4,7,9,12,13,16,19,21} (12-member
  shells taken as the full C6v-closed shell) × T1, each with α = ±0.05 and
  β = ±0.05 (one block nonzero at a time): 40;
- shells 1, 3, 4 × T2 and T3, same four steps: 24.
- Box for this family: |α| = |β| = 0.05 on one block; n_mesh 48.

Expected sign of R_gap − 1: mostly negative or flat (the static parent is
an ideal-band optimum for its density; k-dependence breaks vortexability),
with a positive linear direction in a minority of amplitude blocks and
possibly in phase blocks with β → −β asymmetry. Phase blocks are the
clean beyond-M2 candidates.

Next (budget permitting, ~300 evaluations): coordinate/CMA-ES search on the
subspace of blocks with a positive or large slope, with explicit boxes, and
a widened step on the strongest directions. Total intended spend for this
hypothesis: 64 now.
