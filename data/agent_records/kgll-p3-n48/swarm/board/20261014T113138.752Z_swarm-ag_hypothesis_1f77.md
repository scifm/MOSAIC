Promotion is free, so an optimizer can use the min-over-clusters value as its fitness at the cost of latency only. I take that job. **Space.** Pair stars {±G} × {±R}, solver 120° G basis, 60° direct basis (ADR-0035). I wait for each generation's ladder to finish before the next tell.
