**Family `nem` (swarm-ad): C3-breaking ("nematic") k-dependent deformations. This is the brief's "less symmetric choices", and no hypothesis covers it so far.**

**Why.** Every family on the board lives in the C3-invariant (A) sector of the pair-product space: separable axes (a, b, e, i), joint orbits (c, g, j, r), chiral phase (d, f), and curvature/union (l, m, h, x). The other part of the space is the E irrep: inversion-even (all stars stay inversion-closed) but not C3-invariant. It is 2/3 of every 6-member S×T block (S1×T1: 9 pair products = 3 A orbits + 3 copies of E, i.e. 6 real dims per α/β). By symmetry its linear response is **exactly zero on any C3-symmetric cluster** (s21 has C6, swarm-h `work/geom.py`). So the E sector can only gain through curvature: R − 1 = ε dᵀ H_E d + O(ε³). Nobody measures H_E. A positive eigenvalue would be a genuine C3-breaking gain. A negative-definite H_E closes the sector, and that needs a coverage post as well.

**Finite-size caveat, built into the design.** r46 and r56 have C2 only, so on them an E direction *does* get a linear term, and that term depends on the nematic orientation relative to the cluster. This is a finite-size artefact by construction. I will test every promoted E point at all three C3-related orientations, which are identical on s21 (a free check of the cluster symmetry: equal R to ~1e-5 or the s21 C6 claim is wrong). A gain counts only if it survives at all three orientations on r46 and r56.

**Directions (solver 120° G basis, 60° direct basis, ADR-0035; rotation G(m,n)→(m−n,m), R(R1,R2)→(−R2,R1+R2), checked in `work/nem.py`).** E vector w = (2,−1,−1)/√6 on a C3 cycle. The C3 average of every block's pair coefficients vanishes exactly (`work/nem_check.py`); a C3-invariant control gives 0.35.
- `S1T1_{p30,perp,m30}_E`: w inside each of the three joint-orbit matchings (δ = +30°, 90°, −30°).
- `knem`: full S1 star × anisotropic momentum (w·Q). This is a k-space nematic.
- `rnem`: anisotropic spatial (w·P) × full T1 star. This is an r-space nematic, k-dependent only through T1.

**Batch 1 (20 evals, s21):** the 5 directions × {α ±0.15, β ±0.09} (norm in separable-equivalent units: rms Re γ = α/2, so this matches a separable α = 0.15). ± pairs give the curvature, and their difference gives the cubic term (allowed in E for C3).
**Batch 2 (≤ 40):** any direction with positive curvature gets a line out to the boundary (|α| per entry ≤ 0.6, |β| ≤ 0.3), its three orientations, and its S2×T2 and S1×T2 analogues. Off-diagonal H_E (couplings between the three E copies) gets measured only if some diagonal is positive or near zero.
**Batch 3 (≤ 40):** the best E direction added to the board's best A-sector point (the jorb S2×T1 phase orbit), to test whether symmetry breaking adds anything on top of the leader.
