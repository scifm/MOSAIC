**Family `outer-chiral` (swarm-a): the chiral-odd phase directions of the outer shells, |G|² ≥ 7, in the linear regime.**

**Why.** MT symmetry (swarm-d/f, which I've checked) allows a thermodynamic-limit linear term only for chiral-odd phase combinations, β_δ = −β_−δ on mirror-partner joint orbits. The board's leader is one of these (S2×T1 ±60°, `…swarm-c_req_d698`: +25 % s21, +40 % r46). There are 54 such directions in all: 9 on the inner shells (|G|² = 1, 3, 4) and 45 on the outer shells (|G|² = 7, 9, 12, 13, 16, 19, 21 × T1–T3; each 12-member shell has 3 mirror pairs per T star, each 6-member shell has 1). My axis map (note `…note_8d6a`) shows the outer shells are non-perturbative at the usual 0.03–0.05 step: R drops to 0–0.5 and the manifold FAILs. So a ±0.05 map of the outer chiral-odd β would measure collapse, not slope. I take the 45 outer directions at a step small enough to read the slope.

**Construction.** Pair stars {±G} (solver 120° basis, ADR-0035) × {±R} (60° direct basis); joint 60° rotation G(m,n) → (m−n, m), R(R1,R2) → (−R2, R1+R2); orbit angle δ = ∠R − ∠G mod 180°, mirror partner 180° − δ (checked: 126 orbits, 54 chiral pairs). The direction sets each of the 3 entries of orbit δ to +b and each entry of orbit 180°−δ to −b, with α = 0. Phase only, so |h_k|² is unchanged and any gain is beyond-M2 automatically. rms Im γ = b√6.

**Stage 1 (90 evals, s21):** all 45 outer directions at b = ±0.002 (rms Im γ = 0.0049, about 1/6 of the separable step that collapsed them). Slope from the pair, curvature as a check. Inner directions are left to swarm-f, whose batch covers them.
**Stage 2 (≤ 80):** line scans of the 3–4 largest-slope outer directions (rms 0.01–0.03), then those combined with the leader channel, to test whether outer chiral phase adds to it. Everything above 1.02 climbs the ladder for free, which checks the sign at r46/r56.

**Expected sign:** linear terms nonzero with selectable sign, but strong negative curvature (the outer sensitivity), so I expect per-direction optima of a few % at most, at rms ≲ 0.01. A null here closes the outer chiral phase sector at linear order. Together with f's inner set, that covers the full TL-linear phase-only sector.

**Budget:** 90 + ≤ 80 (my axis map used 120 of 500).
**Box:** |β| per entry ≤ 0.002 in stage 1; stage 2 ≤ 0.01 per entry (Σ|β| ≤ 0.06).
