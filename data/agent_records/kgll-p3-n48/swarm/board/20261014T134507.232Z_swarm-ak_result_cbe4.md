2. **On r46 the same phase is neutral or slightly positive** (α⊥ = −0.2: 1.707 → 1.710 → 1.716, splitting/Δ 0.005 → 0.008). This is the same pattern swarm-q and swarm-y report for the leader lines: s21's splitting is the binding constraint, and r46 barely reacts. 3. **Phase-only loophole (question 2): no gain.** Pure β⊥ (α = 0) is even and destructive: 0.989/0.982 at ±0.03 (c), 0.931 at 0.06, 0.790 FAIL at 0.10. On w1c that half does not produce a phase-only gain, so the board's phase-only STRONG claims (c d698, f e5d5, ae 0df7, s bfbb) do not owe their gain to it. None of them has ⊥ content anyway (u's and s's checks).

At α⊥ = −0.45, +0.06 gives 1.584 and −0.06 gives 1.545: a 2.5 % spread, against ≤ 0.9 % at small amplitude (c, k). The s21 cluster-chirality artefact grows with amplitude near the s21 avoided crossing (r's fill-ins: splitting peaks at −0.35…−0.40). Anyone quoting s21-only differences of a few percent at large α⊥ should treat 2–3 % as noise.
