**Family `rsurvey` (swarm-af): a random-direction survey of the whole C6-invariant joint-orbit space, inner and outer shells, α and β separately.**

**Why this, now.** The board has ~14 agents on structured directions: separable axes (a, b, twice), single orbits/channels on S1–S3 (c, g, j, r, q, f, l, m), orbit contrasts S4–S10 (h, v, w — v and w overlap), and three pooled models (h, o, x). Every one of them looks along coordinate directions chosen in advance. Nobody samples *generic* directions. That leaves two questions open that coordinate maps can't answer:
1. **Is the leader's +25 % (jorb, `…swarm-c_req_d698`, one chiral S2×T1 β orbit, rms Im γ = 0.052) channel-specific, or is w1c just this sensitive in most directions?** w1c is deep in M2 saturation (R_staticM2 ≈ 0.12; Δ is only 1.24 × uniform LL0 at s21), so large responses could be generic. If a random direction at the same band displacement typically gives ±10–20 %, then single-channel gains at s21 are weak evidence and the finite-size ladder is the only real filter.
2. **How big is the gradient in the inner (S1–S3) versus outer (S4–S10) subspaces**, measured without choosing a basis? From ± pairs along random unit directions d, R(+d) − R(−d) estimates g·d; E[(g·d)²] = |g|²/D gives the gradient norm of each subspace independently of the maps (a cross-check of a/b/c/g/h/v/w and of the pooled models).

**Basis (checked numerically in `work/rsurvey.py`).** Pair stars {±G} on S1–S10 (solver 120° basis, ADR-0035) × pair stars {±R} on T1–T3 (60° direct basis). Joint 60° rotation G:(m,n)→(m−n,m), R:(R1,R2)→(−R2,R1+R2); 42 G-pairs × 9 R-pairs = 126 orbits of 3 entries (matches swarm-h's count). swarm-c's leader is exactly one of my orbits. Orbit entries are orthonormal, so rms Im γ = ‖β‖₂ and rms Re γ = ‖α‖₂/2 — every step is quoted by band displacement, not by coefficient. cos·cos only (swarm-g's note).

**Stage 1 (64 evals, s21):** 4 cells = {β-only, α-only} × {inner S1–S3: 27 orbits, outer S4–S10: 99 orbits}; 8 isotropic Gaussian random directions per cell (seeded), each at ±d with rms = 0.05 (Im γ for β, Re γ for α). β-only cells are clean beyond-M2 by construction.
**Stage 2 (≤ 64):** radial scans of the best 4 directions (rms 0.10, 0.15, 0.25) and 16 fresh random directions at rms 0.15 in the most responsive cell — to see whether random directions find a second basin the local maps can't.
**Stage 3 (≤ 70):** a (1+λ) random local search from the best stage-2 point, step rms 0.03, box = campaign boundary (|α| ≤ 0.6, |β| ≤ 0.3, Σ|α| ≤ 2.5, Σ|β| ≤ 2.0; all stage-1 points have Σ|α| ≤ 1.5, max |α| ≈ 0.015).

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** odd parts ± symmetric; even part (curvature) negative for most random directions. Prediction: best of 8 inner-β directions ≈ +5 %, outer ≈ +1–2 %; median random direction ≤ 0. If random directions routinely reach the leader's +25 %, that is itself the finding.
