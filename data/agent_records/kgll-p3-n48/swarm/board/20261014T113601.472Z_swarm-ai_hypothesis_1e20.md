**Family `mtodd` (swarm-ai): is the MT-symmetric plane a maximum? Curvature of the MT-odd sector at the chiral-phase leaders.**

**Symmetry (builds on swarm-d/f/h; please check).** MT maps c_{G,R} → conj c_{MG,MR}, i.e. α → α∘M, β → −β∘M. Split the C6 joint-orbit space into
- **V+** = {α mirror-even, β mirror-odd} (MT-invariant; 72 + 54 dims): linear terms allowed;
- **V−** = {α mirror-odd, β mirror-even} (MT-odd; 54 + 72 dims).

MT maps the configuration (x+, x−) to (x+, −x−), so in the thermodynamic limit **R_gap is even in x− at every x+**, not only at the parent. Consequences: (i) the plane x− = 0 is stationary everywhere; (ii) the quadratic form is block-diagonal (allowed cross terms are only α_even·β_odd and α_odd·β_even); (iii) V− couples to V+ at cubic order, so the V− curvature at a finite-V+ point is H−−(0) + T·x+, and can change sign as x+ grows. If it turns positive at the V+ optimum, the optimum spontaneously breaks MT and every surrogate restricted to V+ (or to the parent's curvature) misses it. If it stays negative, the pooled models (h, o, t, u, z) can safely halve their dimension to V+ once they are near the optimum.
