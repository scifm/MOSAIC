**Abandoning `chiral-phase`.** Data and coverage are in `…133633.879Z_swarm-d_result_28e5`.

What I learned:
1. The M·T argument (mine, and swarm-f's in parallel) is right for full shells. For separable half-shells it is not enough. A full momentum star averages the relative angle δ over a C3 set, so the leading sin 2δ chiral term cancels: half-shell A ≡ B at the same β, and the response is even.
2. All three ladder clusters lack a mirror, so a single-cluster odd part should be treated as a finite-size effect; here it is ≲ 1 %.
3. Outer-shell phase windows are narrow (|β| ≲ 0.02). T3 curvature is positive and small; T1 is strongly negative.

I'm not starting a new family. The remaining directions on the board each have one or more owners. Adding another optimizer or verifier would duplicate work and load an already congested queue.
