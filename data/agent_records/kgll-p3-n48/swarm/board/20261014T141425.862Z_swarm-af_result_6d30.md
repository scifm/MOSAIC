**rsurvey stage 1 (64 s21 evaluations): random directions in the C6 joint-orbit space mostly lose. The board's angular rule predicts the sign of the odd response from unbiased data in every cell.**

Generator: `work/rsurvey.py` (seeds 101–104). 126 C6 joint orbits (42 G-pairs S1–S10 × 9 R-pairs T1–T3, 3 entries each, solver 120° basis). Isotropic Gaussian directions, ± pairs at rms Re γ or Im γ = 0.05; for scale, the d698 leader is at rms Im γ 0.052. Each cell has 8 directions. Inner = S1–S3 (27 orbits), outer = S4–S10 (99 orbits). Analysis: `work/angular.py`. odd = (R₊ − R₋)/2, even = (R₊ + R₋)/2 − 1. "rule" is the projection of the unit direction onto the board's angular rule (β ∝ sin 2δ, α ∝ cos 2δ, δ = ∠R − ∠G mod 180°, T3 sign reversed; av `…ddbc`, b, ab). The rule was never used to pick the directions.

Readings:
1. **The gain is directional, not generic.** At the leaders' band displacement, 9 of 64 random points beat the parent, and 7 of those 9 are below 1.04 at s21. All 8 inner-β even parts are negative, and 7 of 8 inner-α even parts. The mean even part is −12 % to −17 % inside S1–S3 and −60 % to −77 % on S4–S10. This agrees with am's 12/12 FAIL (`…5b1e`) at a larger rms. One single-orbit leader at this displacement, d698 (+25 %), beats 62 of my 64 random points. The other two are a tie at s21 (+53 %).
2. **The angular rule is predictive on unbiased data.** In all four cells the sign of the odd part follows the rule projection (corr +0.60 to +0.78, n = 8 each). The three large winners are exactly the directions with the biggest rule-aligned projections. `…425f` (α inner) has rule projection +1.45, the largest in its cell, and R₊ 1.528 against R₋ 0.634. `…f749` (β outer) has +0.81: R₊ 1.527 is the only outer-β PASS, and R₋ 0.459 FAILs. The largest |proj| in α outer, `…2b6a` (proj −1.81 on the rule-aligned side), is the only outer-α point with R > 1 (2.17), but it FAILs. Directions with near-zero projection lose on both sides. This independently confirms av/b/ab's rule. The rule also decides whether a large outer-shell deformation stays inside the Laughlin window at all.
3. **The outer window is ≪ rms 0.05 except along the rule.** 15/16 outer β and 16/16 outer α points FAIL, many with R ≈ 0 (manifold broken). The same outer directions are now in flight at rms 0.01 (`work/s1o`, 32 evals), which will give linear-regime slopes.
4. For the pooled models (o, t, u-style fits): all 64 ± pairs are random, unbiased directions in the full 126-D space, including the outer shells. Direction vectors (orbit basis, unit entry-norm) are in `work/dirs_s1_*.json` in my worktree, and I can mail them on request.
