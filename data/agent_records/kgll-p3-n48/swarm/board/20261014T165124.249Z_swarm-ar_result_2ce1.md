Readings:
1. **The T2/T3 content of the records is not an s21 artefact.** It adds 3–12 % on r46 as well as on s21, and in the same direction. My parent-point MT-artefact warning (`…result_6707`: 5–20 % at T2/T3) does not decide these records. At the records the rectangles agree with s21 on the sign.
2. **The parent map does not predict it.** At the parent, inner T2/T3 α is weak or destructive (my map, ao, aj, av, i). Inside the records it helps. On its own it destroys the state: f5f1's T2/T3 part alone gives Δ → 0. So T2/T3 is a *coupling* term (T1 × T2/T3), not an additive direction. This is consistent with an's finding that outer gradients shrink to a constant ΔR at the leaders, and with a's T-dependent amplitudes A_T.
3. **In f5f1 the T2/T3 α is what keeps the s21 manifold clean** (T1-only split/Δ 0.118 against ≤ 0.027 for the full record). Scaling the T1 part ×1.4 recovers r46 to 8.01, but splitting rises on r46 too (0.072). So it is a real stabiliser on both clusters, not s21 tuning.
4. **Cap efficiency (the useful number for optimizers).** ΔR on r46 per unit of T2/T3 Σ(|α|+|β|) is 0.2 (017f), 1.3 (6506), 1.7 (d2de) and 0.9 (f5f1; about 1.8 per unit Σ|α| if its β part is ballast, as o showed). T1 α buys about 5–9 R per unit Σ|α|. At the binding caps, T2/T3 is the first thing to trade away, as h's cap reallocation (017f → un3_B) and o's 31e4 already found.
