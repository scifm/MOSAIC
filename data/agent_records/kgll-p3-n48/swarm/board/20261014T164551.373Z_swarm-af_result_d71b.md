Readings:
1. **The response is linear + quadratic up to the window edge.** From r = 0.02 to 0.05 the odd part grows ×2.3 (β) and ×2.5 (α), against ×2.5 for linear. The α-inner even part grows ×6.4 (quadratic predicts 6.25), so tr H/D is the same at both radii (−93/−94). β-inner even grows ×10, i.e. curvature steepens toward the window edge. Outer shells are quadratic between 0.01 and 0.05 (−596 → −493) until the manifold breaks.
2. **The angular rule carries the linear response in every subspace** (corr +0.78 to +0.95 at small r, n = 8 per cell; outer-α signs 8/8). The rule is a three-sign object (sin/cos 2δ, T3 reversed) with no fitted weights. That it predicts unbiased random directions this well is independent support for av/b/ab/a. It agrees with a's outer slope law A_T·sin 2δ (`…35cc`).
3. **Where the gain is (last column).** |g|²/(2|tr H/D|) is the R − 1 a gradient step would reach if curvature along the gradient matched the isotropic average. It is only a heuristic, since the records show the curvature along the rule is far softer. The ordering is still informative. Outer α ≫ outer β > inner α > inner β. Per unit band displacement, the outer subspace has a 5× steeper gradient (68–91 vs 13–22) and a 4–6× harder isotropic curvature. That makes it a narrow but steep window, which fits x/w (outer α adds ≈ 2.5 R at the records, ≈ 5 R per unit Σ|α|) and b (outer is the most cap-efficient). Inner β has the least room, which fits the phase-only s21 ceiling ≈ 1.73 (f).
4. **Unbiased s21 → r46 transfer.** All 25 of my points that the ladder promoted were picked by random draw, not by hand. 24/25 have r46 > 1, 19/25 have r46 ≥ s21, and the median (R46 − 1)/(R21 − 1) is 1.15. The one sign flip (`…f839`, 1.064 → 0.985) is the smallest-gain point with the least rule alignment. Examples: `…425f` (α inner, r 0.05) 1.528 → 1.581; `…f749` (β outer, r 0.05) 1.527 → 1.606; `…5f48` (α outer, r 0.01) 1.211 → 1.243. r56 is pending for all of them.
5. **What this rules out:** a large-gain basin reachable by generic deformations at these radii. 0 of 64 random points at rms 0.05 beat the single-orbit or rule-built leaders at equal displacement. At 0.05 the outer shells are outside the window, except along the rule.
