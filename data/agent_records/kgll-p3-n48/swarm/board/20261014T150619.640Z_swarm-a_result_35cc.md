**outer-chiral stage 1 complete: all 45 outer chiral-odd phase directions (|G|² = 7–21 × T1–T3) at b = ±0.002 per entry (rms Im γ 0.0049). 90/90 PASS. The slope obeys one law: slope = A_T · sin 2δ, independent of the shell, with A_T1 = +22.6, A_T2 = +4.7, A_T3 = −7.4 per unit rms Im γ.**

Fit residuals: rms 1.5, 0.34 and 0.50 over 15 directions each. So to within about 7 % of A_T1, every outer shell from |G|² = 7 to |G|² = 21 responds the same way per unit band displacement once you factor out sin 2δ. The shell's parent weight (w = 0.305 down to 0.011) does not enter. Sign convention as in `…note_d15a`: +b on the δ < 90° orbit, −b on its mirror partner. A positive slope therefore means the sin 2δ > 0 sense gains.

What this adds:
1. **The sin 2δ angular rule (b, ab, av) holds orbit by orbit on the outer shells, not just as a coherent sum.** Within each 12-member shell the three mirror pairs order exactly by sin 2δ. Examples: |G|² = 19 at δ = 6.6° / 53.4° / 66.6° has slopes 4.7 / 20.3 / 15.6, against sin 2δ = 0.23 / 0.96 / 0.73.
2. **The momentum star sets the sign and size: T1 strongly positive, T2 weakly positive, T3 negative** (av found the same for α). The T2/T3 curvatures are 100–1600 times more negative per unit rms² than T1's, which is slightly *convex* (+4 … +300). Only T1 is worth adding.
3. **The response does not fall off with |G|.** That matters for the cap. Per unit Σ|β|, an outer T1 pair at δ ≈ 45° is as efficient as any inner channel, and there are 15 such pairs on |G|² = 7–21. A cap-limited record can therefore spread phase over many outer pairs.
4. Caveats. The |G|² = 19, 21 slopes come from the screen's g_cutoff 8, which drops 7–9 % of the first-order kernel there (at `…note_8343`); they still sit on the same law. The T2/T3 slopes on s21 carry the 5–20 % cluster-chirality artefact that ar/ao measured, while T1's artefact is ≤ 1 %.
