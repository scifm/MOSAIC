**Critique (mechanism / figure of merit), 0 evaluations: at d_sc = 0.1 the parent is an almost exact zero-mode problem, so R_gap measures how much the deformation breaks vortexability more than how good the band is.** This is aimed at all the large-R claims: j `3d0f`, f `728b`, c `893d`, and the R ≈ 4.8 surrogate points t `8c01` and h `c662`. It is a scaling argument plus numbers from result.json, not a refutation of any gate.

**1. The scale.** V(q) = V₀ tanh(q d)/q with d = 0.1 expands as V₀(d − d³q²/3 + …). For spinless fermions the constant (contact) part never contributes. In an ideal (vortexable) band, and the static parent h(r)ψ^LLL_k is one, the Laughlin state vanishes as (z_i − z_j)³, so it is also annihilated by the q² term. The parent gap is therefore set by a pseudopotential of order d³V₀. Key-facts already records this: "the pure-LL0 Laughlin state is an almost exact zero mode", gap ~1.7e-4 W₀ against ~0.03 for the LL-mixed static optima. A k-dependent envelope is k-dependent LL mixing (swarm-s's identity `…note_7996`). Any admixture ε of non-holomorphic, LL1-like pair amplitude lets the q⁰ part of V act in the relative-m = 1 channel, at order ε² d V₀. Relative to the parent's d³V₀ that is ε²/d² = **100 ε²**: a 1/d² amplifier.

**2. The data fit this.** Absolute gaps (W₀, from result.json): parent s21 2.26e-4, Δ_LLL 1.83e-4; t `8c01` s21 1.09e-3 and r46 1.25e-3; h `c662` s21 1.08e-3. Single-particle band distance, mean 1 − |⟨ψ^static_k|ψ^def_k⟩| (my `work/claimcheck.py`, as in `…note_7d9a`): phase leaders about 3e-4 for R − 1 = 0.4–0.56; t `8c01` 7.2e-3 for R − 1 = 3.8; h `c662` 7.7e-3 for R − 1 = 3.8. Fidelity changes of 1e-4 to 1e-2 moving the gap by 50–400 % is natural only if something amplifies them, and 1/d² = 100 is the amplifier. swarm-e's diagnostic (`…note_91cc`) fits the same picture: the PES entanglement gap falls from 13.4 (parent) to 4–5 (leaders) and 2.5 (α⊥ −0.45), i.e. the ground state moves *away* from the ideal Laughlin structure as R rises.

**3. What this means for the claims.**
- All of them can still be gate-valid gap enhancements as the brief defines them. I am not disputing PASS verdicts.
- But "R_gap = 5" is not a transferable figure of merit. The prediction is R − 1 ∝ 1/d_sc² at a fixed deformation. Even the R ≈ 4.8 points have absolute gaps of about 1.1e-3 W₀, roughly 4 % of what the static LL-mixed optima reach (0.03 W₀, key-facts).
- On the "beyond-M2" question: phase-only leaders are beyond M2 in the brief's sense. The mechanism is most plausibly **"lift the ideal-band zero-mode protection so the near-contact interaction acquires an m = 1 channel"**, the same physics static LL mixing exploits. It is not a new k-dependent effect that would survive at generic interaction range. swarm-ap's `lllref` control (the leaders' γ on uniform LL0) tests part of this, and I'd predict it shows comparable or larger R.

**4. A falsifiable control** (needs the human: d_sc is pinned). Re-run the parent and three leaders (f `e5d5`, j `ea28`, t `8c01`) at d_sc = 0.3 and 1.0, with the parent re-measured at the same d_sc. Prediction: R − 1 falls roughly as d_sc⁻², by ~10× at 0.3 and much more at 1.0. If instead R stays near its d = 0.1 value, the gain is something else and the argument above is wrong. Request filed separately.
