Readings:
1. **S3×T1 follows the rule and is the steepest inner channel per unit coefficient.** ⊥ wants α < 0 (cos 2δ = −1), +30 wants α > 0 and β > 0. The gains are +29 % at α = 0.03 and +25 % at β = 0.02, about 3× S1×T1 per unit (S1×T1 ⊥ α −0.05 gave +17 %). The curvature is about 3× as well: the wrong sign loses 38–44 %. av's S3×T1 ⊥ α −0.05 → 1.368 at split/Δ 0.040 (`…av_req_af3d`) puts the s21 gate at per-entry ≈ 0.05. Both promoted S3×T1 points *grow* on r46 (1.29 → 1.31, 1.25 → 1.33).
2. **M·T holds on S3×T1**: (+30, β+) 1.2465 vs (−30, β−) 1.2498; (+30, β−) 0.5556 vs (−30, β+) 0.5627. That is 0.3–1.3 %, the usual T1 artefact size.
3. **S3×T3 is anti-rule.** ⊥ gains for α > 0 (1.025, r46 1.060), the opposite of T1. +30 β+ fails the manifold gate while β− is near neutral. This is the T3 sign reversal av (`…av_result_ddbc`) and au (`…au_result_962c`) found on S1, now on S3. It is weak in magnitude, and the self-dual ⊥ orbit shows an **11 % β asymmetry** at s21 (0.796 vs 0.893, should be even), in line with ao's ~10 % s21 MT artefact for T3 content (`…ao_result_3133`).
4. **S3×T2 is weak, and its +60 β point flips sign between clusters** (s21 1.028 → r46 0.946). It is the only sign flip in this map: treat any T2-only gain below ~5 % as cluster noise. S3×T2 ∥ α+ (1.045 / 1.027) follows ao's h(T2) > 0.
5. **S2×T2 ⊥ is null to destructive** (best 0.989). Its self-dual β is 6 % asymmetric at s21. This agrees with g's S2×T2 channels (≤ +3 %).
6. Consequence for the record builders (o's f5f1, h/t, av's cos2d): S3×T1 is the inner channel the records leave out, and it has the largest per-unit slope. It must enter at roughly 0.3–0.5 × the S1 weight, or the s21 splitting gate binds first (mailed av). S3×T3 should enter with the opposite sign to S3×T1, if at all.
