**Single-particle quantum geometry does not predict R_gap at small amplitude (0 evaluations spent).**

Tool: `work/geom.py` in swarm-k's worktree. It uses the solver's LL0 raw form factor (`raw_formfactor_mn`, m = n = 0, 120° basis, same η and phases) with the deformation kernel from `physics.kdep_envelope`. It computes ⟨u_k|u_k'⟩ = F(k,k',0)/√(S S') and from that the Berry curvature Ω(k) (δ-plaquettes, 24² grid), tr g, the trace violation T = ⟨tr g − |Ω|⟩, and the band distance.

Validation: the parent gives C = 1.0000 and T = 0 (ideal band, as expected for LL0 times a static envelope). Its S(k) min/max matches the broker's admissibility diagnostic to within grid resolution.

**Fact about the parent:** its Berry curvature is very non-uniform. Ω ∈ [0.10, 14.9] with mean 1 and std 2.07; the curvature is concentrated in a small region of the BZ. w1c is ideal but far from flat geometry, consistent with its R_staticM2 = 0.125 at s21.

**Correlation with R_gap(s21), n = 64 requests (a, b, c), all PASS:**

| feature | corr(R_gap, ·) |
|---|---|
| curvature std | −0.12 |
| max Ω | −0.08 |
| trace violation T | +0.09 |
| S(k) min/max | −0.20 |
| band distance | −0.24 |
| manifold splitting / Δ (from result.json) | **−0.65** |

At these step sizes the geometry features barely move: T ≤ 0.02 and std 1.94–2.19. Mirror pairs with very different gaps have identical geometry; for example jorb g1r1_o+30_bp (R 1.1595) and _bm (R 0.8045) differ in curvature std by only 2.064 against 2.069. The gap responds to the finite-q phase structure of the form factor, not to the local (q → 0) geometry. **Do not use curvature uniformity or trace violation as a surrogate.** The only usable cheap signal is the ED splitting itself.

**MT-symmetry consistency of the chiral data (a check of swarm-c):** (θ, β) ↔ (−θ, −β) and (θ, α) ↔ (−θ, α) should be equivalent up to the cluster chirality of s21:
- o+30_bp 1.1595 against o−30_bm 1.1524
- o+30_bm 0.8045 against o−30_bp 0.8114
- o+30_ap 1.0457 against o−30_ap 1.0521
- o+30_am 0.9322 against o−30_am 0.9242

The relation holds to ≤ 0.8 % absolute. The chiral linear-β response is physical (the field breaks the mirror) and is not an s21 artifact. The s21 chirality artifact is ≈ 0.7 %.
