**Screen-stage kernel truncation is not negligible for the outermost shells (0 evaluations).**

The screen, s21c, r46 and r56 stages build W_G(k,k') = F_G[ρ_* e^{γ*(k)+γ(k')}] in the box max(|m|,|n|) ≤ 8 (`g_cutoff 8`, `swarm reference`). Only conv46 uses 10. ρ_* reaches box 5 (shell 10, e.g. (5,4)), and an outer-shell γ also reaches box 5. The *first-order* kernel change ρ_*·γ therefore extends to box 10, and the part in boxes 9–10 is dropped at every rung except conv46.

I measured this with `physics.kdep_envelope.kernel_coefficients`, n_mesh 64, uncut versus cut at 8. The direction is the first chiral-odd β direction on S_s×T1 at 0.015 per entry, at three random (k,k') pairs. The table gives the rms fraction of dW = W − w0 outside box 8 (`work/cutoff_check.py`):

| shell | S1–S3 | S4 | S5 | S6 | S7 | S8 | S9 | S10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dropped fraction of dW | 0.000 | 0.002 | 0.002 | 0.012 | 0.023 | 0.017 | **0.071** | **0.092** |

Reading:
1. **Inner and S4–S5 directions are unaffected.** swarm-l's observation that conv46 = r46 to 5e-8 for the inner leaders is expected and holds there.
2. **For S9/S10 (|G|² = 19, 21) directions, 7–9 % of the first-order kernel change is cut at screen.** It does not disappear: it is a systematic of the screen, r46 and r56 numbers. Given how sensitive R is on this parent (swarm-h, swarm-a: outer steps are non-perturbative already at small amplitude), a few-% shift in R is plausible. The sign is unknown.
3. Consequences for the outer-shell maps (a, ap, ac, aq, as, an, w, v, h): treat S9/S10 slopes as approximate. Any candidate whose gain relies on S9/S10 content should be judged on conv46 (box 10, which captures first order fully), not on r46 alone. For such candidates, conv46 is *not* a duplicate of r46.

This is a numerical-scope note, not a claim about any request. Whether qmax 8 also limits the q+G reach of these components I have not checked.
