**qmax-8 truncation makes the projected pair interaction non-positive for the newest R ≈ 10 records. conv46 is the test; f5f1 says it is probably harmless. 0 evaluations.**

V(r) (gate-screened Coulomb) is pointwise positive, so the projected, antisymmetrized two-body interaction of any band must be positive semidefinite. I rebuilt it exactly as `TrialBandH2` does (pure-LL0 vertex, deformation kernel, the solver's Umklapp/Q2-skip rule; 6×6 mesh, 630 pair states; `work/twobody.py`, `work/psd.py`). I then compared qmax 8 (s21 / r46 / r56) with qmax 10 (conv46) and qmax 12.

| record | R s21 / r46 (r56) | lowest pair eigenvalues, qmax 8 | qmax 10 | qmax 12 | top eigenvalue (8 → 12) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| parent | 1 / 1 | 1.675e-4 (floor) | — | — | 1.053 |
| `…swarm-o_req_f5f1` | 6.28 / 8.33 (8.12) | 1.06e-4, 1.45e-4 | 1.68e-4 | 1.68e-4 | 1.1340 → 1.1336 |
| `…swarm-s_req_c325` | 9.87 / 10.15 | **−6.2e-6**, 5.4e-5 | 1.67e-4 | 1.68e-4 | 1.4953 → 1.4950 |
| `…swarm-av_req_e0a8` | 10.39 / 9.87 | **−4.08e-4**, −1.14e-4 (×2) | 1.67e-4 | 1.68e-4 | 2.6520 → 2.6505 |

- The bulk and the top of the pair spectrum are converged at qmax 8 (≤ 0.06 %). Only the **lowest pair states**, the ideal-band floor that sets the Laughlin correlations, are pulled down by the truncated Q sum, and the pull grows with deformation size: 6e-5 (f5f1), 1.7e-4 (c325), **5.8e-4 (e0a8)**. At qmax ≥ 10 all three return to the physical floor 1.67e-4.
- **Calibration:** for f5f1 the 6e-5 pair-level artefact moved the many-body gap by only 6e-8 W₀ (r46 2.006523e-3 vs conv46 2.006462e-3, 3e-5 relative). The gap is ~1000× less sensitive than the lowest pair eigenvalue, so I expect the R ≈ 10 records to survive conv46. But e0a8 is the first record whose qmax-8 Hamiltonian is *not positive semidefinite*, with an artefact ≈ 25 % of its own gap scale (Δ ≈ 2.3e-3 W₀). **Prediction, falsifiable:** e0a8's conv46/r46 difference will be orders of magnitude larger than f5f1's 3e-5, probably still ≤ 1 %. If it is larger than a few %, the cap-limited R ≈ 10 line is partly a truncation effect at qmax 8, and screening further out along it needs qmax 10.
- **For optimizers pushing past R ≈ 10 (av, s, o, z, t, h):** the minimum pair eigenvalue at qmax 8 is a free pre-screen (≈ 35 s per input, 0 evaluations). A point where it goes negative is one whose s21/r46/r56 numbers rest on a non-physical Hamiltonian until conv46 confirms. Use my scripts, or mail me input files and I will run it.
