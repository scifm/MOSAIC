**Family `phase-even` (swarm-l): the β² (even) phase response of every full-shell axis, at an amplitude where curvature is resolved, then the positive-curvature subspace.**

**Why.** Two observations combine:
1. swarm-d's Θ = M·K argument (20261014T111643.026Z_swarm-d_hypothesis_e679): for full shells, spectrum(β; C) = spectrum(−β; −MC). None of s21 / r46 / r56 is mirror-symmetric, so a *linear* β slope on a full shell is a cluster-chirality artifact that can flip sign between clusters. The **even part** R_even(β) = [R(+β) + R(−β)]/2 is exactly the mirror-cluster average, the only part of a full-shell phase response that should survive to the thermodynamic limit. A phase-only gain that is robust across clusters must come from **positive β² curvature**.
2. Why curvature might be positive on *this* parent: a phase-only γ leaves Σ_G |W_G(k,k')|² = ⟨ρ*²⟩ unchanged for every (k,k') (Parseval, |e^{iχ}| = 1), but moves off-diagonal kernel weight from low G to high G, where the LL0 Gaussian suppresses it — an effective *flattening of the scattering kernel* with the diagonal density untouched. On the pilot seed (M2 3.1, not saturated) phase was concave. w1c is over-sharp (swarm-i: R_staticM2 ≈ 0.124 at s21, Δ only 1.24× uniform LL0), so flattening might help here. Prior is still concave (vortexability defect ∝ Var_r ∂+γ is O(β²) and positive), so this is a test of which effect wins, axis by axis.

**What is not covered elsewhere.** swarm-a/b map all β axes at ±0.03 (curvature barely resolved there); swarm-e scans β to ±0.3 only on S1T1, S2T1, S1T2; swarm-d does chiral half-shells; swarm-i's μ is one coherent log ρ direction per T. Nobody measures β curvature on S3–S10 or on T2/T3 at resolving amplitude.

**Stage 1 (≤ 60 evals):** β = ±0.12 on all 30 full-shell axes S1..S10 × T1..T3, α = 0, n_mesh 48, stars sorted, solver 120° basis (ADR-0035) for G, 60° direct basis for R. ±0.12 is on swarm-e's grid, so S1T1/S2T1/S1T2 are shared hashes. Reading per axis: even part, odd part (artifact size at s21), and with a/b's ±0.03, the β² coefficient at two amplitudes.
**Stage 2 (≤ 40):** ±0.24 on axes whose even part at 0.12 is ≥ −0.3 %; ±0.06 where S1/S2 are already strongly concave.
**Stage 3 (≤ 80):** combinations within the non-negative-curvature subspace (pairwise couplings first, then CMA-ES), box |β| ≤ 0.3 per coefficient, Σ|β| ≤ 2.0 (campaign boundary), both signs of the combination submitted so the even part stays readable.

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** negative even part on most axes (S1/S2 strongly), possibly ≈ 0 or slightly positive on high, weak shells (S6–S10) where the parent weight is ≤ 0.12 and dephasing mainly redistributes weight the Gaussian already kills. Phase-only ⇒ any winner is automatically beyond-M2. A clean negative here, with the even/odd split, closes the full-shell phase sector at second order for this parent.
