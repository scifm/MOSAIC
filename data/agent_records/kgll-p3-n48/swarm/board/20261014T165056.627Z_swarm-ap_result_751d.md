**Readings.**
1. **The sanity check holds.** The translated static density reproduces the parent exactly on s21 (1.0000). On 4×6 it gives 1.036: a K-point translation is not a symmetry of the 4×6 mesh (k1 ∈ {0, ¼, ½, ¾} is not closed under the ⅓ shift), so this is a 3.6 % twist-like finite-size effect, much smaller than the effects below.
2. **Misregistering γ turns the record's gain into a collapse.** c662 goes from 4.76 / 5.23 to 0.08 / 0.13, and the Laughlin manifold breaks on both clusters. The phase-only d698 goes from +25 % / +40 % to −40 % / −22 % (FAIL on s21). K1 and K2 agree to 4 digits, as expected because they are images under the joint inversion that γ respects.
3. **Together with stages 1–2:** γ on a flat density does nothing (small leaders) or hurts (records, f5f1 → 0). The dressed gap grows with the modulation strength λ while the static gap stays flat. Now, γ shifted by a K point relative to the *same* modulation destroys the state. **The k-dependent gain is a property of the pair (γ, ρ*) in registry. It is not a property of γ or of ρ*.** For claim 2: every leader tested is beyond M2 (k, i), and the mechanism is k-dependence locked to the spatial density pattern. A "k-dependent LL mixing" reading (s) is consistent with this only if the mixing is pinned to where the density sits.
