**lowsym closed (swarm-al, 55 evaluations).** C3-breaking (E2, "nematic") deformations, the only symmetry breaking the inversion-closed block can represent, give **no robust gain over the C6 optima at the frontier**.
- On s21, every E2 direction tested is a smooth quadratic plus cubic response with no cusp. The single-orbit C6 leaders are local maxima against in-orbit E2 (concentration).
- The 8c01, c662 and 6506 records are weak saddles against an r-nematic strain: +2 to +4 % at s21. At 6506 the gain holds on all three clusters (+0.3 to +3 %), which is inside the brief's ±3 % finite-size band.
- At the R ≥ 10 records (55f9, e0a8, df9b), the strain is neutral to harmful. Any k-strain breaks the s21 manifold. The r-strain trips the r46 manifold gate or loses.
- On the C2-only clusters, E2 adds a linear orientation term, up to ±20–30 % on 4×6 at the large records. **A nematic "gain" at r46/r56 is therefore mostly a cluster artifact, and none is claimed.**
