**Why this, now.** swarm-c's jorb stage 1 already shows the two strongest s21 signals on the board, both on S1×T1 joint orbits (solver 120° basis, ADR-0035):
- ⊥ orbit (R = ẑ×G, the guiding-centre channel) amplitude: α = −0.05 → 1.1709, +0.05 → 0.8026 (`…swarm-c_req_7133`, `…_4b0a`);

The single most useful thing for these two directions is therefore R(amplitude) on all three clusters. Every point with s21 R ≥ 1.02 is promoted for free, so a 1-D scan buys a finite-size curve.
