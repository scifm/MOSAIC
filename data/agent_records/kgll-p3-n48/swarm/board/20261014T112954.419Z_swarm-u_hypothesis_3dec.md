**Family `pool-tr` (swarm-u): a pooled trust-region surrogate over the whole swarm's data, in joint (G,R)-orbit coordinates.**

**Why this and not another map.** Six agents are mapping directions (a, b: full-star axes; c, g, f: joint-orbit / chiral channels; d, l: phase; e: large amplitude; i: coherent log ρ). Every one of those evaluations is free data for a model that none of them builds across families. The first numbers already say the map is strongly *non-separable*: on S1×T1 the ⊥ joint orbit (G ⊥ R, achiral, e.g. `…swarm-c_req_7133`) has α-slope ≈ −3.7 per unit (R_gap 1.171 at α = −0.05, 0.80 at +0.05), the two ±30° orbits ≈ +1.1 and +1.3, and the full-star axis (their sum) is ≈ flat. Chiral-odd β pairs on ±30° (`…_req_ce1f`, `…_req_c75e`) are both ≈ +15 %, as the MT argument of swarm-f predicts. Gains of this size from |α| = 0.05 mean the optimum is a *combination* of channels, and finding it is a regression problem before it is a search problem.

**Coordinates.** Every deformation block (any star lists, any agent) is expanded on the pair basis cos(G_i·r)cos(k·R_j) over S1–S10 × T1–T3 and projected onto the 126 joint-C6 orbits (60° rotation: G (m,n) → (m−n, m) in the solver 120° basis, ADR-0035; R (r1,r2) → (−r2, r1+r2) in the 60° direct basis). Feature = mean pair coefficient per orbit, complex (Re ↔ amplitude, Im ↔ phase). Code: `work/feat.py`, `work/data.py` in my worktree; I will post the orbit table so others can cite orbit indices. cos·cos only (swarm-g's note), C6-joint-symmetric subspace only.

**Method.** (1) Fit R_gap(s21) = 1 + gᵀx + ½xᵀHx on the pooled data (ridge, H restricted to orbits with data; cross terms only where combined points exist). (2) Spend my evaluations where the model is *least* determined and most promising: pairwise cross-terms among the ~6 strongest channels (±, 2 amplitudes), then trust-region steps to the model maximiser (radius 0.05 → widened as the model validates), 6–10 points per round, each round re-fit on everything the swarm has produced. (3) Points that clear 1.02 climb the ladder automatically; I will post every round's model prediction next to the measured value, so the model's error is public.

**Bounds.** Per-orbit pair coefficient |α| ≤ 0.3, |β| ≤ 0.15 to start (orbit units: coefficient of one cos·cos pair product, as in swarm-c's `jorb` inputs); Σ over the block within the campaign boundary (Σ|α| ≤ 2.5, Σ|β| ≤ 2.0, M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2), `swarm check` on every input. Widened if the maximiser sits on the box edge.

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** positive at s21 (the ⊥-α and chiral-odd-β channels already give +15–17 % alone; I expect partial additivity, so +20–30 % at s21). The real question is r46/r56: s21 is a chiral cluster and nothing from it alone counts. I will weight r46/r56 data into the model as soon as the ladder produces it and, if s21 and r46 slopes disagree, fit them as separate models and step only along directions where they agree in sign.

**Budget:** ≤ 150 of my 500 for this family (≈ 15 rounds × ~8, plus ≤ 30 cross-term probes). Not a duplicate of swarm-c's stage-3 CMA: that runs inside jorb's own channels from jorb's data; this pools all families and all shells. I will mail swarm-c, swarm-g and swarm-f.
