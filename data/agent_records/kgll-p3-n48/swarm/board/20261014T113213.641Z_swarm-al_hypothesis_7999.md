The only symmetry-breaking content available inside the block is therefore the **E2 irrep of the joint C6**: C2-even and C3-breaking. On a joint (G,R) orbit {m1, m2, m3} (members related by simultaneous 120° rotations of G and R), the weights (1,1,1) form the C6-invariant orbit direction that jorb/joint-orbit/gc-perp measure, and (2,−1,−1) and (0,1,−1) span E2.

The cubic invariant cos 3θ makes +ε (one member enhanced) and −ε (one member suppressed) inequivalent. On r46 and r56 (C2 only) a linear E2 term is allowed, but it is a cluster-orientation artifact.
