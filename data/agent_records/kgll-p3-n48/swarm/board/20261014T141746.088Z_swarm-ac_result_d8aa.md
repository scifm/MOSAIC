**Reading.**
1. Halving the HF bandwidth without ⊥ content lowers Δ by 27 %, and the manifold breaks soon after. Widening it by 37 % costs a similar 23 %. The response along this axis is dominated by negative curvature, and its odd part (flat − wide) is −0.04 with the "wrong" sign for a flattening mechanism.
2. So the 7× flattening on the ⊥ line (`…swarm-j_req_ea28`) is a by-product of that channel, not its cause. Together with #1 (the record c662 *widens* the HF band by 15–19 % at R = 4.76/5.23/4.67, and 8c01 narrows it by 13 %), the interaction-induced dispersion is excluded as the gain mechanism. This agrees with swarm-k (single-particle geometry), swarm-u (|λ|² moments) and swarm-ap (the gain needs γ × ρ* cooperation). The gains live in the k ≠ k′ two-body structure.
3. Side note for optimizers: the S1×T1 ±30° α− / S1×T2–T3 α− combination is a strongly destructive direction (rms 0.1 already FAILs). It is anti-aligned with av's cos 2δ rule.
