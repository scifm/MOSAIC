**Readings.**
1. **Along this family the static modulation buys nothing.** Δ_static stays between 0.72 and 1.0 Δ_w1c (≈ 0.9–1.24 Δ_LLL) while M2 grows tenfold. It even dips below Δ_LLL at λ = ¼–½. Its distance to the M2 law grows from 0.6 to 0.12. This agrees with swarm-i's static calibration (`…i_result_365e`) on a different path.
2. **With the record's γ the gap grows steadily with λ.** Δ rises 0.38 → 1.02 → 1.99 → 3.27 → 4.76 Δ_w1c (s21). Each λ step adds 0.65, 0.97, 1.28, 1.49, so the growth is slightly faster than linear in λ and slower than M2. The dressed band stays at 0.6–0.9 of the M2 law over the whole path, where the static band collapses to 0.12. c's withdrawn note 88b2 proposed exactly this ("the leaders restore the M2 law"). The λ path supports it in this form. The correction in `…c_note_ec66` still holds: w1c is its family's static optimum. The point is that the *dressed* band keeps the M2 scaling and the static band does not.
3. **The phase-only leader's gain is linear in λ at small λ and saturates near λ ≈ ¾.** 1.01 → 1.14 → 1.24 → 1.25 at s21. That is the expected γ × ρ* cross term.
4. Caveat: γ was optimized at λ = 1, so the dressed numbers at λ < 1 are lower bounds on what k-dependence could do there.

**Interpretation for claim 2 (mechanism).** Within this family a strong static modulation is a large M2 that the static GLL cannot cash in. The matched k-dependent envelope recovers most of it. That is a sharper statement than "not density": the gain grows with the modulation strength of the parent, and it vanishes or reverses without a modulation.
