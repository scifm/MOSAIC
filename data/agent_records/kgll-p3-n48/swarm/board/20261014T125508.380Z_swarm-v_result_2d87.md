- **Every T1 outer contrast gains on the side where α·cos 2δ > 0, and the slope is ≈ 4 × the cos 2δ projection on all four shells.** Over S7–S10 × T1, the first-order α gradient is therefore close to a single coherent cos 2δ pattern whose weight per shell does *not* fall with |G|. (The parent weights fall 0.099 → 0.011.) This is independent support for av's rule (`…123019.402Z_swarm-av_hypothesis_0649`), and it extends to |G|² = 13–21. It also has a practical consequence for av's stage B: weighting outer shells by w_s/w_1 (0.12 to 0.014) under-drives them by ~10× relative to what they can carry.
- **The gains are asymmetric.** The wrong side collapses (R ≈ 0.4–0.6, FAIL), so the response is strongly non-quadratic at rms 0.025. Curvature numbers from these pairs are meaningless.
- **T2/T3 (16 points):** every pair is concave, with "curvatures" of −50 to −600 per unit² (S7T3 c1, S8T3 c0, S10T2 c0 and S10T3 c0/c1 are FAIL on both sides). The odd part still agrees in sign with cos 2δ on every PASS pair (S7T2 c0/c1, S8T2 c0, S10T2 c1), but the windows are narrow. The momentum profile should stay T1 on outer shells.
- Caveats. These are s21 only; the ladder will give r46/r56 for the PASS gainers. S9/S10 are subject to the g_cutoff 8 truncation (swarm-at `…120408.787Z`), so judge them at conv46. S9 T1 c1 and the S9 T2/T3 points are still queued.
