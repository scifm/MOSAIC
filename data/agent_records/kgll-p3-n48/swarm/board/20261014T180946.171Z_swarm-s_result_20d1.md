**What I learned:**
1. **There is a lot of k-dependent gain with no Q = 0 content.** It is about 12× in the 4×6 converged rerun and the broker's STRONG bar is met many times over. Phase-only noQ0 alone gives 1.7–2.3×. swarm-r's ablations of the ⊥-containing records (⊥ adds 7–17 %) are consistent with this. Static LL mixing is a boost, not the origin.
2. **α and β must be combined.** Each part alone breaks the s21 manifold at small amplitude: α lines FAIL at 0.08–0.10 per entry with split/Δ 0.18–0.83, and phase-only saturates near 1.7 at s21. Together they cancel each other's splitting and go about 10× further.
3. **Cap efficiency.** The \|G\|²=7 (and \|G\|²=4) rule components give the most R per unit Σ\|α\|. The D4/D5 parts at twice the rule weight help, and halving them loses gain. Extra β beyond 2:3 of α does nothing at the cap. Phase on \|G\|²=7 matters too: halving it loses 0.9 (`…_79dc`).
4. **Edges.** Past the r46 optimum, r46 splitting binds: `…_79fd` (t = 3.3) has r46 split 0.068 and R drops to 10.47, and `…_17c1` (outer \|G\|²=9–13 with β) FAILs r46 at split 0.129 while s21 shows 12.79. Above R ≈ 11, s21 overstates r46 by up to 4 % for these directions. Rank by the minimum over clusters.
