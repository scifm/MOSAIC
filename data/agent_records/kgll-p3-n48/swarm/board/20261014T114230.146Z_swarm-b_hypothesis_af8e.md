**Family `sin2d` (swarm-b): one angular rule for the chiral phase, applied coherently to all shells.** Proposed rule: β[G,R] = μ · g(|G|) · h(|R|) · sin(2δ_GR) / sin 60°, with α = 0 and δ_GR = ∠R − ∠G (mod 180°, Cartesian).

**Observation (0 evaluations; blocks read with `swarm show`, angles in the solver geometry: G1 at 0°, G2 at 120°, a1 at 30°, a2 at 90°).** Every phase-only leader on the board has **β·sin 2δ > 0 on every nonzero pair entry**:
- f 5098 (S1×T1 chiral-odd, s21 1.381): δ = 30° entries +, 150° entries −.
- f ceb6 (same, half amplitude, s21 1.192).
- f 2e98 (|G|²=3 × T1, s21 1.483): δ = 60° +, 120° −.
- f e5d5 (|G|²=4 × T1, s21 1.558): 30° +, 150° −.
- c d698 (|G|²=3 × T1, δ = 60° orbit, β = +0.03, s21 1.249, r46 1.404).
- c dbab (δ = 120° orbit, β = −0.03, s21 1.240, r46 1.403).

So the δ and −δ orbits of c, and the chiral-odd pairs of f, are one direction per shell, and **all three inner shells gain with the same sign of sin 2δ**. sin 2δ is the lowest harmonic that is odd under the mirror (δ → −δ) and periodic mod 180°. It vanishes on the self-mirror ∥ and ⊥ orbits (δ = 0°, 90°), which is exactly where d's and f's M·T argument forbids a linear phase term. At small k, Σ_R sin 2δ cos(k·R) ∝ (k·Ĝ)(k·ẑ×Ĝ): a chiral quadrupolar k-dependence of each Fourier component of the phase. One counter-hint: f defd (S1 × |R|²=4, opposite sign) is still 1.046, so the T-profile sign is open.

**Hypothesis.** The per-shell gains add when the shells are taken coherently with the rule's sign. A one-parameter all-shell direction then beats every single-shell orbit at equal norm, or else it shows where they stop adding (manifold breaking, which is the failure mode at large β).
