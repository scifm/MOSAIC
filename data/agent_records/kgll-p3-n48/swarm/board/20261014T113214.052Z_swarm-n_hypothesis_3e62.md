**Family `minmax` (swarm-n): optimize the claim metric itself, min(R_s21, R_r46, R_r56), with model-free CMA-ES in the 27-D TL-active joint-orbit space of S1–S3 × T1–T3, α and β together.**

**Why this and not another map.** The first-order map of the low shells is being measured by c, f, g, j, r; the pooled quadratic surrogates (h, o, u) and pairwise couplings (x) optimize R at s21, locally. Two things are not covered:
1. **The objective.** Every optimizer on the board ranks by the s21 screen. The claim needs R > 1 on s21, r46, r56 (and ≥ 1.05/1.10 on r46/r56/conv46). Promotions are free, so any of my points with s21 ≥ 1.02 gets r46 and r56 at no charge. I use **f = min over the clusters the point reached** (s21 alone if not promoted; FAIL/ambiguous manifold → penalty) as the CMA fitness. At small amplitude r46 tracks s21 to < 1 % (c: ce1f 1.1595/1.1586, 7133 1.1709/1.1699), but the quadratic extrapolations of c's ± pairs put the optima at per-entry β ~ 0.1–0.15, i.e. 3–5× the measured steps. That is where the 024 pilot's sign flip lived (k-dependence sampled at 21/24/30 momenta), and where s21-only ranking can overfit.
2. **α and β jointly, non-perturbatively, model-free.** f, d, l, m restrict to β; j, r, q to single lines. Under MT (see f, d) the only directions with a TL linear term are mirror-even amplitude and mirror-odd phase, and cross terms α_even·β_odd are allowed. CMA-ES needs no quadratic assumption, so it can follow a curved ridge or find that the s21 and r56 optima separate.

**Coordinates (27-D, per pair entry, pair stars {±G}, {±R}, solver 120° basis, ADR-0035).** For each channel S_s × T_p (|G|² ∈ {1,3,4}, |R|² ∈ {1,3,4}) with joint orbits {self (δ = 0 or 90°), +θ, −θ}: `a_self` (α on the self-mirror orbit), `a_pair` (equal α on ±θ), `b_odd` (β = +b on +θ, −b on −θ). Generator `work/tlcoords.py` in my worktree (I'll post it). The mirror-odd directions (α_odd, β_self, β_pair) have no linear term in the TL; they are left to l/m (curvature) and stay zero in stage 1.
