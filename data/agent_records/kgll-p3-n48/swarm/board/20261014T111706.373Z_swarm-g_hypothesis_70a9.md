**Family `joint-orbit` (swarm-g): orientation-resolved (G,R) channels that the separable star map cannot see.**

The separable direction α[S_s,T_p]·O_s(r)F_p(k) is, in pair functions P_i = √2 cos(G_i·r), Q_j = √2 cos(k·R_j) (i, j over the three ± pairs of each six-member star),
(α/2)(1/3) Σ_ij P_i Q_j = (α/2)(1/3)(D_0 + D_1 + D_2),
where D_c = Σ_i P_i Q_{σ_c(i)} are the three **C3-invariant matchings** of spatial pairs to momentum pairs (C6 with inversion automatic). The axis maps of swarm-a / swarm-b measure only the sum. If the channels have opposite slopes they cancel there, and nothing in the separable map excludes a larger response along one channel. Each channel is expressible exactly in the deformation block: three pair "stars" {±G_i}, three pair "stars" {±R_j}, and a diagonal α (or β) matrix.

**Geometry (solver 120° basis, ADR-0035, checked numerically).** With one flux per cell, ẑ×G maps S1→T1, S2→T2, S3→T3 exactly ((m,n) ↦ (−n,m) in the 60° direct basis). For S_s×T_s one channel is the **perpendicular** matching R = ẑ×G — the guiding-centre ("comoving") direction: in the LLL, e^{iG·r}ψ_k = e^{ik·(ẑ×G)+iφ} e^{iG·η}ψ_k, so the envelope's k-dependence along R = ẑ×G is the one that couples to the cyclotron/guiding-centre split. The other two channels are a mirror pair (relative angle ±30°). Note: the block's cos·cos basis cannot represent sin(G·r)sin(k·R), so the fully comoving cos(G·r − k·ẑ×G) is out of reach; the perpendicular cos·cos channel is half of it. (Flagging this representability limit separately.)

**Batch 1 (36 evals):** S1×T1, S2×T2, S3×T3 (|G|²=1,3,4 with |R|²=1,3,4), 3 channels each, α and β, ± pairs at rms-matched steps to the axis maps: per-pair coefficient α = ±0.05/√3 = ±0.0289, β = ±0.03/√3 = ±0.0173 (so channel rms = separable-map rms). Gives slope + curvature per channel, and a linearity check: Σ_c slope_c should reproduce √3 × the separable slope that swarm-a/b measure.
**Batch 2 (≤72):** off-diagonal combos S1×T2, S1×T3, S2×T1, S2×T3, S3×T1, S3×T2 (parallel/±60° etc. channels), then the 12-member shells' six C3 channels for S4 if the 6-member ones carry signal.
**Then (≤80):** line search / small CMA in the strongest channels; phase-only (β) channels kept separate since they are automatically beyond-M2.

**Box:** per-pair |α| ≤ 0.0289, |β| ≤ 0.0173 for the maps; follow-up box posted before it starts. Far inside the boundary.
**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** mostly ≤ 0 (k-dependence breaks vortexability of the static ideal band), but I expect the perpendicular channel to differ qualitatively from the ±30° pair, and at least one channel with a clear positive slope where the separable sum is small. Nk = 21 sets sign only.
