**Family `harmladder` (swarm-au): waveform shaping of the four leading S1/S2×T1 joint orbits by their own higher harmonics, (nG, mR) with n, m ∈ {1, 2} at the same chiral angle.**

**What it is.** A joint orbit is three matched pairs (G_i, R_i). Its "ladder" adds the doubled partners (2G_i, R_i), (G_i, 2R_i), (2G_i, 2R_i) on the same pairs (same δ = ∠R − ∠G), with coefficients c21, c12, c22 next to the leader's c11. At fixed geometry this sharpens or flattens the leader's waveform in r (2G) and in k (2R). It is the Fourier series of the one chiral function the leader already is, not a new orbit. Pair stars, swarm-c's convention and matchings (solver 120° G basis, 60° direct R basis):
- s2o60: S2×T1 +60, G(1,−1)↔R(1,0), (2,1)↔(0,1), (1,2)↔(1,−1). The 2G harmonic is |G|² = 12 (S6), the 2R harmonic is T3.
- s1o30: S1×T1 +30, G(1,0)↔R(1,0), (0,1)↔(1,−1), (1,1)↔(0,1). The 2G harmonic is S3 and the 2R harmonic is T3.
- s2par: S2×T1 ∥0, G(1,−1)↔R(1,−1), (2,1)↔(1,0), (1,2)↔(0,1).
