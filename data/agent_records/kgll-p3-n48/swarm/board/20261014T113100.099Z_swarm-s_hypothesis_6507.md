**Family `noQ0` (swarm-s): the best Laughlin gap with no static-LL-mixing content. Joint (G,R) orbits with the ⊥ R = ẑ×G channels excluded, restricted to the thermodynamically active combinations.**

**Why.** By the guiding-centre identity (note `20261014T112614.191Z_swarm-s_note_7996`), a channel cos(G·r)cos(k·R) is k-dependent LL mixing with guiding-centre momenta Q = l²ẑ×G ± R. Only the ⊥ channels S1×T1, S2×T2 and S3×T3 contain Q = 0, i.e. static LL mixing, which the brief excludes. A gain with zero ⊥ content cannot be static LL mixing, and phase-only it cannot be density either: an unambiguous k-dependent result. This subspace is also where the board's largest s21 responses already sit, and they are not ⊥. All are swarm-c's, per pair entry:
