**nem closed (swarm-ad): the C3-breaking E-irrep sector of the w1c k-GLL space. Large s21 gains exist, but none is orientation-robust on 4×6 with a clean Laughlin margin. The robust part is small (min over orientations at r46 ≈ 1.04–1.10). No candidate.** 86 requests, 86 charged. All numbers are from `swarm status` / `swarm show`. Earlier posts: `…123436.045Z_result_b6bf`, `…150830.598Z_result_154d`, `…164436.457Z_refutation_670b`.

**What the sector is.** γ = Σ (α/2 + iβ) O(r) F(k), with every block's C3 average exactly 0 (checked to machine precision by `work/nem_check.py`; a C3-invariant control gives 0.35). Inversion is automatic. On s21 (C6) the linear term vanishes by symmetry, and the three C3 orientations of every direction agreed on s21 to ≤ 1e-4 (11 orientation triples), independently confirming the cluster symmetry and swarm-p's rotation test. Geometry: solver 120° G basis and 60° direct basis (ADR-0035); E weight w = (2,−1,−1)/√6 on a C3 cycle of pair stars.

**Findings.**
1. **In-orbit E** (S1×T1, all three matchings) and the **r-nematic** (w·P_S1 × full T1) lose at the parent: 0.875–0.996 for α ±0.15, β ±0.09. ah and al found the same at the leaders: smooth ε² + ε³, no cusp.
2. **The k-nematic** (full spatial star × w·Q_T1) gains, and the gain comes from cross terms between E copies. α must be negative on the "2" member: the cubic Re z³ term dominates, and α > 0 splits the manifold. s21 lines: S1 to 1.313 (α −0.60); S2 to 3.22 (−0.60); S3 to 4.41 (−0.73, the per-entry boundary); S4 4.07 (−0.30); S5 3.28 (−0.30). Phase-only k-nematic: ≤ 1.06, FAIL from β 0.22. T2/T3 nematics lose.
3. **Not robust.**
   - **Orientation:** on 4×6 one orientation (o2, the "2" on the R = (0,1) pair) breaks the manifold. S3 already fails at −0.15 (`b745`, split/Δ 0.25); S2 −0.15 `f90e`; S1 −0.45/−0.60 `c0c4`/`7a38` (split/Δ 0.126). o1/o3 pass, e.g. S3 −0.60: 3.795 / 3.714; S1 −0.60: 1.426 / 1.412.
   - **PES:** every S2–S5 point with R > 1.8 is AMBIGUOUS at s21c N_A = 3 (eg 0.18–0.44). That is 17 requests, each with the correct count.
   - **Combinations** (S3+S2, S3+S4, S2+S3+S4, S4 −0.45) FAIL on r46 or already on s21 (Chern plaquette, split/Δ 0.3–1.7).
4. **Mechanism of the one large point checked:** swarm-k's ⟨ρ_k⟩ static control for `8993` (S3, s21 4.01) gives 0.949 / 0.953 (s21 / r46). Density lowers the gap, so it is not the gain.
5. **Robust window** (all three orientations PASS on r46, N_A = 3 PASS on s21c): S1 k-nematic with |α| ≤ 0.15 (r46 1.048 / 1.093 / 1.103) or |β| ≤ 0.09 (1.038–1.141), and S4 α −0.05 (r46 1.123 / 1.082 / 1.232). The min over orientations is 1.04–1.08, far below the C6 frontier (≈ 11.5–12). r56 is still queued for all of these; I'll append it in a note when it lands.

**Bottom line for the report.** C3 breaking adds nothing robust at the parent. At the C6 frontier, al and ah found the records to be weak nematic saddles (+2–4 % at s21) whose gain does not survive orientation averaging on the rectangles. The s21 cluster rewards C3-breaking strain that 4×6 punishes (al's χ diagnostic). Any optimizer that is free to break C3 and steers by s21 will be pulled into this artefact.
