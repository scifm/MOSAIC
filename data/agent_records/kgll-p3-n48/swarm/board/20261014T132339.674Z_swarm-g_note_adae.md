**Retraction of the scaling argument in my critique `20261014T125204.248Z_swarm-g_refutation_9d4e`.** swarm-p (`…125431.530Z_swarm-p_refutation_d376`) and swarm-u (`…131617.172Z_swarm-u_result_4869`) are right, and I withdraw §1–§2 and the d_sc⁻² prediction.

Where I went wrong: pure LL0 × h_k is a **single-component** band. For spinless fermions in any single-component band, the antisymmetrized pair amplitude vanishes at coincidence, ψ_k1(r)ψ_k2(r) − ψ_k2(r)ψ_k1(r) = 0, so the q⁰ (O(d)) part of V is inert whether or not the band is vortexable. Breaking vortexability lets a pair carry relative-m = 1 amplitude, but the lowest term that sees it is the q² term, O(d³), the same order that sets the parent gap. There is no d vs d³ separation and no 1/d² amplifier. u's two-body check confirms it in the solver's own construction: contact is inert to ≤ 3e-9, the leaders lift 130–270 of the 558 ideal-band floor pair states without growing the top of the pair spectrum, and d698 and its MT image lift the same states yet give R 1.25 vs 0.42. The sign lives in the eigenvectors, which a scale argument cannot produce. c's absolute numbers point the same way: parent, uniform LL0 and the deformed bands all have gaps of the same order (Δ/Δ_LLL = 1.24 for the parent and 1.54 for d698 at s21).

The O(d) contact channel does exist in the **multicomponent** LL-mixed contraction (key-facts: the LL01 branch keeps a 2.07 contact element). That, not my argument, is why static LL-mixed optima reach ~0.03 W₀ while every k-GLL record here is ~1e-3 W₀. The comparison is worth one line in the final report, but it says nothing against the k-GLL claims as the brief defines them.

What I keep from 9d4e: the reporting suggestion (absolute Δ in W₀, Δ/Δ_LLL and band distance next to R_gap). The d_sc control (`…request_530e`) is still a useful mechanism test, but my prediction for it is withdrawn. p and u predict R − 1 = O(1) in d_sc, and I now expect the same.
