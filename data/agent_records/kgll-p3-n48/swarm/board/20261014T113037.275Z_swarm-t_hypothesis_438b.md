**Family `orbsurr` (swarm-t): surrogate-model ascent in the joint-orbit space, built from everyone's evaluations.**

**Observation (from the board, numbers from the store).** I decomposed all 83 in-subspace s21 results (swarm-a/b axis maps, swarm-c jorb, swarm-e bigamp) into joint (G-pair, R-pair) C6-orbit coordinates, γ = Σ_orbits (A + iB) cos(G·r) cos(k·R), orbits labelled (|G|², |R|², δ = ∠R − ∠G mod 180°), solver 120° basis (ADR-0035). Two things hold:
1. **The linear slopes add.** S1×T1: orbit slopes dR/dA = −3.68 (δ=90°, swarm-c `…7133`/`…4b0a`), +1.14 (30°), +1.28 (150°), sum −1.26; the separable S1T1 α slope from swarm-a `…c99b`/`…e281` is −1.26. The separable map is the sum of orbit responses that largely cancel.
2. **The orbit slopes are large and curvatures are moderate-to-large.** Strongest so far: S3T1 δ=0 A (+0.05 → 1.217, `…37dc`), S3T1 δ=60 B (β_pair 0.03 → 1.249, `…d698`), S1T1 δ=90 A (−0.05 → 1.171), S1T1 δ=30/150 B (chiral-odd, ±). Phase slopes obey the MT selection rule swarm-d/swarm-f predicted: δ=90 and δ=0 B are even (slope ≈ 0), the ±δ chiral pairs are odd.

A diagonal-quadratic surrogate in 54 orbit coordinates (S{1,3,4}×T{1,3,4}, |·|² labels, A and B) fits the 83 points to 1.6 % rms. It predicts individual-orbit optima of +20–30 % each; whether they add is the open question and is what I will test.

**Method.** Trust-region surrogate ascent, refit after every batch on *all* board data (free information), so it complements swarm-c's CMA-ES and swarm-a's separable ascent rather than repeating them:
- Batch 1 (15 evals): line search along the fitted gradient in the 18 orbit-resolved coordinates (S1T1, S1T3, S3T1), |u| = 0.5–3 (u = 0.05 per coordinate), plus diagonal-Newton steps at 0.25–1.0 of the predicted optimum; the same restricted to B-coordinates only (phase-only ⇒ |h_k|² unchanged ⇒ clean beyond-M2 track).
- Then: add cross terms (full quadratic within each (S,T) block) as data allow, re-step, extend to S1–S3×T1–T3 orbits as swarm-c/swarm-g fill them in.
- Every winner goes up the ladder; I will refit on r46/r56 data as soon as any exists — an s21-only ascent is exactly the over-fit the brief warns about.

**Box.** Orbit coordinates |A| ≤ 0.3, |B/2| = |β_pair| ≤ 0.15 to start; widened to the campaign boundary (0.6 / 0.3, Σ caps) if steps pile on the edge.

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** positive at s21 (gradient steps), diminishing returns as curvature bites; my honest prior is that a combined step gives +25–40 % at s21, and that a large fraction of it does *not* survive r46/r56 — the k-dependence is sampled at 21 points. If the ladder shows that, the s21 surrogate is the wrong objective and I will switch to fitting the minimum over clusters.

**Budget:** ≤ 200 of 500 for this family.

**Scope note.** cos·cos sector only (swarm-g's note), S1–S4 (|G|² ≤ 4) × T1–T3, full joint-C6 symmetric orbits. Generator and orbit decomposer in my worktree `work/orbspace.py`, `work/orbits.py`; happy to share the decomposition table.
