**2. lllref, mechanism controls (26 `--control` requests, 52 evaluations on s21 + r46, never ranked).** The question was whether the leaders' gain is a standalone k-dependence effect or depends on w1c's modulation.
- **#1 / #1b** (`…131827.322Z_swarm-ap_result_eed1`, `…142010.163Z_swarm-ap_result_ca3b`): each leader's exact γ on **uniform LL0**. Small leaders (d698, e5d5, 37dc, d524, bfbb, da76) are inert to second order (R_lll 0.89–1.03). Records lower the gap: c662 0.47/0.73, 8c01 0.36/0.69, ea28 0.84/0.87. The record f5f1's γ destroys the Laughlin state (Δ → 0, FAIL).
- **#2** (`…145347.592Z_swarm-ap_result_bf67`): **ρ_λ = 1 + λ(ρ* − 1)**. The static gap stays flat at 0.72–1.0 Δ_w1c while M2 grows tenfold. With c662's γ the gap rises 0.38 → 1.02 → 1.99 → 3.27 → 4.76 Δ_w1c, keeping 0.6–0.9 of the static M2 law, where the static band falls to 0.12. The phase-only d698 gain is linear in λ and saturates near λ ≈ ¾.
- **#3 / #4** (`…165056.627Z_swarm-ap_result_751d`, `…172407.352Z_swarm-ap_result_f1ed`): **registration test**, with ρ* translated by a K point and γ unshifted (the static spectrum is unchanged). c662 goes 4.76/5.23 → 0.08/0.13 FAIL; d698 1.25/1.40 → 0.60 FAIL / 0.78; noQ0 c325 9.87/10.15 → 0.46/0.99 FAIL.
- **Conclusion for claim 2:** the k-dependent gain belongs to the pair (γ, ρ*) in registry, not to γ or ρ* alone. It holds for phase-only, ⊥-containing and noQ0 leaders. The k-dependent envelope recovers M2 that w1c's strong static modulation cannot use by itself, and on a flat or misregistered density it hurts. This complements the density controls (k, i), which exclude the diagonal density.
