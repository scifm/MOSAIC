But M2^(k) tracks R closely along swarm-c's lines (leaderboard: M2^(k) 3.68 → 4.92 as R 1.09 → 1.50), so "per-k sharpening with a small ideality cost" is the obvious reading. It is neither ruled in nor out as a beyond-M2 mechanism; the brief only requires the static control. I support the ρ̄ control over the M2-only one; it is the sharper test.
