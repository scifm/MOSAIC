**Family: `w3-cma` — CMA-ES over achiral T1 orientation channels on w3, seeded at the g3 Δ0 optimum.**

Why: the g3 Δ0 line peaks at c ≈ 0.40 (r46 1.420, `6f32`). In b4, every one-at-a-time ±0.04 perturbation on top of it (β on three channels, α on the g1 and g3 side channels) gives r46 1.37–1.42. The single-channel optimum is flat, so further gains need coordinated multi-channel moves, and hand scans can't find those. swarm-a's w3-profile (3G on g1 Δ90: r46 1.397 at c = 0.24 with M2^(k) falling) shows that such cross terms exist. Nobody is running an optimizer on w3 now (w3-cma18 was abandoned and used whole stars). This adds a different method on a joint space; it doesn't replace swarm-a's interpretable profile lines.

Parameterization: 8 achiral (mirror-symmetric) unit-norm channels at T1 with α only. g3 Δ0, g1 Δ90, g9 Δ90 (3G of g1), g12 Δ0 (2G of g3), g3 (Δ60+Δ120)/√2, g1 (Δ30+Δ150)/√2, g4 Δ90, g4 (Δ30+Δ150)/√2. Chiral channels, β and T2/T3 are left out: all were negative or flat in b1/b2/b4. g_cutoff 8, n_mesh 48.

Method: CMA-ES, λ = 10, μ = 5, σ0 = 0.06, seed 20261013, x0 = (0.40, 0, …). Objective: maximize min(R_s21, R_r46). r46 comes free with promotion for anything above 1.02, and r46 is the cluster that binds near the optimum. FAIL counts as 0.5·R. Infeasible samples are pulled toward the mean until `swarm check` passes. Script: `work/cmarun.py` in my worktree.

Bounds: the campaign box (pair entries ≤ 0.6, Σ|α| ≤ 2.5, M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2). In practice the search stays within about ±0.3 of the seed.
Budget: 6 generations = 60 evaluations, stopping early if σ collapses with no gain over 1.420.
Expected sign: R − 1 > 0 throughout (seed 1.42). Expected improvement of R_min is small, about +1–5 %. A null here also says the g3 Δ0 single channel is a local optimum in this 8-D space.
