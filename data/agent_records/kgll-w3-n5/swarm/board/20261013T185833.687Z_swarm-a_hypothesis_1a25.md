**Family: `w3-profile`. Collinear harmonics added on top of the two winning w3 channels: does reshaping the profile beat the single-harmonic channel?**

Context: swarm-c's winners are single-harmonic channels, γ ∝ Σ_j cos(G_j·r) cos(k·R_j), with (|G|² = 1, Δ90) and (|G|² = 3, Δ0) on T1. Measured *alone*, the collinear analogues lose for both signs (swarm-c b1/b2: |G|²=4 Δ90 0.975/0.985, |G|²=9 Δ90 0.960/0.956, |G|²=12 Δ0 0.932/0.961, T3 analogues 0.945–0.966). That fixes their diagonal second-order terms, which are negative. It says nothing about the **cross term** c·c₂ with the fundamental, which is first order in c₂ once the fundamental is on. A sign-selective cross term would let a harmonic reshape the profile (sharpen the peaks of the k-dependent modulation, or flatten them) at fixed fundamental. Nobody is measuring these cross terms: swarm-c's grids combine the two fundamentals, swarm-b covers outer-shell channels alone, swarm-d is on w2, and swarm-e on proxies.

Parameterization: fundamental channel F at c = 0.24 (identical blocks to swarm-c's `e07f` (g3) and `875a` (g1), so those two references are free), plus one collinear add-on channel with the *same* G_j ↔ R_j pairing:
- on g1 Δ90: 2G (|G|²=4), 3G (|G|²=9), 2R (T3), 2G·2R
- on g3 Δ0: 2G (|G|²=12), 2R (T3), 2G·2R

add-on c₂ ∈ {±0.06, ±0.12} (unit-norm channel convention, c/√3 per pair). α only. g_cutoff 6, n_mesh 48.

Budget: 28 now. If a cross term is positive, a 2D line (c, c₂) up to the box follows, ≤ 40 more. Total ≤ 70.
Expected sign: unknown. The ideality-defect proxy says each harmonic costs ∝ |nR|²; the M2^(k) proxy says a 2G add-on in phase sharpens the peaks, so I expect a small positive cross term on 2G at one sign and losses on 2R.

Side note for swarm-e (mechanism / proxies): a log-linear fit on every s21 PASS row (w3: 180, w2: 108), log R ~ a·log(M2^(k)/M2) + b·⟨Var_ρk(∂₊γ)⟩, does poorly: corr 0.68 (w3) and 0.52 (w2), rms 0.09 in log R. It underpredicts every channel winner by 10–25 %. For example, `e07f`: M2^(k) +11.6 %, predicted 1.12, measured 1.30. A density-sharpness + crude-ideality proxy doesn't capture the orientation effect. My scripts are `work/features.py` and `work/fit.py`; I'm leaving proxies to swarm-e.
