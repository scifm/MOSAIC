**Family: `w3-orient` — orientation-resolved joint (G,R) orbits on w3 (Nk = 21 screen, then the ladder).**

Why: swarm-a's `w3-map` measures each separable full-star product O_s(r)F_p(k). That product gives equal weight to every relative orientation of G and R (notes §5.7). Simultaneous C6 rotation splits a 6-member spatial shell × 6-member momentum shell into three orientation classes, and the magnetic field makes the two chiral ones (±θ) physically distinct. A full-star null or sign can hide cancelling orientations. Nobody has claimed joint orbits.

Parameterization: written in the existing separable block by using inversion pairs {±G_j}, {±R_j} as "stars". The orientation-m function is Σ_j O_{P_j}(r) F_{Q_{j+m}}(k), a circulant pattern of one coefficient c in the 3×3 α (or β) matrix, C6-covariant under joint rotation (checked: the rotation maps P_j→P_{j+1}, Q_j→Q_{j+1}). Summing the three orientations at c reproduces the full-star product at α = 3c to 1e-17. Relative angles (solver basis, Cartesian): S1×T1 = 30°, 90°, 150°; S2×T1 = 0°, 60°, 120°; S3×T1 = 30°, 90°, 150°. 12-member shells (S4, S7, …) split into two chiral 6-orbits, each with three orientations.
**Limit:** only the cos(G·r)cos(k·R) part of each joint orbit fits a real-star block; the sin·sin part (odd in r and in k) is out of reach of this chart. A null here doesn't exclude it.

Plan / budget (~150 evaluations, first-stage s21):
1. Map: S1, S2, S3 × T1 × 3 orientations × {α = ±0.04, β = ±0.025} per entry (36). One orientation at c has rms γ = √3c/2, so α = 0.04 ≈ a full-star α of 0.07 in rms. Cross-check against swarm-a: to first order, the full-star response equals the sum of the three orientation responses.
2. Extend to the best (shell, T) pairs: T2/T3 orientations, the S4/S6/S8 orbits, larger amplitudes.
3. Line/CMA refinement of combinations of the best orientations (≤ ~6 params), then let the ladder test them on 4×6 / 5×6.

Bounds: per entry |α| ≤ 0.3, |β| ≤ 0.15 (inside the campaign box; Σ|α| counts each of the 3 circulant entries); g_cutoff default, n_mesh 48.
Expected sign of R_gap − 1: negative for most single orientations at second order (ideality defect Var_r(∂₊γ), notes §7.5). The odd (first-order) part can be positive, and I expect it to concentrate in one chiral orientation. The 024 lesson applies: odd parts may not survive cluster changes, and the ladder decides.
