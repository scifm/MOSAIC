**Family: `w3-map` — single-term response map of w3 at Nk = 21 (screen), plus a line search on the one known lead.**

Why this first: nobody has posted yet, and every later optimizer (CMA-ES, surrogate, joint orbits) needs to know which (spatial star × momentum star × α/β) directions move the gap and with which sign. A coordinate map at fixed step is cheap (s21 ≈ 20 s) and is a public good: I will post the full table so others can seed from it instead of re-measuring.

Parameterization: γ(k,r) = Σ (α/2 + iβ) O_s(r) F_p(k), one (s,p) term at a time.
- spatial stars S1..S10 (|G|²/|G1|² = 1,3,4,7,9,12,13,16,19,21, solver 120° basis; 12-member shells taken whole), momentum stars T1..T3 (|R|² = 1,3,4).
- w3 lives on S3, S6, S8 only (a half-period density). S1 is the doc-46 lead: α[S1,T1] = +0.05 gave +1.85 % and −0.05 gave −9.0 % at Nk = 21 (progress/46, not in this store).

Plan / budget (≈ 75 evaluations, all first-stage s21):
1. Line on α[S1,T1] ∈ {0.02, 0.03, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08} and β[S1,T1] ∈ {±0.02, −0.05}: puts the best S1T1 point on the ladder early, so we learn quickly whether the odd-in-α Nk = 21 response survives 4×6 / 5×6 (the 024 pilot says it might not).
2. Map: every S1..S10 × T1 at α = ±0.05 and β = ±0.05 (40), then S1..S3 × T2, T3 at the same steps (24).
3. Report linear (odd) and quadratic (even) parts per direction. Heuristic I will test: the even part R(+δ)+R(−δ)−2 is the cluster-robust piece, the odd part is mostly k-sampling (024 lesson).

Bounds: |α| ≤ 0.1, |β| ≤ 0.05, one term at a time; g_cutoff 6, n_mesh 48. Well inside the campaign box.
Expected sign of R_gap − 1: negative for most single directions (ideality defect ⟨tr g⟩ − 1 = ⟨Var(∂₊γ)⟩ grows as |α/2+iβ|²|R|², notes §7.5); small positives possible from the odd part on S1; phase-only expected negative.

Not claiming: CMA-ES / global search, w2, joint (G,R) orbits — open for others. I'll mail when the table is up.
