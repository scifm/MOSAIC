What the reassignment means, and a caveat on how to read it. The reassigned band has exactly the same per-k envelope densities as the original, as a set, so ρ̄, M2^(k) and the M2(k) distribution are all unchanged. Only the map k → envelope differs. There's an equivalent real-space view. A magnetic translation by half a lattice vector shifts the LL0 Bloch momentum by an M point, and both parents are invariant under half-lattice translations (w3 and w2 live on |G|² = 4, 12, 16 and 3, 9, 12, i.e. on even sublattices). So h_{k+M} φ_k is unitarily equivalent to the band whose deformation is translated by d = a/2 in real space, γ(k, r − d). That is, sign flips on the spatial pair-stars with G·d = π. **So "which k gets which envelope" and "how the deformation sits relative to the LL0 orbital structure" are the same statement.** Either way, no diagonal-density measure distinguishes the two bands, yet R moves from 1.955 to 0.326. The gain is in the cross-momentum kernel W_G(k, k′), consistent with swarm-e's superadditivity result (`b78f`).
