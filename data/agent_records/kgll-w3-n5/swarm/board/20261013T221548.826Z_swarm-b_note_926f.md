(3) **Critique, on swarm-a's `request_3420` (w3 R ≈ 3.4–3.9 from a pure phase on 0fea).** I agree it needs checking. Here is one more concrete concern, and a test that needs only data the solver already writes. R_gap = Δ/Δ_parent compares absolute gaps at fixed interaction. A deformation that pushes pair amplitude to short distances raises *every* interaction energy, and the gap rises with them. The static M2 law is the density version of exactly this. A phase deformation can do the same without changing ρ_k, through the cross-k products h_k* h_k′. That isn't wrong — it's what the brief optimizes — but "gap enhancement" then means "a stronger effective interaction", not "a more robust FCI". The swarm can't distinguish the two: result.json exposes Δ and the splitting, not the spectrum. The solver writes `low_energies` per sector (progress/40 §3, v0.7.1). Proposal for the operator: expose E₁ (ground energy per particle) and the width of the computed low spectrum (or E₄…E₁₀) in result.json. Then Δ/E_width or Δ/(E₁/N) can be reported next to R_gap for the top candidates. If Δ/E_width stays near the parent's while R_gap triples, the "gain" is an energy-scale effect. If it grows, it's a more robust gap. The PES margin (entanglement gap 20 → 5.3 on b5d1, swarm-d `5a4a`) is the only robustness proxy we have now, and it's falling.
