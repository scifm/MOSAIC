**Family: `w3-joint` — joint (G,R) relative-orientation channels on w3 (notes §5.7), the directions the star-product map cannot see.**

Why: swarm-a's `w3-map` measures star products O_s(r)F_p(k), which force equal weight on every relative orientation between a spatial harmonic G and a momentum harmonic R. The joint-C6 family splits each (6-member S, 6-member T) product into **three channels** by the angle Δ between G and R (mod 180°). Computed from the solver's Cartesian vectors (120° reciprocal / 60° direct basis, ADR-0035):
- S1, S3 (|G|²=1,4) × T1 or T3: Δ ∈ {30°, 90°, 150°}; × T2: Δ ∈ {0°, 60°, 120°}
- S2 (|G|²=3) × T1, T3: Δ ∈ {0°, 60°, 120°}; × T2: {30°, 90°, 150°}
Each channel C_Δ = Σ_{(i,j)∈Δ} O_{±G_i}(r) F_{±R_j}(k)/√3 (unit mean square), built in the block as inversion-pair stars, coefficient c/√3 per pair entry. The star product is (C_Δ1 + C_Δ2 + C_Δ3)/√3, so my channel table should sum (linearly) to swarm-a's star numbers — a built-in cross-check of both tables.
Limit stated up front: the block admits only real (inversion-symmetric) star functions, so γ is cos(G·r)cos(k·R)-type; the sin·sin half of a true joint orbit cos(G·r + k·R) is not representable. Δ and 180°−Δ are mirror images (30↔150, 60↔120); their difference is a chiral (mirror-odd) direction, and the 21-site cluster is itself chiral, so a 30/150 asymmetry at Nk = 21 is a candidate cluster artefact — I will flag it rather than trust it.

Plan / budget (w3, all screen-stage s21, g_cutoff 6, n_mesh 48):
1. Map (36): S1, S2, S3 × T1 × 3 channels × {c_α = ±0.08, c_β = ±0.04}, one channel at a time.
2. Combine (~30): line searches along the best odd-part channel combination and the mirror-symmetric combos (30+150, 90).
3. Local optimizer (~80): CMA-ES (seeded, sigma 0.05) in the up-to-18-dim channel space (S1–S3 × T1, α and β), extended to T2/T3 only if T1 shows a lead.
Total ≈ 150 evaluations on w3; w2 afterwards if w3 is null.

Bounds: per-channel |c_α| ≤ 0.3, |c_β| ≤ 0.15 to start (pair entries ≤ 0.17 / 0.09); widened toward the campaign box if winners pile up at the edge. Note Σ|α| counts pair entries, so a channel costs √3·|c| of the 2.5 budget.

Expected sign of R_gap − 1: negative for most channels (ideality defect grows as |α/2+iβ|²|R|²; T1 is the least damaging), with small odd-part positives possible on S1. Phase-only expected negative but worth mapping per channel because it is the clean beyond-M2 route.
