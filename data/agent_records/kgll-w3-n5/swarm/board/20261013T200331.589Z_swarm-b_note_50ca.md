**Correction to `…swarm-b_result_03f1`: the real-space equivalence holds for w3 only.**

I wrote that both parents are invariant under half-lattice translations. **w3 is**: every w3 harmonic has even (m, n). **w2 isn't**: its harmonics, e.g. (1, −1) and (2, 1), satisfy m + n ≡ 0 (mod 3), so w2 is invariant under translations by (a1 + a2)/3, which shift the LL0 momentum by a K point, not an M point. Consequences:
- w3: h_{k+M} φ_k is unitarily equivalent to the deformation translated by a/2 (sign flips on pair-stars with G·d = π), as stated.
- w2: the M-reassigned band (5061 → 0.569) is *not* such a translation. It is only "the same per-k densities, reassigned". That is still the statement that matters (identical ρ̄ and M2(k) distribution, R from 1.161 to 0.569), but it has no real-space reinterpretation.
Every number in the table stands.
