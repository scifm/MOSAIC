# Hypothesis H1 (ctrl-b): single-term response map of w3, then a quadratic-model step

**Family:** `w3-single` — one (spatial star S_s, momentum star T_p) term at a time,
amplitude (α) and phase (β) separately, w3 parent.

**Rationale.** Doc 46 shows a strong, asymmetric response on Nk=21 for
α[S1,T1] = ±0.05 (+1.85 % / −9.04 %): a large linear term with strong negative
curvature, vertex near α ≈ +0.04. Before any optimizer I want the linear and
quadratic coefficients of every single direction in the brief's box, so that
(i) the coverage is explicit and (ii) a quadratic model can predict a combined
step. Gains are expected to be small per direction (concave response), so the
real question is whether several positive linear terms add.

**Directions.** Spatial stars by |G|²/|G1|²: S1, S3, S4, S7a, S7b, S9, S12,
S13a, S13b, S16, S19a, S19b, S21a, S21b (12-member shells split into their two
C6 orbits — a less symmetric, chiral choice). Momentum stars T1 (all 14
spatial), T2 and T3 (spatial S1, S3, S4, S7a, S7b, S9, S12, S16).

**Steps / box (phase 1).** α = ±0.04, β = ±0.03, one nonzero coefficient per
request. Kernel box g_cutoff = max(4, max|m,n| + 4), n_mesh = 48.

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1.** Mostly negative (concave); positive for one
sign of α on a few low stars (S1T1 known). Phase-only (β) expected ≤ 0 at
these steps, by analogy with the 024 pilot.

**Budget.** Phase 1 ≈ 120 evaluations. Phase 2 (quadratic model in the top
directions, Newton step plus a small CMA-ES/line search around it, bounded at
|α| ≤ 0.3, |β| ≤ 0.15 initially) ≈ 150. Phase 3: transfer of the best
directions to w2 ≈ 60. Reserve ≈ 170.
