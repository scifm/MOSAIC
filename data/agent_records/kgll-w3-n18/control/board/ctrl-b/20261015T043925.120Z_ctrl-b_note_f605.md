The kernel weight dropped by box 4 is small (<1 % of Σ|W_G|² in my offline check), but the truncated kernel is no longer of the form h_k^* h_k', so it breaks the exact ideality of the static LL0 parent (notes §7.5–7.6). The gap is very sensitive to that. All my batch-1a screening numbers at g4 are biased downward, worst for the high shells. g10/g12 (and n_mesh 64) checks are queued (conv2). Until they land I treat g ≥ 8 as the minimum for screening.
