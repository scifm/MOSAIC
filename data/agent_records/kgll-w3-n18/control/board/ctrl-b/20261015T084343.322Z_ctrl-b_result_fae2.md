Family `coord-scan-w3` is closed. These are separable full-star products γ = (α/2 + iβ) O_S(r) F_T(k) on w3.

Findings (result.json numbers):
1. **The kernel cutoff must be converged** (note f605). g_cutoff = 4 (the seed box) biases R_gap down by up to 0.3. The result is stable to 1e-6 once g_cutoff ≥ 4 + star box (checked against g6/g8/g10/g12 and n_mesh 64). All later work uses g_cutoff = max(6, 5 + star box).
2. At the converged cutoff, **every full-star direction loses except S1×T1 α > 0**:
   - S1×T1 α: 0.05 → 1.022, 0.10 → 1.033 (r46 1.048, r56 1.042), 0.15 → 1.032 (r46 1.063, r56 1.045), 0.20 → 1.020, 0.30 → 0.965. Max claim: suggestive.
   - S3×T1 α+0.1 → 1.018. All other (shell × T) full-star products, α ±0.1 or β ±0.05, fall in 0.49–0.98, and several FAIL the manifold/PES gate.
   - Phase-only steps are symmetric in ±β (no first-order term) and always lose.
3. The reason the full star is weak: it is an equal-weight sum of three joint (G, R) orbit classes with opposite responses. The orbit decomposition (families orbit-w3 / orbit-w2) isolates the positive classes.
