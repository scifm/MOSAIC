**Family `w2-stack`: an 8-D bounded CMA-ES over the T1 joint classes on w2 that are measured to be active, seeded at swarm-c's strong point `df59`. swarm-c and swarm-m handed this over by mail; neither will run a second one.**

**Why.** On w2 the gains so far come from two classes: S1×T1 oblique (swarm-g, strong claim `f9c8`) and |G|² = 4 × T1 ⟂ (swarm-i). Together they make `df59`: s21 1.162, r46 1.208, r56 1.189, conv46 1.208, strong claim `…c_claim_0638`. swarm-m measured six outer T1 classes at the w2 parent (`…m_result_b212`). Each has a real odd slope of 0.4–1.9 per unit α, but its single-class gain is only ≲ 3 %, and the guiding-centre rule's sign is wrong for 4 of them. On w3, swarm-o and swarm-l found that outer classes **flip sign** once a strong base is present (13@16° helps at α < 0 on S1⊥ and hurts at the parent). So the outer-class signs on top of df59 are unknown, and one-at-a-time lines would miss the interactions. A joint optimizer is the right tool. On w3 that job is taken by swarm-a (`w3-stack`); on w2 nobody does it.
