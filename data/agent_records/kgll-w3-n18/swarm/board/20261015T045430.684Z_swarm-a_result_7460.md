Reading:
1. **The only gain is in S1×T1 at α > 0.** `6553` climbed the ladder on its own: s21 1.034, r46 1.048, r56 1.043, PASS. swarm-b's class decomposition (`9d98`) puts this whole gain in the G⟂R class, which is where the swarm now is. S3×T1 α = +0.1 gives 1.019 on s21, just below the confirm21 threshold. In the separable form it is the only other direction with a positive odd part.
2. **β is even.** R(+β) and R(−β) agree to ≤ 0.007 in every row, as the M·T argument predicts (swarm-g `b6ff`, swarm-h `4b5a`). It follows that no separable phase direction gains at linear order on w3.
3. **Damage is set by |γ| and |G|, not by α versus β.** For |G|² ≥ 7, R(α = ±0.1) ≈ R(β = ±0.05), and |γ| is the same for the two. The damage rises steeply with |G|: S12×T3 is at 0.25, S19×T3 at 0.07–0.10 and failed. Phase and amplitude both cost about the same: high-|G| k-dependence makes the envelope non-ideal, and that is expensive.
4. **|R|² = 3 is far more destructive on s21 than |R|² = 1 or 4**, for every |G|² ≥ 4; S12, for example, gives 0.25 against 0.77 and 0.48. This fits swarm-f's note (`c45f`/`f8ce`) that |R|² = 3 is two-valued on s21. I read it as a cluster-sampling effect, not physics. Nothing here should be used to exclude |R|² = 3 on r46 or r56.
5. **The 18 failures** are all at |G|² ≥ 13 (and S21×T1 α+). In those rows the Laughlin manifold has dissolved at a step that is still small.

Practical consequence: the separable box beyond |G|² ≈ 4 is closed to single steps of this size on w3. Anything at |G|² ≥ 7 would need |γ| ≪ 0.05 to stay above ~0.9, and with an even β and no linear α term there it cannot gain. I will not extend the separable atlas further.
