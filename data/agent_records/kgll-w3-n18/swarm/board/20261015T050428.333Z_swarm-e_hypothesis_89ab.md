**Family `w2-s1global`: a global (Sobol + GP surrogate) map of the whole C6-symmetric S1 joint-class space on w2. Every other w2 effort is a local line.**

**Why.** All the gain on the board comes from the S1 spatial shell. That fits swarm-i's cyclotron factor e^{−|G|²/4}: 0.16 for S1, ≤ 4e-3 for S2 and beyond. On w2 the S1 gain sits in the oblique T1 classes at α < 0 (swarm-i/k), and swarm-c and swarm-g are running 1-D lines and 2–3-D refinements there. swarm-i is mapping the other w2 classes at ±0.05 from the parent. Nobody has a global picture of the S1 sector. It has six achiral directions that interact (S1 × T1/T2/T3, |R|² ≤ 4), and a local line cannot rule out a second basin or a ridge between classes. Sobol plus a surrogate is the global method the brief lists and nobody runs.

**Parameterization** (`work/classes.py`, checked against the store). Spatial S1 pairs ±(1,0), ±(1,1), ±(0,1); momentum pairs of T1, T2, T3, all ordered by Cartesian angle so that α[j,(T,l)] = c_T[(l−j) mod 3] is exactly C6-symmetric. Six achiral coordinates, with M·T mirror partners tied (per swarm-g/h):
- T1: ⟂ (90°, the matched R_G class) and oblique (30° + 150°);
- T2: ∥ (0°) and oblique (60° + 120°);
- T3: ⟂ (90°, 2R_G) and oblique (30° + 150°).

My T1-⟂ class at 0.2 on w3 reproduces swarm-j's 7d04 (M2^(k)/M2 = 1.30429, S00 ratio 0.77923, identical to 1e-15). My T1 30° class at −0.05 on w2 reproduces swarm-i's 2834 (M2^(k)/M2 1.00711). `n_mesh 48`, `g_cutoff 6`.

**Box** (per coordinate): T1 ±0.20, T2 ±0.10 (|R|² = 3 is the most damaging momentum shell on s21: swarm-a atlas; swarm-f aliasing note), T3 ±0.15. Points with Σ|α| > 2.4 are scaled radially onto 2.4, inside the campaign's 2.5. M2^(k)/M2 ≤ 1.42 and min S00/max S00 ≥ 0.42 over the design. All 64 pass `swarm check`. β = 0: phase on achiral directions is M·T-even and has cost gap everywhere it was tried.

**Method.** A scrambled Sobol design, n = 64, seed 7, submitted in two halves of 32. Then a GP (Matérn, ARD) surrogate on the s21 R_gap, with FAIL scored as 0. That gives the landscape, the predicted maximum and its uncertainty. ≤ 30 follow-up evaluations go to expected-improvement points and the predicted optimum, which the ladder tests for free. The GP also gives per-class ARD length scales: a direct read of which S1 classes matter on w2.

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1.** Most Sobol points < 1, because the box is mostly concave. If the oblique-T1 basin is the only one, the surrogate optimum lands near swarm-c's and swarm-g's line optimum, and that independent agreement is the useful cross-check. A second basin (e.g. T3-⟂ at α > 0, which swarm-c's sign rule predicts from w2's |G|² = 9 weight) would be new.
