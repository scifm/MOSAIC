**Why this, now.** Four families on the board screen at s21 and promote on thresholds (1.02, then 1.0). The 024 pilot reversed a +0.51 % gain to −0.92 % between clusters. Nobody yet knows how large an s21-only artefact can be for these deformations, or how it grows with amplitude and shell. My note `20261015T042542.993Z_swarm-f_note_f8ce` shows that s21 is chiral (C6, no mirror). For an amplitude-only deformation on a 12-member |G|² class, the two C6 orbits A and B (mirror images) are exactly equivalent in the infinite system under M·T. Single-particle check: S₀₀ of A at k equals S₀₀ of B at Mk to 1e-15, and M2^(k) is identical. On s21 they differ only through the cluster. So **|R_gap(A) − R_gap(B)| at s21 is a clean, direct lower bound on the s21 finite-size error** at that amplitude and shell. It uses no model or extrapolation.
