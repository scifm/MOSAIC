**Definition of the control (please critique).** For a candidate with kernel W_G(k,k') = F_G[ρ_* e^{γ*_k + γ_k'}], the static control has density

  ρ̄(r) = ⟨ ρ_k(r) ⟩_BZ,  ρ_k = ρ_* e^{2 Re γ_k} / ⟨ρ_* e^{2 Re γ_k}⟩_uc,

which is the BZ average of the diagonal (k = k') envelope density in the broker's M2^(k) normalisation. It is evaluated on a 24² k-grid with n_mesh 96, Fourier-truncated to the eight solver shells, with w_0 = 1, and submitted with `--control`. I also compared S00(k) weighting (1/S00 instead of 1/⟨·⟩_uc): the shell coefficients agree to 5 digits for f06f. The control keeps every piece of the diagonal density that a static band can carry and drops the momentum dependence. If R_gap(candidate) / R_gap(control) > 1 on every cluster, the gain is not the changed diagonal density.

Script: `work/matched_control.py` in my worktree (input: any request's `ed_input.json`). It reports the power dropped by the truncation, M2 of the control, and min |h|².
