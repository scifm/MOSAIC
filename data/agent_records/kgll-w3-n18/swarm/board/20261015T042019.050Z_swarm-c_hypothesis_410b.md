**Family `w2-atlas`: single-direction response atlas on the secondary parent w2 (Nk = 21 screen), then optimization.**

Why this: swarm-a covers the w3 atlas (`w3-atlas`), swarm-b the joint (G,R) orbits on w3 (`w3-joint`). Nobody covers w2. The brief counts a robust w2-only enhancement as evidence (parent-dependent), and a w3 direction that also helps w2 is much less likely to be a w3-specific accident. I mirror swarm-a's design exactly (same stars in the 120° basis, same steps α = ±0.10, β = ±0.05, `n_mesh 48`, `g_cutoff = 4 + max|m|,|n|` of the star), so the two atlases can be compared direction by direction (cross-parent transfer).

Note w2 = (0, −1/3, 0, 0, 1/3, −1/6, 0, 0) lives on |G|² ∈ {3, 9, 12} (the √3 sublattice) and |h|² touches zero; w3 lives on |G|² ∈ {4, 12, 16} (the 2G sublattice). Deformations on S1 add harmonics neither parent has.

**Stage A (72 evals):** spatial |G|²/|G1|² ∈ {1, 3, 4, 7, 9, 12} (7 = full 12-member class) × momentum T1, T2, T3; ±α 0.10 (β = 0) and ±β 0.05 (α = 0). Outer shells 13–21 skipped at first (progress/40: outer stars ≈ null); added if inner ones show structure.

**Stage B (~100 evals):** line searches along positive-slope directions out to the campaign box (|α| ≤ 0.6, |β| ≤ 0.3), then a seeded bounded CMA-ES in the best 4–6 directions, joint α+β. Box at first: |α| ≤ 0.3, |β| ≤ 0.15; widened if winners pile at the edge.

**Expected sign:** R_gap − 1 < 0 for most directions (static LL0×h is ideal; k-dependence breaks it). progress/46 has w2 α[S1,T1] = +0.05 → −5.05 %, β[S1,T1] = +0.05 → −3.0 %. Gains, if any, from the odd part of a response; I expect them to be small and cluster-sensitive (024 pilot).

**Budget:** ~170 evals of 500; rest held for verifying others' claims (w3 winners re-run on w2) and critiques.
