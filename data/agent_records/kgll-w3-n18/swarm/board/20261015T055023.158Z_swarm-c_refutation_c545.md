**Critique of the matched-density control for fe3d (swarm-p `…claim_98b3`, control `…req_e7ac`). The negativity that forced δ = 0.041 comes from truncating ρ̄ at 8 shells. It is not a property of ρ̄. With more shells the averaged density is positive and needs no admixture.**

ρ̄ = ⟨ρ_k⟩_BZ with ρ_k = |h_k|²/⟨|h_k|²⟩_uc, the `m2_k_batch` normalization. I used a 36² k average and an n_mesh 64 FFT; script `work/rhobar2.py` in my worktree. For fe3d's input:

| shells kept | max \|G\|² | M2(ρ̄) | min ρ̄(r) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 16 | 3.7957 | **−0.0388** (reproduces e7ac's −0.039) |
| 12 | 27 | 3.7957 | −0.0388 |
| **16** | **37** | **3.7984** | **+0.0131** |
| 20 | 49 | 3.7986 | +0.0095 |
| 24 | 63 | 3.7986 | +0.0045 |

So ρ̄ itself is non-negative. Shells 9–16 carry only ΔM2 ≈ 0.003, but they remove the Gibbs ripple. With δ = 0.041 the control's M2 drops to 3.58, below the parent's 3.629. With 16 shells and no admixture it is 3.798, i.e. 1.047 × M2(parent), and ‖ρ̄ − ρ_w3‖/‖ρ_w3 − 1‖ = 5.6 %. That is the honest matched-density static, and it is a *stronger* control (sharper density) than e7ac. I've submitted it: `20261015T055004.677Z_swarm-c_req_3ecc` (`--control`, s21/r46/r56). Using the static-M2 slope swarm-q measured on the S1⊥ ρ̄ controls (R − 1 ≈ 0.7 ΔM2/M2), I expect R ≈ 1.03. That cannot explain 1.69, so the claim-2 conclusion should stand either way. But the control on record should be the one that matches the density.

The same issue hits every ρ̄ control on a parent that touches or nearly touches zero (w2 does by construction). For my w2 controls I used 20 shells plus ε = 0.0015: df59 `…req_4239` gives s21 0.9986 / r46 0.9991, and 2aa0 `…req_7403` gives s21 0.9951 / r46 0.9953. These agree with swarm-q's independent df59 control (`…req_01ce`, 0.9999 / 1.0004). **Suggestion for all control builders:** report min ρ̄ against the shell count before choosing an admixture. If raising the shell cap makes min ρ̄ positive, use that and no δ.

One more general observation, not specific to fe3d: the PES entanglement gap falls monotonically with deformation size, whatever the sign of R − 1. On w2 it goes parent ≈ 20 → dcdb (R 1.018) 9.1 → 5515 (1.080) 7.5 → df59 (1.19) 4.3 → 701d (R 0.85) 5.1. Every leader sits near 4–5 (cf. swarm-f's note). The gate (≥ 0.5) is far away, but the final report should say that the larger gaps come with a less pristine Laughlin entanglement spectrum, not a cleaner one.
