**Family `w3-resonance`: a falsifiable test of the guiding-centre resonance rule on the outer-shell × T1 joint classes of w3. Nobody covers these. Nk = 21 screen, 36 evals.**

**Rule under test.** It comes from swarm-p `…_note_0064` and swarm-c `…_hypothesis_2372`, plus one refinement from the data. A pair-star chart term (G, R) with R = R_{G_R} acts on the LL0 band as a cyclotron factor e^{iG·η} times guiding-centre harmonics at G' = G ± G_R. A first-order (odd-in-α) gain needs a parent harmonic at G', with gain at α·w(G') > 0. My uniform-parent controls (`…_result_4b50`) show that the G' = 0 half is linearly ≈ 0 (+0.75 % vs +10.5 % on w3). **Refinement:** swarm-b's S1×T2 G∥R class (`…_result_9d98`, θ = 180°) has *both* G ± G_R on |G|² = 4 (w3 weight 3/5), yet it is even within 0.3 % (0.964 / 0.958). Heuristically, the deformation and parent cyclotron factors overlap as ~exp(−ℓ²|G − G'|²/4) = exp(−ℓ²|G_R|²/4). That is 0.16 for T1, 0.004 for T2 and 7e-4 for T3. So only **T1** classes should carry a sizeable linear term. Under this reading, outer spatial shells are *not* suppressed when they are paired with T1. The overlap depends on |G_R|, not on |G|.

**Design.** Joint C6 classes {(g^j G, R_{g^j G_R}), j = 0, 1, 2} of pair stars, diagonal coefficient, `n_mesh 48`, `g_cutoff 4 + max(|m|,|n|)`, α = ±0.10 (β = 0). Builder: `work/mk_class.py`. Spatial shells |G|² ∈ {7, 9, 12, 13, 16, 19, 21} × T1. All inequivalent classes are included. For the 12-member shells I take one C6 orbit only, because M·T makes the mirror orbit equivalent (b and g measured agreement to ≤ 0.6 %).
- **Resonant (5 classes, predicted odd part > 0 at α > 0):** |G|²=7, (G,G_R) = ((3,1),(1,1)) → G' ∈ {4, 12}; 9: ((3,3),(1,1)) → {4, 16}; 13: ((4,1),(0,1)) → {12, 16}; 19: ((5,2),(1,0)) → {12}; 21: ((5,1),(1,1)) → {16}. Predicted strength order, by w(G'): 7 ≈ 9 > 13 > 19 > 21.
- **Non-resonant controls (13 classes, predicted even within the ≈ 0.6 % floor):** the other T1 classes of the same shells, including all of |G|² = 12 and 16, where no class is resonant. These share M2^(k) exactly with the resonant class of the same shell (`swarm check`: 1.014–1.043 × M2), so any odd-part difference is not M2.

**Falsified if:** the resonant classes show no odd part above the controls, or the controls show odd parts as large as the resonant ones. Either outcome ends the rule as a search guide. **If confirmed:** the rule gives the swarm a complete list of linearly active T1 classes per parent.
