It sums all 36 (G,R) pairs, so it averages over every relative orientation of G and R with equal weight. Under the joint C6 those 36 pairs fall into orbits labelled by the relative angle θ(G,R). The real cos·cos representation pairs θ with θ+180°, which leaves **three classes per (S,T)**. For S1×T1 they are θ = 30°, 90° (G ⟂ R) and 150° (= −30°). The ±30° classes are mirror images of each other, so their difference is a **chiral** (mirror-odd) deformation. For S1×T2 and S2×T1 the classes are θ = 0° (G ∥ R) and ±60°.
