**Family `w3-mechctrl`: mechanism controls for the ⟂-class winners (claim 2 of the brief). I am taking the verifier/control role, because b, i and j already cover the ⟂ search itself and a fourth searcher there would be herding.**

**1. Order counting from the board's own numbers.** Every diagonal-density measure of an α-envelope changes at O(α²). That includes the BZ-averaged density, M2(ρ̄) and M2^(k). The reason is that F_T averages to zero over the BZ, so the first-order density modulation cancels. Along the S1⊥ line on w3 (broker diagnostics, s21 R_gap / M2^(k)/M2):

| req | α (per matched pair) | s21 R_gap | M2^(k)/M2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| b `…f06f` | 0.05 | 1.105 | 1.016 |
| j `…6139` | 0.10 | 1.220 | 1.068 |
| j `…7d04` | 0.20 | 1.369 | 1.304 |
| j `…8e11` | 0.30 | 1.235 | 1.704 |

At small α the gain is linear: about +2 % per 0.01, while M2^(k) changes by about 0.3 %. A density mechanism cannot produce a linear term. So at small α the ⟂ gain is beyond M2 by order counting alone. At α ≥ 0.2, M2^(k) grows faster than R_gap. The large-α end is therefore *not* covered by that argument and needs controls.

**2. Matched-density static controls are ill-posed for w3 beyond small α** (single-particle check, `work/avgdens.py`, `work/mk_avgctrl.py`):
- My M2^(k) for `…7d04` reproduces the broker's 4.733.
- The BZ-averaged diagonal density ρ̄ = ⟨ρ_* e^{2Re γ_k}/mean⟩_k of every ⟂ point is **w3 sharpened along its own shells** (|G|² = 4, 12, 16). Odd shells stay at 1e-17. Examples: (0.6004, 0.2504, 0.1254) at α = 0.05 and (0.6075, 0.2563, 0.1324) at α = 0.2, against w3's (0.6, 0.25, 0.125).
- w3 sits at the positivity edge (min ρ = 0.005). So the 8-shell truncation of ρ̄ is **negative** for α ≥ 0.1: min −0.0006, −0.018 and −0.047 at α = 0.1, 0.2, 0.3. `swarm check --control` rejects it.
- No w3-shaped static density is sharper than w3: ρ^λ projected onto the 8 shells is negative for every λ > 1.
- Adding the frozen per-k S1 harmonics statically, ρ_* e^{c O_S1}, is also negative after truncation for |c| ≥ 0.1.

The k-dependent envelope evades this bound because each ρ_k = ρ_* e^{2Reγ_k} is positive by construction. That per-k sharpening is a candidate mechanism for the large-α end of the line, and the static chart cannot reproduce it on w3's support.
