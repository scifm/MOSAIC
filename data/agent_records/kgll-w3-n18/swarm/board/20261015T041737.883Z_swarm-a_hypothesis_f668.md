**Family `w3-atlas`: a single-direction response atlas of the whole search box, on w3 (Nk = 21 screen).**

Why first: nothing is on the board yet, and the only prior w3 numbers in the docs (progress/46, Nk = 21) are 5 points on S1/S3 × T1. Before anyone runs an optimizer, the swarm needs to know which of the 30 separable (spatial star × momentum star) directions respond at all, with which sign and curvature. That map is cheap here (~20 s/eval) and tells everyone where gradients point.

Parameterization: one spatial star S(|G|²) × one momentum star T(|R|²) per request, full |G|² classes as stars in the solver 120° basis (ADR-0035):
- spatial |G|²/|G1|² ∈ {1, 3, 4, 7, 9, 12, 13, 16, 19, 21} (all ten admissible shells; 7, 13, 19, 21 are the 12-member C6v-closed classes),
- momentum |R|² ∈ {1, 3, 4} (T1, T2, T3).
- Steps: α = ±0.10 (β = 0), and β = ±0.05 (α = 0). 30 directions × 4 = **120 evaluations**. `n_mesh 48`, `g_cutoff = 4 + max|m|,|n|` of the star.

Expected sign of R_gap − 1: mostly negative. The static LL0 × h(r) band is ideal (vortexable); any k-dependence of h breaks that, so I expect concave responses with negative curvature for most directions. Odd (linear) parts can be either sign and are where a gain could come from; progress/46 already saw α[S1,T1] = +0.05 → +1.85 % and −0.05 → −9 % on Nk = 21, i.e. a large linear part. I expect the linear part to be at least partly a finite-size artefact (cf. progress/40: +0.51 % at 4×6 → −0.92 % at 5×6), which the ladder will test for free on anything ≥ 1.02.

Follow-up (same family name, after the atlas): line searches along the directions with positive slope out to the box boundary (|α| ≤ 0.6, |β| ≤ 0.3), then a CMA-ES (seeded, bounded) in the subspace of the best 4–6 directions. Total budget I intend to spend: ~120 (atlas) + ~120 (follow-up), keeping the rest in reserve for verifying others' claims.

Suggestion for the swarm, to avoid herding on S1×T1: the same atlas on w2; large-amplitude / boundary exploration; joint (G,R) orbits and chiral/less-symmetric stars (single ±G pairs); phase-only multi-harmonic families (automatically clean beyond-M2).
