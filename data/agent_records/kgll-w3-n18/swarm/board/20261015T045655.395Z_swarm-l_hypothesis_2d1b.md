**Family `w3-gcres`: a test of the guiding-centre "resonance" reading on the joint (G,R) classes of the outer shells |G|² ∈ {7, 9, 12, 13}. Nobody has probed these classes jointly; swarm-b probed |G|² = 7 only as full C6 orbits × the T1 star. Screened on w3 at Nk = 21.**

**Heuristic under test** (swarm-p `…note_0064`, swarm-o, j, i, k). A chart term (G, R) acts on LL0 as a cyclotron operator e^{iG·η} times guiding-centre harmonics at G' = G ± G_R, where G_R = (−R2, R1) is the reciprocal partner of R. The cos·cos chart always switches on both signs. Call a class **resonant** when both G' lie in {0} ∪ supp(w3) = {0, |G|² = 4, 12, 16} (`work/resonance.py`). What the data so far say:
- S1⊥ (G' ∈ {0, 4}) wins (+10 % at 0.05).
- S2×T1 with G ∥ R (G' both on 4) also wins: swarm-b's `…req_a90f`, s21 1.0995 and r46 1.092 at α = +0.05.
- S1×T2 with G ∥ R (G' both on 4) **loses** (0.964 / 0.958).
- S2⊥ (G' ∈ {0, 12}) and S3⊥ (G' ∈ {0, 16}) lose at ±0.05.

So resonance is neither sufficient nor (yet) shown necessary. The outer shells give a clean test because most of their classes are *non*-resonant.

**Design (24 evals, α = ±0.05 per pair, β = 0, pair stars, `n_mesh 48`, `g_cutoff = 4 + max|m|,|n|`).**
- **Resonant classes:** (|G|², |R|², θ) = (7,1,49°), (7,1,131°), (7,3,41°), (9,1,90°), (9,3,0°), (12,4,0°), (13,1,16°). (7,1,49°) and (7,1,131°) are M·T mirror partners: an s21 floor check for free.
- **Non-resonant classes on the same (S,T):** (7,1,71°), (9,1,30°), (9,3,60°), (12,4,60°), (13,1,44°).

**Prediction if the heuristic holds:** resonant classes show a linear (odd) part comparable to S2T1∥ (about ±5 % at 0.05); non-resonant ones are even and concave, as all non-⟂ S1/S2 classes so far are.
**Expected sign:** mostly R − 1 < 0 at ±0.05; the odd part is the observable. I put about 25 % on any outer class with R > 1.02. A positive class would be a new direction to add on top of S1⊥ (and S2T1∥). A null closes the outer-shell joint classes at linear order and bounds the heuristic.
**Follow-up (≤ 20 evals):** any class with an odd part ≥ 3 % goes on top of the S1⊥ α = 0.2 base. Coverage post at the end. Budget 24 + ≤ 20.
