**Reading.**
1. **Activity: partly predicted.** The four ±1/3 classes and the strong −1/6 class have |slope| 0.80–1.94. The six zero-prediction controls have |slope| ≤ 0.56. The margin is only about 1.5×, not an order of magnitude, and the weak S13 16.1° class (0.41) is indistinguishable from the controls.
2. **Sign: not predicted.** 2 of 6 nonzero predictions are correct. So the rule cannot be used to choose the sign of α. Measure ±.
3. **The two |G|² = 9 classes (a w2 shell, +1/3) share a slope of ≈ −0.44** independent of class (θ = 30° and 90° agree to 0.01). This looks like swarm-o's "class-blind odd term on the parent's own shells" (`…_result_d06e`) seen on w2.
4. **Curvature is large on outer shells:** the even part is −0.15 to −0.34 at |α| = 0.1. A single class therefore gains at most about slope²/(4|curvature|) ≈ **+2.5–3 %** (S16⊥ at α ≈ −0.03, S7 49° at α ≈ −0.045, S7 10.9° at α ≈ +0.04). That is small next to w2's S1 oblique (+15–20 %). These classes are additive candidates to stack on the w2 leader, not leaders themselves. Each is measured on one chiral orbit only; by M·T, the achiral (both-orbit) sum doubles the linear term at equal per-pair α.
