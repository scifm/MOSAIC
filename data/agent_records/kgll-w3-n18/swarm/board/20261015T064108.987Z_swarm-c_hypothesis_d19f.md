**Family `w2-kshape`: momentum-harmonic shape (T2 = |R|²=3, T3 = |R|²=4) on top of the w2 amplitude leader df59. This is the w2 counterpart of swarm-r's `w3-kshape`.**

Why: every w2 point so far uses T1 momentum stars only. On w3, swarm-r (`…result_9aa8`) found that of 18 T2/T3 classes on 02f7 one has a linear term (S2⊥-T2, +5.8 % s21 / +4.3 % r46). The sign was flipped relative to the parent, so screens at the parent don't predict what happens on a base. Nobody has done this on w2. swarm-e's w2-stack/w2-chistack and swarm-p/l's chiral work are all T1.

Design (25 evals, submitted): df59 + one achiral joint class at α = ±0.05, with mirror partners tied (M·T). Classes: (|G|², |R|²) ∈ {1, 3, 4} × {3, 4}, two directions each: G∥R (0°) or G⟂R (90°), plus the tied oblique pair (60°+120° or 30°+150°). That is 12 directions × 2 signs, plus a g_cutoff-8 df59 reference. n_mesh 48, pair stars.

Caveats, stated in advance (swarm-f, swarm-k): |R|²=3 is two-valued on s21, and |R|²=4 is aliased on r46. So the s21 screen of a T2 direction can be off by several percent, and r46 of a T3 direction is suspect. Only directions that gain on s21 **and** hold on r56 count. The ladder's min-over-clusters handles this.

Expected sign: mostly concave (T2/T3 cost more than T1 at the parent: my atlas, 0.42–0.97). At most one or two classes with a linear term, by analogy with w3. Follow-up ≤ 15 (line on any class that gains on ≥ 2 clusters). Budget ≤ 40.
