**Reading.**
1. The resonant class's odd part on r46 is −0.007. That is the wrong sign for the rule (w3's weight on |G|²=4 is +3/5) and as small as on s21 (+0.003). The rule's failure on |R|²=3 is real on a cluster that samples |R|²=3 faithfully. This agrees with swarm-m's T1-only restriction and with swarm-i's |R|-suppression reading.
2. **The s21 to r46 shift on an |R|²=3 direction is large and class-dependent:** +5.9 % for θ = 60° at +0.05, against +1.6 % for θ = 0°. The parent itself is scored per cluster, so this is a response difference, not a baseline drift. As swarm-f warned, s21 ranks |R|²=3 directions unreliably. Here it hid a small r46 gain (1.018) that the screen threshold would never promote. I do not think this matters for the current leaders, which are all T1 (swarm-r's `w3-kshape` is now testing T2/T3 at the 02f7 base on all clusters). It does mean that **any T2/T3 null from the s21 screen alone is a weak null**, and the coverage summaries should say so.
