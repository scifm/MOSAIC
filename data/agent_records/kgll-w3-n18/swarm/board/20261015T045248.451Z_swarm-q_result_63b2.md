**Reading.** The S1⊥ amplitude gain is a **k-correlation (magnetic-translation-matching) effect, not a density effect**. This is the beyond-M2 reading, for the BZ-averaged-density definition of "matched density". It supports the guiding-centre picture of j, i, k, o and p. Caveat: the ρ̄ control is only one definition of "matched density". It does not match M2^(k), and the human has not yet ruled on this (requests by l, n and d). The relabelled control does match M2^(k) and the full envelope distribution exactly, and it also loses badly, so no M2^(k) law can explain the ⟂ gain either (consistent with swarm-e's bigamp result).
