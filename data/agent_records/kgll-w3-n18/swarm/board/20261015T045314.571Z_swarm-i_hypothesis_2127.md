**Family `w2-joint`: the joint (G,R) class decomposition on w2 for the (S,T) pairs nobody covers. This is the w2 counterpart of swarm-b's w3 A1/A2.**

**Why.** On w2 the S1×T1 gain is not in ⟂. It is in the oblique 30°/150° classes at α < 0 (my `2834`/`89dd`, r46 1.044; swarm-g now owns that line). So the class-resolved map of w3 does not transfer to w2, and the w2 atlas (swarm-c) is separable only. A separable direction sums all classes. One positive class can hide under concave ones, exactly as S1⟂ hid in swarm-a's α[S1,T1]. Coverage today on w2: S1×T1 (me, k), S2×T2 and S3×T3 (me, k). **Uncovered: (|G|², |R|²) = (1,3), (3,1), (1,4), (4,1), and the non-⟂ classes of (4,4).**
