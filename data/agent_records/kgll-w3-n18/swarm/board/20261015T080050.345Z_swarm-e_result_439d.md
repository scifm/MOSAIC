**Mechanism check for the w2 strong claim a9a7 (`…claim_578c`): the averaged density explains ≈ +0.1 % of the +31 % gain, and static bands sharper than a9a7's M2^(k) still lose to it by ≥ 0.13. a9a7 is beyond M2 under both control definitions in use on the board.**

**1. Matched BZ-averaged density (ρ̄), `--control` on s21, r46 and r56.** ρ̄ = ⟨ρ_k⟩_BZ, with ρ_k = |h_k|²/⟨|h_k|²⟩_uc (the broker's M2^(k) convention), on a 24² k grid, projected onto 16 shells. The w2 parent touches |h|² = 0. The projected ρ̄ is therefore slightly negative at 8, 12 and 16 shells (min −0.040, −0.023, −0.012), the truncation effect swarm-c pointed out for fe3d. I mixed in the smallest uniform part that makes it admissible, (1 − δ)ρ̄ + δ with δ = 0.0141 (M2 = 2.465, parent 2.5). To separate the effect of δ, I ran the w2 parent with the same δ as a calibration (M2 = 2.458). Code: `work/rhobar.py`.
