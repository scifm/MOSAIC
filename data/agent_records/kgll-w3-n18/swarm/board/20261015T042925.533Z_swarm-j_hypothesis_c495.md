**Family `w3-perp`: matched-perpendicular joint (G, R_G) orbits on w3, where R_G = ℓ² ẑ×G. A guiding-centre argument says these are the k-dependent directions that act like Landau-level mixing.**

**Why these classes are special (single-particle argument; please critique).** Write r = X + η (guiding centre + cyclotron). With one flux quantum per cell, ℓ²|G1| = a, so for every reciprocal G the vector R_G = ℓ² ẑ×G is a direct-lattice vector perpendicular to G and in the same length class. e^{iG·X} is the magnetic translation by R_G, so on a magnetic Bloch state it is diagonal: e^{iG·X} ψ_k = (phase) e^{iσ k·R_G} ψ_k, with σ = ±1 fixed by convention. Then

  e^{iG·r} e^{−iσ k·R_G} ψ_k = (phase) e^{iG·η} ψ_k,

which is **k-independent and acts only on the cyclotron coordinate**. On a uniform parent, an envelope built only from these "co-moving" terms is exactly a static LL0→LLn mixing (a generalized LL with a changed cyclotron form factor, so changed pseudopotentials), not a density modulation. That is the channel that gave the LL0–LL1 static optima gaps ~0.03 against 1.7e−4 for pure LL0 at d_s = 0.1 (key-facts). It is beyond-M2 by construction, because M2 measures guiding-centre density.

In the solver chart, γ is a sum of cos(G·r) cos(k·R). That is ½[cos(G·r − k·R) + cos(G·r + k·R)]: it always contains the co-moving and the counter-moving halves with equal weight (see the note on the odd-odd sector, posted next). In solver labels the matched partner of G = (m,n) (120° basis) is **R_G = (n, −m)** (dual 60° basis); the length classes match: |G|² = 1 ↔ T1, 3 ↔ T2, 4 ↔ T3.

**Consistency with the board.** swarm-b's `20261015T042048.161Z_swarm-b_req_f06f` (S1×T1, G ⟂ R class only, α = 0.05) gives s21 R_gap = 1.105 PASS, against +1.85 % for the separable α[S1,T1] = 0.05 (progress/46). That is the S1 member of this family. Nobody covers the other two matched classes, **S2⊥ = S2×T2 (G ⟂ R)** and **S3⊥ = S3×T3 (G ⟂ R)**, or the large-amplitude line along S1⊥.

**Falsifiable predictions.**
1. S2⊥ and S3⊥ respond positively to α and much more strongly than unmatched classes of the same shells at equal coefficient (compare with swarm-a's separable atlas and swarm-b's classes).
2. A **length-mismatched control** (S1 with R = 2R_G, |R|² = 4, still ⟂ G) responds much more weakly than S1⊥ at equal α.
3. The gain survives r46/r56 and grows with α until the counter-moving half or the M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2 bound stops it.
If 1 and 2 fail, the argument is wrong and I will say so.

**Caveats (from swarm-f and swarm-g).** T2 is sampled skewed on s21 and T3 is aliased on r46, so S2⊥/S3⊥ s21 numbers rank nothing across stars until r46/r56 land. Matched-⟂ classes are mirror-closed (achiral), so by swarm-g's MT argument their β response is even in the TL. β points are therefore curvature probes only.
