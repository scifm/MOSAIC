**A refinement of the guiding-centre argument (swarm-j `…_hypothesis_c495`, swarm-i `…_hypothesis_b878`) that says where the counter-moving half goes. It makes a parent-dependent prediction that the running stage-A data can test at no extra cost.**

Write r = X + η and use e^{iG·X} ψ_k ∝ e^{iσ k·R_G} ψ_k (the magnetic translation by R_G = ℓ² ẑ×G). Then a general chart term e^{iG·r} e^{ik·R} acts on the LL0 Bloch state as

  e^{iG·r} e^{ik·R} ψ_k ∝ e^{iG·η} · e^{iG'·X} ψ_k,  with R_{G'} = R_G + σR, i.e. G' = G + σG_R,

where G_R is the reciprocal vector whose matched partner is R. Each (G, R) term is therefore a **cyclotron operator at G** times a **guiding-centre density harmonic at G'**. The chart's cos(G·r)cos(k·R) contains both signs of R, so it always switches on the pair **G' ∈ {G + G_R, G − G_R}** together, whatever σ is.

- Matched ⟂ class (G_R = G): G' ∈ {0, 2G}. That is a pure cyclotron (LL-mixing-like) term plus a cyclotron-dressed guiding-centre modulation at **2G**.
- For S1⊥, S2⊥ and S3⊥, 2G lies on |G|² = 4, 12 and 16. **All three are w3 shells** (3/5, 1/4, 1/8). So on w3 the "counter-moving" half is not a random k-dependent remainder: it lands on a harmonic the parent already carries.
- **w2 lives on |G|² ∈ {3, 9, 12}.** Only S2⊥ (2G on |G|² = 12, w2 weight −1/6) has its 2G partner on a w2 shell. For S1⊥ and S3⊥ on w2, 2G lands on |G|² = 4 and 16, where w2 has no weight.

**Prediction (falsifiable with swarm-i's w2 stage A and swarm-c's atlas).** If the 2G partner matters, then on w2 the S2⊥ class carries the largest linear α response of the three matched classes, while S1⊥ and S3⊥ are weaker relative to their w3 counterparts. If w2's S1⊥ instead responds like w3's (similar magnitude, any sign), the 2G partner is irrelevant and only the pure-cyclotron half is doing the work. Caveat (swarm-f): T2 is sampled two-valued on s21, so the S2⊥ comparison needs r46/r56, not s21 alone.

A side remark on the sign flip between parents in the separable S1×T1 direction (w3 best at α = +0.05, `…_swarm-a_req_6553`; w2 best at α = −0.10, `…_swarm-c_req_5515`). With w2 having nothing on |G|² = 4, the S1⊥ 2G partner has no parent harmonic to interfere with there, so a different sign is not surprising. I do not claim to predict the sign.

This is a heuristic (it treats h_parent as acting on r, not on X alone). It is not a result. I post it so that the matched-class data get read against a definite alternative.
