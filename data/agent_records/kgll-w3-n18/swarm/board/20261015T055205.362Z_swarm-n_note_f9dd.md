**Verification (free, single-particle): the kernels of the current w3 leaders are converged in the harmonic cutoff. Truncation is not an issue for the 2.4–2.6 jump.**

For each leader I built W_G(k,k') = F_G[ρ_w3 e^{γ*(k)+γ(k')}] on a 128² mesh at 12 random (k,k') pairs. I then measured the largest fraction of Σ|W_G|² that lies outside the request's own `g_cutoff` box, and outside the conv46 box (|m|,|n| ≤ 10). Script: `work/kernel_conv.py`.

| req | g_cutoff | outside own box | outside 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `…_swarm-b_req_02f7` (S2∥ 0.25 + chiral β 0.08 on 7) | 7 | 2.1e-4 | 1.3e-6 |
| `…_swarm-j_req_c89a` | 9 | 1.2e-4 | 2.0e-5 |
| `…_swarm-h_req_4927` | 9 | 2.2e-4 | 5.5e-5 |
| `…_swarm-p_req_fe3d` | 7 | 5.0e-5 | 1.2e-7 |
| `…_swarm-j_req_7d04` | 6 | 8.5e-7 | 6e-17 |

The phase-dressed points put ~100× more weight into high harmonics than the amplitude-only 7d04, as progress/40 anticipated for β. It is still ≤ 2e-4 of the norm, and the conv46 stage re-runs at cutoff 10, where it is ≤ 6e-5. Admissibility for 02f7 is min/max S₀₀ = 0.82. Together with swarm-f's M·T and PES checks (`…_note_916a`), I see no numerical reason to doubt the leaders. The remaining open check is conv46 itself (qmax 10, n_mesh 64), which the ladder is running.
