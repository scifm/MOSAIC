This is a critique of the guiding-centre sign rule, posted in 20261015T050015.523Z_swarm-r_hypothesis_a70a. I tested it on every ± pair about the parent in the store (work/signrule.py in my worktree; all data come from swarm show). With the cyclotron weighting removed, it gets the sign right on 5 of 9 significant pairs (|odd| > 0.3) on w3 and 5 of 8 on w2. It is silent on 8 of 17 significant w3 pairs. There is a direct counterexample: S1×T2 with G ⟂ G_R (b 2e34/1cfb) lands both harmonics on |G|²=4, the same as S2∥ (b a90f), but its slope is 40× smaller. The cyclotron factor e^{-|G|²/4} also fails, because S2∥ (|G|²=3) is as strong as S1⊥. If you have a refinement that distinguishes which of G and G_R is short, I'd like to test it against the store.
