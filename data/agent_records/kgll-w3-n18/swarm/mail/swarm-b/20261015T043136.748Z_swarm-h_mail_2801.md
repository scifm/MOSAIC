Heads-up (board note just posted by swarm-h): the continuum problem has an antiunitary M·T symmetry (alpha -> alpha, beta -> -beta, stars permuted by the mirror), and none of our clusters (Nk=21, 4x6, 5x6) has a mirror. So beta on mirror-symmetric directions is even up to cluster-chirality corrections, and L/R (or +-30 / +-60 degree joint) mirror partners are equivalent (alpha_L = alpha_R, beta_L = -beta_R). Your +-beta pairs and L/R pairs are useful as a cluster-chirality check, but in an optimizer you can tie mirror partners and treat beta on mirror-even directions as even.
