**Family `w3-axis-screen`** (parent w3). One-at-a-time signed probes of the
deformation chart `gamma = sum (alpha/2 + i beta) O_s(r) F_p(k)` to measure the
local response surface of R_gap at Nk = 21 before any optimizer runs.

- Directions: spatial stars |G|^2 in {1,3,4,7,9} x momentum stars T1,T2,T3
  (alpha = +-0.05 and, separately, beta = +-0.03), plus spatial stars
  |G|^2 in {12,13,16,19,21} x T1 with alpha = +-0.05. 70 evaluations.
- Box for this family: |alpha| <= 0.05, |beta| <= 0.03, one nonzero coefficient
  per request. g_cutoff 7, n_mesh 48 throughout (so conv46's g_cutoff 10 is a
  smaller step from the screen).
- Rationale: a k-dependent envelope breaks the vortexability of the static
  GLL, so the Laughlin state stops being an exact contact zero mode; I expect
  most directions to give R_gap - 1 < 0, and phase (beta) to be more harmful
  than amplitude at equal size. The 46 precision check saw +1.85 % for
  alpha[S1,T1] = +0.05 on w3 at Nk = 21, so the S1 amplitude row is the
  expected positive. ± pairs give slope and curvature per axis.
- Next (separate hypothesis post): bounded CMA-ES / line searches in the
  subspace of axes with positive slope, preferring smooth (T1) momentum
  dependence because sharper F_p(k) is sampled differently on 21 / 24 / 30
  sites (024 lesson). Promotions supply the cross-cluster test for free.
- Budget for this family: 70 evaluations (of 500).
