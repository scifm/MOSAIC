**Two static controls per candidate** (`--control`, run on s21, r46 and r56, never ranked). Scripts are in `work/control_build.py` and `work/control_m2sq.py`.
- **A `kavg`, the literal matched diagonal density:** the static |h|² equals the BZ average (36² grid) of ρ_k(r) = |h_k|²/⟨|h_k|²⟩_uc. This is the normalization of the M2^(k) diagnostic. The result is truncated to the parent's 8 shells; harmonics outside them are ≤ 8e-4 and Gaussian-suppressed. Result: ρ̄ differs from ρ_* by at most 4e-4 (56da) and 1.3e-3 (fa76) in any w_G, and M2(ρ̄) is 3.634 and 3.644. That is expected, since ⟨F_T1(k)⟩_BZ = 0, so ρ̄ = ρ_* + O(α²).
- **B `m2`, the static M2 law at matched M2:** ρ_t = (1−t)ρ_* + t·ρ_*²/M2_*, with t chosen so that Σ|w_G|² = M2^(k) of the candidate. Both terms are non-negative and C6-symmetric with finite Fourier series. Two simpler constructions fail the positivity gate because w3 nearly touches zero: the linear rescaling 1 + λ(ρ_*−1), and ρ_*^p projected on 8 shells.

**Structural expectation (stated before the data):** the ⟂-orbit gain is linear in amplitude: +10.5 % at 0.05 and +18.9 % at 0.0866, a ratio of 1.80 against an amplitude ratio of 1.73. Every k-averaged density measure (ρ̄ − ρ_*, M2^(k) − M2) is even in α, so it cannot produce a linear term. I expect control A to give R ≈ 1.00 and control B to give R ≈ 1 + O(M2 change), i.e. about +1.5 % and +5 %. If so, the ⟂-orbit gain is not the changed k-averaged density. **Caveat:** the k-resolved diagonal density ρ_k does change at first order. No static control can match it, so "beyond M2" here means beyond the k-averaged density and beyond the M2 law, not beyond W_G(k,k).
