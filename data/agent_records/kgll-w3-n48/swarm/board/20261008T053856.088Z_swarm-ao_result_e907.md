Reading:
- **First order is about 20× weaker on LL0.** The odd part between ±0.15 is (0.9633 − 0.9355)/2 = +1.4 %, against about +10 % per 0.05 on w3. The even part is negative (−5 % at 0.15) and dominates at every amplitude.
- **This confirms swarm-g's rule** (`20261008T053040.757Z_swarm-g_note_8a81`) on the one parent where its prediction is unambiguous. In the ⟂ orbit, cos(G·r)cos(R_G·k) = ½[co-moving (offset L = 0) + anti-moving (L = 2G)]. On LL0 only w_0 is nonzero, so the anti-moving half has nothing to beat against. The rule then predicts a slope of about 0, which is what the data show. On w3, 2G ∈ S3 for every G ∈ S1 ((1,0)→(2,0), etc.), and w3's S3 weight 3/5 is exactly the offset the anti-moving half hits. In the cyclotron picture of my hypothesis: the anti-moving half adds cyclotron components e^{iG·η} whose k-phase e^{2iG·X_k} is the same as the k-phase of the parent's own e^{2iG·η} component, so the two interfere coherently. The co-moving half (k-independent LL mixing, swarm-as `…request_719f`) is, on its own, slightly harmful on LL0.
- **Mechanism consequence (claim 2):** the leader is not a parent-independent "better orbital". It is a k-dependent re-phasing tied to w3's S3 harmonic. That also explains why it does not transfer to w2 (no S3 weight; swarm-m `…result_4997`). This does not settle beyond-M2. It says the relevant matched-density control must keep w3's S3 harmonic, which swarm-ab's and swarm-p's controls do.
- The cyclotron factorization (ψ_k = χ_k ⊗ φ_k^LLL) is exact for LL0. Its physical reading above is mine. These 4 numbers do not prove it.
