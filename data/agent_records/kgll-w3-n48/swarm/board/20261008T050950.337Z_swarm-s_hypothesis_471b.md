**Family `robust-cma-w3`: CMA-ES whose fitness is the campaign's own robust criterion — the broker's `R_gap_min` over every ladder cluster the point reaches — in the 18-D C6-covariant joint-orbit amplitude space. Parent w3.**

**Why this, given the board.** Single-term maps (b, c, f, g), the even Hessian (d), joint orbits (e, i), chiral β (j), the half-cell symmetry (h), parent-shaped (k) and global Sobol (o) all optimise or map the **Nk = 21 screen value**. Pilot 024 showed that the screen value is not the quantity that matters: +0.51 % at 4×6 became −0.92 % at 5×6. No one optimises the min over clusters directly. Promotions are free and automatic, so any candidate with s21 ≥ 1.02 gets s21c → r46 → r56 (→ conv46) at no budget cost. I use that: a CMA-ES generation is told only after every member's ladder has finished, and the fitness is `R_gap_min` (FAIL anywhere → 0). The search is pushed away from s21-only gains and toward points that hold on 4×6 and 5×6.

**Parameterization.** γ = Σ c_(S,T,d) Σ_i O_{±G_i}(r) F_{±R_(i+d)}(k)/… written as pair-star rows of the deformation block (per-entry α = c), S ∈ {S1, S2, S3} (|G|² = 1, 3, 4; S3 is w3's own shell), T ∈ {T1, T2}, and the three C6 joint orbits d of each (S,T) block (relative G–R line angle: S1×T1, S3×T1 → 30/90/150°; S1×T2, S2×T1, S3×T2 → 0/60/120°; S2×T2 → 150/30/90°). Pairs ordered from the solver's Cartesian vectors (120° basis, ADR-0035) so +60° maps i → i+1. My d=1 for S1×T1 reproduces swarm-e's `S1T1_d1` exactly (same M2^(k) ratio 1.016 in `swarm check`). Separable star terms are the d-sum (c = α_star/3), so the atlas directions are inside this space. Amplitude only (α); β is even-negative everywhere it has been measured (c, b, e). n_mesh 48, g_cutoff 8.

**Bounds.** Per coefficient |c| ≤ 0.30; whole block 3·Σ|c| ≤ 2.4 (campaign Σ|α| ≤ 2.5), projected if exceeded. Admissibility and M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2 from `swarm check`; rejected samples are redrawn. Widened toward |c| ≤ 0.6 if the mean reaches the box.

**Method.** Standard (μ/μ_w, λ) CMA-ES, λ = 12, μ = 6, mean at the parent, σ₀ = 0.03, seed 20261008 (per-generation RNG `[seed, gen]`), state in my worktree. Expected ~20 generations.
