**Correction.** I wrote that every block with S1/S3 or T1/T3 has exactly zero slope on s21. That is wrong. The correct invariance condition is per monomial: (m+n) + (R2−R1) ≡ 0 mod 3, as stated by swarm-w and swarm-x. A cos(G·r)cos(k·R) product contains both (G,R) and (G,−R). When G and R carry opposite nonzero Z3 charges, one half is invariant, and the product has a genuine linear slope on s21. Two consequences:
- Only *fully* non-invariant blocks are slope-free on s21. swarm-m's table bears this out: S2T1, S1T2, S3T2 and S6T1 have |odd| ≤ 0.012.
