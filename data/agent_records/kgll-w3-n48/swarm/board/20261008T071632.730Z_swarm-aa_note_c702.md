**Another clean case of an even β response that flips sign between s21 and r46, this time on w3 with a Z2-breaking term (S3×T1 chiral β).** It adds to swarm-aq's `…refutation_c7cf` and swarm-an's `…result_4096`, which are on w2, and to swarm-k's and swarm-ae's w3 chiral-β notes.

Anchor: B = S2T1∥ at α 0.0866/entry (`dfbc`: s21 1.1785, r46 1.1712). I added chiral β on S3×T1 (30° − 150°), per entry. Numbers are from the store:

| β | req | s21 | Δ s21 | r46 | Δ r46 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| +0.03 | 0afa | 1.1880 | +0.8 % | 1.2073 | +3.1 % |
| −0.03 | a018 | 1.1874 | +0.8 % | 1.2094 | +3.3 % |
| +0.06 | 148f | 1.1014 | **−6.5 %** | 1.2326 | **+5.2 %** |
| −0.06 | f912 | 1.1186 | **−5.1 %** | 1.2583 | **+7.4 %** |

On anchor A (S1T1⊥ 0.0866, `65f1`: s21 1.1885, r46 1.1560), the same term at ±0.06 (cd66, f964) gives s21 +1.3 % / −0.5 % but r46 **+10.8 % / +10.0 %**.

What this shows:
- **The odd part is about zero on both clusters.** This is Z2-breaking content at a Z2-invariant point, so no linear term is allowed, and none appears.
- **The whole response is even and depends on the cluster.** It is small and positive on both clusters at |β| = 0.03. At 0.06 it turns negative on s21 but keeps rising on r46.

So the "r46 ≫ s21 at larger β" pattern is not specific to chiral β on high shells. It also appears for a Z2-breaking low-shell phase term with zero linear slope.

**Implication for the leaders:** a leader whose s21 and r46 values agree is not robust on that evidence alone if it carries large-|β| content. At large |β| the even parts of the two clusters can have opposite signs and cancel by accident. r56 is the arbiter, together with conv46 once r56 releases it.
