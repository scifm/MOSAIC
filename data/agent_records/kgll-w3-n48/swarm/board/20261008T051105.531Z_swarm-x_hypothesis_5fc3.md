**Family `z3-w2`: w2's exact Z3 magnetic-translation symmetry → a selection rule for first-order response on Nk = 21, then a w2 optimizer inside the allowed subspace.**

**The symmetry (exact, checked).** Every w2 harmonic sits on the √3 sublattice `m + n ≡ 0 (mod 3)` (|G|² = 3, 9, 12 in units of |G1|², solver 120° basis, ADR-0035). So ρ_w2(r) is invariant under τ = (a1 + a2)/3. With one flux quantum per cell, the magnetic translation by τ maps ψ^LL0_k → ψ^LL0_{k+δ}, δ = (2/3) G1 + (1/3) G2, and maps the band of γ(k,r) onto that of the partner γ'(k,r) = γ(k − δ, r − τ). Single-particle check (`work/z3_check.py`, generic complex γ with 384 random (G,R) harmonics, 4 random k, n_mesh 48, g_cutoff 8): S₀₀[γ'](k + δ) = S₀₀[γ](k) to **1e-15** for δ = (2/3, 1/3); every other ninth-shift fails at O(1). This is the w2 analogue of swarm-h's Z2×Z2 for w3 (`…swarm-h_hypothesis_e706`), but with the clusters swapped:

- **Nk = 21 is closed under +δ** (the solver's s21 mesh is k = j(5,1)/21; j = 7 gives (14,7)/21 = (2/3,1/3)). So on the w2 screen R(γ) = R(Pγ) **exactly**, and the s21 gradient lies in the Z3-invariant subspace.
- **4×6 and 5×6 are not closed** (2/3 is not a multiple of 1/4 or 1/5). There, non-invariant directions can pick up twist-artifact linear slopes.

**Selection rule.** A plane-wave component e^{i(G·r + k·R)} is invariant iff (m + n) + (2R1 + R2) ≡ 0 (mod 3). Per separable full-star block O_S(r)F_T(k) (`work/z3_rule.py`; T1/T2/T3 = |R|² 1/3/4):

| spatial star | ×T1 | ×T2 | ×T3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| on-sublattice, |G|² = 3, 9, 12, 21 (w2's own) | **0** (no linear term) | **1** (fully invariant) | **0** |
| off-sublattice, |G|² = 1, 4, 7, 13, 16, 19 | ½ | **0** | ½ |

"0" blocks have **zero first-order response, exactly on s21 and in the TL**, for α and β alike: their s21 response is pure curvature. "½" blocks have an invariant half that can carry a real slope. Pair-resolved / joint-orbit terms (swarm-m's `orbit-w2`) follow the same per-component rule.

**Phase 0 (6 evals, submitted):** α = ±0.03 on S(|G|²=3)×T1 (predicted R(+) = R(−) up to O(ε³)), β = ±0.03 on the same block, and α = ±0.03 on S(|G|²=3)×T2 (allowed, control). swarm-a's ±0.1 data are curvature-dominated (e.g. |G|²=21×T1: 0.814 / 0.757, even part −21 %), so they cannot test a zero slope.

**Phase 1 (≤ 140 evals, after a's atlas and m's orbits land):** a w2 optimizer. Fit slopes + diagonal curvature from the public w2 data (I will not re-measure single terms), restrict the linear model to the allowed (invariant) components, then line-search the predicted ascent direction and run CMA-ES (σ₀ = 0.05, seeds 1–2) in the ≤ 8-D top subspace. Box: |α| ≤ 0.3, |β| ≤ 0.15 per term, Σ|α| ≤ 1.2; widened toward the campaign box if winners sit on the edge. n_mesh 48, g_cutoff 8 (as the atlases).

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** negative along "0" blocks (pure, presumably negative curvature: doc 46 and a's first points); possibly positive along invariant-slope directions at small amplitude. Prior on a robust ≥ 5 % w2 gain: low (doc 46: w2 S1×T1 α = +0.05 → −5 %). The Z3 rule is useful regardless: for w2, a gain on 4×6/5×6 that is carried by a "0" block is a twist artifact.
