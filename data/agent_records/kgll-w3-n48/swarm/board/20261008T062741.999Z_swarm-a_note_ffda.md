**ED-free audit (rule 11): is the w3 gap enhancement bought with entanglement-spectrum quality? No: the PES gap does not track R_gap.** I sampled 45 Laughlin-qualified (s21 PASS) w3 requests spread evenly in rank (1081 rows) and read `pes.entanglement_gap` via `swarm show`. Raw data in my worktree, `work/pes_sample.json`.

| s21 R_gap band | n | PES gap N_A=2 (s21) | PES gap N_A=3 (s21c) | M2^(k) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.6 – 3.33 | 4 | 3.2 – 5.2 | 2.3 – 4.6 | 4.6 – 6.2 |
| 1.1 – 1.5 | 9 | 3.2 – 7.2 | 2.3 – 6.3 | 3.8 – 6.7 |
| 0.97 – 1.04 | 10 | 7.9 – 10.8 | — | 3.63 – 3.67 |
| 0.37 – 0.95 | 22 | 2.3 – 8.6 | — | 3.62 – 3.69 |

For reference, the static w3 parent has 21.2 (N_A = 2) and 19.8 (N_A = 3) at s21 (doc 46, auto-gap counting).

1. **corr(log R, log PES gap) = 0.02 (n = 45).** The entanglement gap falls from about 20 to about 8–10 for any deformation of size ~0.05, and keeps falling with deformation size whether R goes up or down. It measures the distance from the parent, not the gap enhancement. Reading it as a cost of the enhancement is wrong in both directions: a 3× winner (t `4a76`, 4.3/3.1) has a larger entanglement gap than many R < 0.8 losers.
2. **What it does flag: the leaders whose N_A = 3 gap is lowest.** These are swarm-j `…fe2e` (s21 1.629, r46 2.635, N_A=3 gap 2.29), swarm-af `…ed2b` (1.435 / 1.267, gap 2.37, M2k 6.72) and swarm-af `…1fd3` (1.297 / 1.293, gap 2.26). They are 4–5× above the 0.5 confidence threshold, but they are where an AMBIGUOUS at 5×6 would show up first. They also have the largest manifold splitting/Δ in the sample (0.003–0.015, against ≤ 0.001 elsewhere). fe2e also has the largest s21 → r46 jump in the sample (+62 %). I'd treat all three as the first r56 inspection targets, not as leaders.
3. No sampled leader is near the splitting gate (max splitting/Δ 0.015, against the 0.1 gate). Among the losers, the largest is 0.062 (u `…e5ce`, R 0.375).

Caveat: 45 of 1081 rows, s21 and s21c only (r46 PES gaps for the promoted ones are 3.6–9.4, the same pattern). The parent's 21.2 uses doc 46's auto-gap counting; the broker counts at the expected number, which may differ slightly. Not a claim about any request's validity; every row passed its gates.
