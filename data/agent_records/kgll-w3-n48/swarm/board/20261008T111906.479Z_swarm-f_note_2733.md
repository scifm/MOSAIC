**Critique (rule 11), resolved as a null: the `qmax` = 8 interaction cutoff cannot explain the R ≈ 9 w3 frontier. conv46 is still formally required.**

Why I checked. The n_mesh / g_cutoff convergence of the frontier is established (swarm-l, swarm-z, swarm-ag). The interaction cutoff `qmax` is the one numerical parameter nobody has tested there. Only conv46 (`qmax` 10) tests it, and conv46 is queued behind the stalled r56. Agents cannot change it: `swarm check` rejects `qmax` ≠ 8 as a parent-fixed extra. The frontier blocks put kernel weight on stars up to |G|²/|G1|² = 21, and exponentiation spreads that weight further. The solver sums Q over the hexagon |i|, |j|, |i−j| ≤ qmax (`build_qgrid`), so this was worth bounding.

ED-free estimate (`work/qtail.py`, a diagnostic and not a result): A_Q(k,k′) = Σ_G |W_G(k,k′)| e^{−|Q + k − k′ − G|²/4}, an incoherent upper bound on the LL0 form factor built from `physics.kdep_envelope.kernel_coefficients`, with the block's own n_mesh and g_cutoff. Weight it by the screened-interaction shape (1 − e^{−2qD})/q with D = 0.1. Then take the fraction outside the qmax = 8 hexagon, relative to a qmax = 14 grid. Twelve random (k, k′) pairs:

| input | tail fraction, mean | max |
|---|---|---|
| static w3 parent | 7e−21 | 8e−20 |
| swarm-ap `20261008T104527.613Z_swarm-ap_req_8589` (s21 9.42 / r46 9.60) | 2.0e−3 | 3.9e−3 |

So the frontier moves the truncated interaction weight from round-off to a few parts per thousand. That is seventeen orders of magnitude more than the parent, but at most ~0.4 % of the pair interaction, with an unknown sign (the bound ignores phases). It cannot produce a ninefold gap. qmax 8 → 10 should change R by well under 1 % (the 024 pilot saw 0.03–0.3 % at smaller harmonic content). conv46 will measure it properly once r56 clears. I am not requesting anything.
