**Family `joint-pair-w3`: pair-resolved (non-separable) joint (G,R) orbits, parent w3.**

What is new: every separable term α·O_S(r)F_T(k) couples *all* directions of S with *all* directions of T equally. The physical C6 acts on joint (G,R) orbits, and for six-member stars that admits three independent couplings per (S,T) block, not one. I build them from inversion pairs {±G}, {±R} (pair functions √2cos, still real and inversion-symmetric, accepted by the block):

    γ ⊃ Σ_{i=0..2} c_d · O_{±G_i}(r) F_{±R_(i+d)}(k),   d = 0,1,2

with G_i, R_i ordered so C6 is the cyclic shift i→i+1 (checked in Cartesian, solver 120° basis, ADR-0035). For S1×T1: d=1 couples G ⟂ R; d=0 and d=2 couple G and R at +30° and −30°. The separable S1×T1 term is exactly c_0=c_1=c_2=α_star/3 (verified: identical M2^(k) in `swarm check`). So per (S,T) block I add two directions the atlas cannot reach: a "perpendicular vs oblique" direction (C6v-symmetric) and a **chiral** direction (c_0 − c_2, +30° vs −30°) that breaks mirror but keeps C6. The magnetic field already breaks mirror, so the chiral direction may have a nonzero linear response with either sign; it is the cheapest genuinely non-separable test.

Blocks: S1×T1, S3×T1 (S3 = w3's |G|²=4 shell, parallel to S1), S2×T1 (G ∥ R or at ±60°); later T2.

Bounds (pair-level coefficients): |c_d| ≤ 0.2 for α, ≤ 0.1 for β in phase 1; widen toward the campaign box (|α|≤0.6, |β|≤0.3) if winners hit the edge. n_mesh 48, g_cutoff 8 (same as swarm-c's atlas, so our numbers compare).

Method: phase 1, single-shift response: 3 blocks × 3 shifts × {α ±0.05, β ±0.03} = 36 evals. Phase 2: line searches along the best signed shift combinations, then a small CMA-ES (σ mixture, seeds stated then) in the ≤18-D C6-covariant pair space, ~100 evals. Phase 3: same on w2 for any positive direction, ~40 evals.

Expected sign of R_gap − 1: mostly negative, as in the atlas; I expect the chiral direction to be odd (one sign positive at Nk=21). Prior on finite-size robustness is low (pilot 024 reversals); the ladder decides.

Budget: ≤ 180 evaluations in this family.

Not duplicating swarm-c: no separable-star terms (I take those from atlas-T1-w3 via `swarm show`). Their planned spatial chiral sub-stars of the 12-member shells are a different (spatial-only) chirality; mine is the joint (G,R) chirality.
