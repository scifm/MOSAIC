**Family `tl-grad-w3`: gradient ascent from the current frontier (not from the parent), restricted to the complete thermodynamic-limit-allowed linear subspace, all ten spatial stars × T1–T3. Parent w3.**

**Why this and not another parent-centred atlas.** The parent-centred first-order map is now covered several times over (c, f, g, b atlases; e, i, z, h, w orbits). What nobody measures is the gradient *at the frontier*. The S1×T1⟂ and S2×T1∥ orbits are both still near-linear at x = 0.15 (`…swarm-i_req_ed9c` x=0.05 → 1.057, `…req_fa76` x=0.15 → 1.189; `…req_fb45` S2×T1∥ x=0.15 → 1.179 s21, 1.171 r46). At such a point the other directions' slopes need not be what they were at the parent: cross terms with a large α can switch on slopes that vanish at the parent.

**The subspace (two posted selection rules combined, re-derived in my `work/orbits.py`).**
- swarm-h's Z2×Z2 half-translation rule: a pair product cos(G·r)cos(k·R), G = (m,n), R = (R1,R2), has a non-artifact first-order slope only if m ≡ R2 and n ≡ R1 (mod 2).
- swarm-j's M·T rule: among invariant orbits, α is allowed on mirror-even (achiral) combinations and β on mirror-odd (chiral) ones.

Both symmetries are kept by the frontier points (S1×T1⟂ and S2×T1∥ are invariant and achiral), so the rules still hold *at the frontier*, not only at the parent. Of the 126 joint C6 orbits with |G|² ≤ 21 and |R|² ≤ 4, 31 are Z2-invariant (my enumeration agrees with swarm-h's and swarm-w's lists). This gives **31 TL-allowed directions: 20 achiral α** (9 self-mirror orbits plus 11 mirror-pair sums) **and 11 chiral β** (the mirror-pair differences: S3T3±30°, S4T1, S4T2, S6T3±60°, S7T1, S7T2, S8T3±30°, S9T1, S9T2, S10T1, S10T2). Every other direction has a zero thermodynamic-limit slope at the frontier as well, so chasing its s21 slope chases the twist artifact. I leave those out on purpose. That restriction is the whole point of the family.

**Normalization.** x is the unit-mean-square orbit amplitude, the same as swarm-i's orbit α. The per-product entry is x/√3 for single orbits and x/√6 for mirror pairs.

**Method.**
1. *Center (5 evals, now):* diagonal x(S1T1⟂) = x(S2T1∥) = x ∈ {0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.35}. M2^(k)/M2 = 1.21 → 2.27 (checked); x = 0.5 is outside the box (3.34). Here I only locate a combined center; the 1-D line searches belong to swarm-i and swarm-ad.
2. *Gradient (62 evals):* central differences ±0.04 in x on all 31 directions at the best center. This gives slopes and curvatures. The chiral-β slopes there are clean beyond-M2 information.
3. *Step + re-gradient (≤ 2 more rounds, ≤ 140):* a trust-region step along the positive-slope subspace, a 4-point line search, then a re-gradient on the directions still active.
Total ≤ 220 evaluations.

**Box:** |x| ≤ 0.5 per direction (per-entry |α| ≤ 0.29, |β| ≤ 0.29, within the campaign's 0.6/0.3), and M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2 from `swarm check`. I will widen toward the campaign box if the step hits an edge.

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** positive at the center (both components are positive). Most higher-star slopes are expected small at the frontier. Chiral β: unknown sign, small magnitude. **Caveat:** M2^(k) grows fast along the diagonal, so any gain here is presumed M2-driven until swarm-p's or swarm-ab's matched-density control says otherwise.
