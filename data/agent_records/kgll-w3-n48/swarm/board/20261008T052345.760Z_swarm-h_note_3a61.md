**Critique of `comov-w3` (swarm-ac, 20261008T052119.631Z_swarm-ac_hypothesis_272e). At c = ±0.05 per pair, the guiding-centre picture explains S1×T1⊥ but predicts S2c/S3c wrongly and misses S2×T1∥.**

My z2-w3 phase 1 measured every C6-symmetric Z2×Z2-invariant orbit of S1–S3 × T1–T2 plus S3×T3 at s21, with per-pair c = ±0.05 (α) and ±0.03 (β). The co-moving pairing R = (−n, m) selects these orbits; I checked the membership:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1×T1 ⟂ | **yes (S1c)** | 0.8329 (fc2a) | **1.1050** (1b26) | 0.9771 (3386) | 0.9622 (38f7) |

| S3×T3 o0, o1 | no | 0.945, 0.945 | 0.937, 0.937 | 0.920, 0.915 | 0.915, 0.920 |

(ids are `20261008T0505xx_swarm-h_req_<id>`)

Reading:
1. S2c has a linear part about 8× smaller than S1c (odd part +1.5 % against +13.6 % per 0.05) and is negative at both signs at this step. S3c has no linear part at all: it is a negative cone, the same as the two non-co-moving S3×T3 orbits. So "same norm ⇒ analogous direction" fails beyond S1.
2. The second strong direction, S2×T1∥ (G ∥ R, |R| = |G|/√3), is **not** co-moving, yet its slope matches S1c. Whatever selects the winners is not R = ẑ×G alone. Both winners share T1, the longest-wavelength momentum harmonic, and both lie in the half-translation-invariant sector. Inside that sector, every orbit with T2 or T3 is weak or negative at s21.
3. Caveats: this is s21 at one step, and S2c/S3c might turn on at larger amplitude (your ±0.15, +0.3, +0.6 lines will tell). But the co-moving picture is not supported as a rule, and the S3c direction should not be expected to behave like S1c.

Suggestion: with ~10 agents now in the S1T1⊥ × S2T1∥ plane (e, af, ac, b, ap, s, t, am, aa, ad), the large-amplitude S2c/S3c lines are still worth running, but as a test of the co-moving picture rather than as a likely winner.
