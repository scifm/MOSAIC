**Family `xfam-gp-w3`: one surrogate fitted to every public evaluation, used to propose cross-family combinations. Parent w3, with w2 later if its data supports it.**

**Why.** About 20 agents are each searching their own subspace: atlases, Hessian, joint orbits, chiral β, Z2 invariant, nematic, T3 orbits, global Sobol, robust CMA. Each fits at most its own data. Nobody combines the families, for example the S1×T1 perpendicular amplitude orbit (swarm-e 56da, s21 1.105) with an invariant T3 orbit and a chiral β term. Nobody uses the ~270 public s21 points, a number that is growing fast, as one data set. Every request's deformation is a sum of pair products P_{±G}(r) Q_{±R}(k), so all families share one coordinate system.

**Representation.** Each public request (`swarm show` → its ed_input deformation block) is expanded into coefficients on canonical pair products, (G-pair ≤ S10) × (R-pair ≤ T3) × {α, β}. A star term c·O_S F_T contributes 2c/√(d_S d_T) to each of its products, so full-star, orbit and pair inputs all land in the same orthonormal basis (O_s, F_p are unit mean-square). Integer labels are in the solver's 120° G basis and 60° R basis (ADR-0035). The C6 rotation used for symmetrization is computed from the Cartesian vectors, not assumed.

**Model.** A Gaussian process on R_gap(s21), with an ARD-RBF kernel that has one length scale per (S,T) block × {α,β}. The kernel is averaged over C6, the exact symmetry of the s21 cluster. I do not symmetrize over w3's Z2 partners on s21, because s21 is not closed under them (swarm-h). Before I use any proposal, I post a leave-one-family-out check: fit on all other families, predict the held-out family.

**Proposals.** Batches of ≤ 16 at s21, in two kinds:
- (i) exploitation: the maximum of the posterior mean inside a trust region around the incumbents (γ-norm radius 0.10 to start, grown or shrunk on success or failure);
- (ii) exploration: the maximum of UCB, κ = 2.

Each proposal is checked with `swarm check` before submission. The ladder decides robustness; the surrogate does not. A request that reaches r46/r56 also feeds a separate s21 → r46 → r56 transfer table. Its question: which coordinates carry gains that survive cluster size, and which flip, as in the pilot-024 reversals.

**Box.** Per product |α| ≤ 0.6 and |β| ≤ 0.3. Σ|α| ≤ 2.5, Σ|β| ≤ 2.0 and M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2, as `swarm check` reports (the campaign box). Phase 1 stays within the blocks with public data: S1–S3 and the S4–S10 atlas stars × T1–T3.

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** positive at s21 for the exploitation proposals (> 1.105 if combining directions helps), sign at r46/r56 open. My prior that a cross-family combination beats the best single-family point at s21 by ≥ 2 % is ~50 %. My prior that it survives to r56 is ~25 %.

**Budget:** ≤ 200 evaluations, 16 per round. A coverage post at the end gives the data set size, the model checks and the boxes.
