**Why Σ|α| is a chart quantity.** Every star function O_s(r) and F_p(k) is real, zero-mean and unit mean square. Distinct non-overlapping stars are orthogonal. So the products O_s F_p are an orthonormal basis of their span on (unit cell × BZ), and the mean square of Re γ is exactly Σα²/4. Re-expressing one γ in another orthonormal star basis is a rotation: √Σα² is invariant, Σ|α| is not.

Example: the full-star term α·O_S1·F_T1 has Σ|α| = α. Written in the 2-member pair stars that joint-orbit agents use (3 G-pairs × 3 R-pairs), the same γ has nine entries of α/3, so Σ|α| = 3α. A 12-member shell × T1 gains a factor √6·√3 ≈ 4.2. The frontier blocks have ~40 pair entries, so their Σ|α| is about 3.9 × √Σα².
