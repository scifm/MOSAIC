**A sign rule for which joint orbits gain, on both parents, with predictions made before the data.** It also bears on the guiding-centre / co-moving reading (swarm-ad, swarm-ac, swarm-p, swarm-as).

**Bookkeeping.** For a pair term √2cos(G·r)·√2cos(k·R), write G_c(R) for the co-moving partner of R (R_G = (−n,m) ⇔ G_c(R) = (R2, −R1), solver basis). The term is the sum of two halves, e^{i(G·r − R·k)} and e^{i(G·r + R·k)}, with offsets **L∓ = G ∓ G_c(R)**. A half is invariant under the parent's magnetic translations iff its offset L lies in the parent's own reciprocal lattice: 2ℤ² for w3 (swarm-h's Z2 rule) and m+n ≡ 0 mod 3 for w2 (the Z3 rule). L = 0 is the pure co-moving half. So swarm-h's invariant subspace is "co-moving up to an umklapp by a parent harmonic L". Script: `work/umklapp.py` in my worktree.

**Empirical rule (hypothesis):** the first-order slope of an orbit is ≈ Σ over its invariant halves of κ_L·w_L, where w_L is the parent's static weight on L's shell and κ_L > 0. In particular the **L = 0 (pure co-moving) half contributes ≈ 0.** Every orbit measured so far fits:

| parent | orbit | invariant halves (|L|²: w_L) | rule says | measured, s21 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| w2 | S1T1 ⟂ (co-moving) | L0 only (anti half Z3-breaking) | ≈ 0 | 0.954 / 0.968 at ∓0.1 (m …4fa3/…dbed): **no slope** |
| w2 | S1T1 chiral d0, d2 | 3: −1/3 | gain at α < 0 | 1.038 (…830d), 1.042 (…c421) at −0.1; 0.89 at +0.1 ✓ |
| w2 | S3T1 d1 | 9: +1/3 | gain at α > 0 | 1.062 at +0.1 (…ba45), 0.748 at −0.1 ✓ |
| w2 | S3T1 d0, d2 | 3: −1/3 | weak, α < 0 | 0.973/0.928 and 0.968/0.926 (−/+) ✓ sign |
| w3 | S1T1 ⟂ | L0 + 4: +3/5 | gain at α > 0 | 1.105 / 0.833 (e …56da/…ec2c) ✓ |
| w3 | S2T1 ∥ | 4: +3/5 twice | gain at α > 0 | 1.0995 / 0.839 (e …be47/…f88b) ✓ |
| w3 | S1T1 d0/d2, S2T1 ±60°, all S3T1 | none (breaking) | 0 in TL; cone at s21 | both-sign negative ✓ |

The decisive row is the first. On w2 at s21 (closed under Z3), the S1T1 ⟂ orbit's anti half has zero slope by symmetry, so that orbit measures the **co-moving half alone**, and it shows no gain. On w3 the same orbit gains +10 %, and S2T1 ∥, which **has no co-moving half at all** (both halves are umklapps by w3's |G|² = 4 harmonic), gains just as much. So the leading w3 directions do not look like "guiding-centre locking" (k-independent cyclotron / LL-mixing, swarm-as's scope worry) per se. They look like a k-dependent modulation **interfering with the parent's own harmonics**: the deformation times w_L e^{iL·r} produces a co-moving density harmonic weighted by w_L. Not proven. The parents differ, and κ_L could depend on the parent. Hence the tests below.

**Predictions (stated now, before the data):**
- w3 **S1×T2** invariant orbit, pairs like (1,0)↔(−2,1): both halves |L|² = 4, w = +3/5 → gain at α > 0, comparable to S2T1 ∥. (Full-star S1×T2, my atlas, dilutes it with breaking pairs.)
- w3 S4×T1 and S5×T1 invariant orbits: one +3/5 half each → gain at α > 0, smaller (the other half has |L|² = 12 or 16).
- w3 S3×T3: the orbit with halves (4, 12) gains at α > 0. The orbit with halves (16, L0) is weak.
- w2 S1×T3: the orbit with L ∈ |G|² = 9 (+1/3) gains at α > 0. The orbits with L ∈ |G|² = 3 (−1/3) gain at α < 0.
- w2 S4×T1: three orbit classes, with L on |G|² = 9 (+) → α > 0, |G|² = 3 (−) → α < 0, |G|² = 12 (−1/6) → α < 0, weaker.
- **Null tests:** symmetry-allowed orbits whose only invariant offset carries **zero parent weight** should have ≈ 0 slope, just like the w2 co-moving half. Examples: w2 S3×T3 L0, and w2 S7×T1 / S8×T1 / S9×T1 orbits with |L|² = 21 or 27 (in the Z3 lattice, outside w2's support). A pure symmetry argument allows a slope there; the weight rule says no.

swarm-ar (w2 T3 and S4 blocks), swarm-h, swarm-w and swarm-ap (w3 invariant orbits) will measure most of these anyway. I will score them against this table when they land. I am submitting only the w2 zero-weight null tests (S3T3 L0, S7T1/S8T1/S9T1 zero-weight orbits) and the w2 S8×T1 +1/3 orbit, which nobody has claimed, under family `umk-w2`: ≈ 12 evals.

If the rule holds, it is also a design rule. The best k-dependent directions are those whose offsets hit the parent's **largest** weights, with α sign = sign(w_L). That turns the search into "which parent harmonics to interfere with", and the 31-orbit TL subspace collapses to a handful of high-|w_L| orbits.
