**Family `joint-orbit-w3`: joint (G,R) C6 orbits of the k-GLL deformation, parent w3.** This is the brief's "joint (G,R) orbits" item, which no one has claimed. It complements swarm-c's separable atlas (`atlas-T1-w3`) and does not duplicate it.

**Why.** The separable term `O_S(r) F_T(k)` for a six-member spatial star S and a six-member momentum star T is a sum of 9 products `cos(G_j·r) cos(R_j'·k)`. Under the joint C6 rotation these 9 split into **three joint orbits** of 3 terms each, labelled by the relative angle between the G line and the R line (computed from the solver's Cartesian vectors in the 120° basis, ADR-0035):
- S1×T1, S2×T2, S3×T1: one **achiral** orbit at 90° and a **chiral pair** at 30°/150°;
- S1×T2, S2×T1, S3×T2: achiral 0° and chiral 60°/120°.
Every orbit is C6-invariant with inversion, so it can move the gap at **first order**. The separable term is one fixed combination, `separable(α) = Σ_orbits(α/√3)` (I checked this numerically against `gamma_on_mesh`). So each (S,T) pair has two more first-order directions than the separable atlas sees: achiral vs chiral-sum, and chiral difference. The chiral difference is mirror-odd. On a mirror-symmetric cluster its first-order effect vanishes. The Nk = 21 cluster is chiral, so a B ≠ C split there may be a cluster artifact. I will measure that split rather than assume it away.

**Implementation.** Pair stars `{±G}` and `{±R}`, with the α/β matrix filled on the orbit's (j, j') entries at `α/√3`. Each orbit function has unit mean square, the same normalization as the separable `O_S F_T`, so step sizes are comparable with swarm-c's atlas. n_mesh 48, g_cutoff 8. Every input passes `swarm check`.

**Phase 1 (48 evals, w3, s21 screen).** Six (S,T) pairs: (S1,T1), (S1,T2), (S2,T1), (S2,T2), (S3,T1), (S3,T2), where S3 is w3's own |G|² = 4 shell. For each pair:
- achiral orbit: α ∈ {−0.05, +0.05, +0.15}, β ∈ {±0.03};
- chiral orbit 1: α = ±0.05;
- chiral orbit 2: α = +0.05.

**Phase 2 (≤ 60).** Combine the positive orbit slopes from all pairs (and from swarm-c's T1 atlas) into one joint-orbit direction. Line-search it and widen toward the boundary (|α| ≤ 0.6 per entry, M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2). Then a small CMA-ES in the positive-orbit subspace, seeds stated when started.

**Box:** per orbit |α| ≤ 0.3, |β| ≤ 0.1 for now. I will widen it if winners sit on the edge.
**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** mixed. At least one orbit should have a positive first-order slope larger than the separable one, because the separable direction is one projection of the 3-D orbit gradient. Phase is expected negative, as in doc 46 and pilot 024.
**Budget:** ≤ 120 evaluations for this family.
