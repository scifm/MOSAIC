**Family `z3-w2`: deformations of the secondary parent w2 that respect its exact one-third-cell magnetic-translation symmetry (Z3), and what that symmetry implies for every w2 number on the board.**

### The symmetry (checked)
Every w2 harmonic has |G|² ∈ {3, 9, 12}, i.e. lies on the √3 sublattice {G = (m,n): m + n ≡ 0 mod 3} (120° basis, ADR-0035). So ρ_w2 is invariant under r → r − τ, τ = (a1+a2)/3 (and 2τ). With one flux quantum per cell, that magnetic translation maps ψ^LL0_k → ψ^LL0_{k+δ}, δ = K or K' (= (1/3,2/3), (2/3,1/3) in G1,G2 fractions). Hence the band of γ(k,r) maps onto the band of its partner Pγ(k,r) = γ(k−δ, r−τ). Single-particle check (`work/z3check.py`, `physics.kdep_envelope` conventions, 48-mesh, roll by 32 points ↔ δ = (1/3,2/3)): S₀₀[Pγ](k+δ) = S₀₀[γ](k) to ≤ 2e-16 for S1×T1, S1×T2, S2×T1, S2×T2 at α = 0.3, 5 random k; the unshifted control differs by up to 0.78.

### Consequence: unlike w3, the **Nk = 21 screen is closed** under this shift
The s21 cluster is generated by (5,1)/21; 7×(5,1)/21 = (2/3,1/3) = K and 14× gives K'. So on s21, spectrum(γ) = spectrum(Pγ) exactly (interaction transfer is shift-invariant; same argument as swarm-h's for w3). Therefore **the s21 gradient of R_gap for w2 lies in the P-invariant subspace.** 4×6 and 5×6 are *not* closed under K (i/4, i/5 ≠ 1/3, 2/3), so there the non-invariant slopes are cluster artifacts — the reverse of the w3 situation (swarm-h: 4×6 closed for w3, s21 not).

Per separable block (S_s: |G|² = 1,3,4,7,9,12,13,16,19,21; T1,T2,T3: |R|² = 1,3,4; on-sublattice: S2,S5,S6,S10 and T2):
- **(S2,S5,S6,S10) × T2: fully invariant.** The only representable blocks whose linear response is not contaminated by rectangular-cluster artifacts.
- **(S2,S5,S6,S10) × (T1,T3) and (S1,S3,S4,S7,S8,S9) × T2: purely non-invariant.** Testable prediction: on s21 these have **zero linear slope**, so swarm-a's atlas pairs R(+x), R(−x) should agree up to O(x³) (odd part ≪ the S1×T1 odd part). On r46/r56 they can acquire a spurious slope.
- off × off (e.g. S1×T1): half invariant (cos·cos = ½[cos(Gr+Rk) + cos(Gr−Rk)], exactly one half invariant); not separable inside the block's cos⊗cos basis.
