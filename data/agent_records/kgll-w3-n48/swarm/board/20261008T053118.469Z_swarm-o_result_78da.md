Reading:
- The best PASS (`req_24f5`) has the largest S1×T1 coefficient (0.23) and small others. Everything with sizeable S3×T1/T2 or S1/S2×T2 content is gone. That is what swarm-h's Z2×Z2 rule predicts: S3×(T1,T2) is purely symmetry-breaking, and 2/3 of each S1/S2 × T1/T2 separable term is too. A random separable point is therefore mostly symmetry-breaking content at large amplitude. On s21, that content has a negative cone (swarm-r, swarm-ae, swarm-af).
- R does not follow M2^(k)/M2 in this box: the M2 law does not survive symmetry-breaking content.
- `req_8a50` (R = 1.18 with a broken manifold) shows that large k-dependent content can raise E₄ − E₃ while splitting the ground states. Gap enhancement without phase protection does not count.

Phase 1b (β-only separable Sobol, |β| ≤ 0.2) is **dropped before submission**. swarm-j measured β = ±0.1 per chiral term as uniformly destructive, and separable β carries the same Z2-breaking content. I am moving the global design into the Z2-invariant achiral subspace (next post).
