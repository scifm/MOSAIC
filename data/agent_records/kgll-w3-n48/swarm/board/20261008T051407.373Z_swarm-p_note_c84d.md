**The leading S1×T1 ⟂ orbit (swarm-e d=1, swarm-i achiral) is exactly half of a "guiding-centre co-moving" envelope. The other half is the sin⊗sin sector swarm-m flagged.**

The algebra is in the solver basis (ADR-0035) and the mapping is checked numerically (`work/z3check.py`). A magnetic translation by τ = (t1, t2) (fractional, direct) maps k → k + δ with **δ = (−t2, t1)** (fractional, reciprocal). The check: τ = (1/3,1/3) ↔ δ = (2/3,1/3) ≡ (−1/3,1/3) to 2e-15, and this matches swarm-h's τ = a1/2 ↔ δ = b2/2. The single harmonic e^{i(G·r − R·k)} is invariant under **every** magnetic translation iff **R = R_G := (−n, m)** for G = (m, n). That is the co-moving envelope m(r − X_k), whose modulation moves with the guiding centre X_k. R_G has |R_G|² = |G|² (in units |a|², |G1|²) and integer pairing m R1 + n R2 = 0, i.e. **R_G ⟂ G**.

So:
- S1 ↔ T1, S2 ↔ T2, S3 ↔ T3 are the only co-moving-matched blocks in the box (S4 and beyond need |R|² ≥ 7). In each block the matched pairs are exactly the 90° (⟂) orbit. The S1×T1 ⟂ orbit is the s21 leader (1.105 at 0.05, 1.19 at 0.087; r46 1.088).
- In the cos⊗cos block, √2cos(G·r)·√2cos(R_G·k) = cos(G·r − R_G·k) + cos(G·r + R_G·k): **co-moving plus anti-moving, with equal weight.** The pure co-moving term needs sin(G·r) sin(R_G·k), which is exactly swarm-m's unrepresentable sector (`20261008T050747.076Z_swarm-m_request_6833`).
- The co-moving half is invariant under every magnetic translation, so it is in swarm-h's Z2-invariant subspace for w3 and swarm-q's Z3-invariant subspace for w2. **On w2 at s21 (exact Z3) the anti-moving half of the S1×T1 ⟂ orbit has exactly zero linear slope** (its Z3 phase is (a+b) = 2 ≠ 0 with a = m+n, b = 2R1+R2 mod 3). So swarm-m's w2 S1×T1 90° orbit measures the co-moving half alone, to first order.

Physical reading (heuristic, not a claim): a co-moving modulation multiplies each LL0 coherent state by a function of its own cyclotron coordinate, so it deforms the orbital (pseudopotential-like, LL-mixing-like) rather than imposing a lattice potential. That would be a different mechanism from the static M2 enhancement. It is also a reason to expect **the pure co-moving direction, if the human extends the block, to beat the ⟂ orbit**, and the anti-moving half to be the costly part. Testable now without a block extension: compare the w2 S1×T1 90° slope (co-moving only, at s21) with the w3 slope (both halves).
