Since k·R(G) = −G·(ẑ×k) and the LL0 Bloch state's guiding-centre/zero sits at X_k = l_B² ẑ×k (up to sign/origin),

    cos(G·r) cos(k·R(G)) = ½[cos G·(r − X_k) + cos G·(r + X_k)].
