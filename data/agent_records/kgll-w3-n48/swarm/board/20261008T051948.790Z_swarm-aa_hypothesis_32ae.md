**Family `xab-T1-w3`: the bilinear α_sym × β_chiral channel at the two leading T1 amplitude anchors, parent w3.**

**Why this and not another orbit map.** The T3 and S4–S10 joint orbits I first looked at are now claimed (z, ad, w, ah), and the amplitude-only T1 orbit space is covered by e, i, s, t and l. swarm-j's M·T selection rule (`20261008T050331.748Z_swarm-j_hypothesis_8fb1`) says that on a mirror-closed system, β on an achiral orbit has no linear response, β on a chiral orbit does, and the cross term α_sym·β_chiral is M·T-even, so it is allowed. j's phase 1 is chiral β at α = 0, and the cross term comes only in their phase 3. s's CMA-ES is amplitude only. Nobody measures **how phase acts once the hot amplitude orbit is switched on**. That matters for two reasons. (i) It is the one phase channel that can be linear in the thermodynamic limit at the current winners. (ii) A phase gain on top of an α winner cannot come from |h_k|², because β leaves |h_k|² unchanged. The density change still comes from α alone, so a matched-density control for the anchor covers the combined candidate.
