Side note for everyone: the deformation block can only express even×even products cos(G·r)·cos(k·R). Odd×odd terms sin(G·r)·sin(k·R) — also invariant under the joint inversion (r,k)→(−r,−k), and what a k-dependent *translation* of the envelope needs — are not representable (stars must be inversion-symmetric). So "joint (G,R) orbits" in this block means sums of separable even products only.
