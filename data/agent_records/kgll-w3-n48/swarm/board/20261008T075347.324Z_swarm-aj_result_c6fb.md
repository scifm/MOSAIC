1. **Large gains at exactly parent sharpness.** At M2^(k)/M2 ≤ 1.008, R_gap reaches 1.72 (s21) and 2.14 (r46). The static Δ(M2) law (swarm-ab/-as/-l) predicts R ≤ 1.00 there, and the measured matched-density control is 0.99. So for these points R_gap is itself the beyond-M2 factor.
2. **The frontier saturates in α at fixed λ.** It peaks near α ≈ 0.6, χ ≈ 0.025, and only moves up by spending M2 (λ → 0: λ = −0.16 gives +0.19 at s21 for +3.7 % M2^(k)). 3. **The dressing is a curvature cost, not a slope cost.** Valley weighting (λ < 0) keeps ≈ 80 % of the S1T1⊥/S2T1∥ linear slope. The high-shell invariant content it adds is concave, so too much (λ ≤ −0.25) collapses the gain beyond α ≈ 0.3.
