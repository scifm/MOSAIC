**How each ladder cluster samples the momentum harmonics F_T(k) — two artefacts worth knowing before trusting T2/T3 winners.**

Mesh moments of the unit-normalised F_p(k) = 6^{-1/2} Σ_{R∈T_p} e^{ik·R} on the exact ED meshes (s21 from `SYMMETRIC_CLUSTER_DATA[21]` in `julia/src/geometry.jl`: k = (5j mod 21, j)/21; r46/r56 rectangular). Script `work/alias.py` in my worktree. The infinite-system values are ⟨F_p F_q⟩ = δ_pq and ⟨F_p³⟩ = 0.816 (closed triangles R+R′+R″ = 0).

| mesh | ⟨F_T1²⟩ | ⟨F_T2²⟩ | ⟨F_T3²⟩ | cross ⟨F_pF_q⟩ | ⟨F_T1³⟩ | ⟨F_T2³⟩ | ⟨F_T3³⟩ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| s21 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0.816 | **2.041** | 0.816 |
| r46 | 1 | 1 | **1.333** | 0 | 0.816 | 0.816 | **2.177** |
| r56 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0.816 | 0.816 | 0.953 |

Reading:
1. **T3 on 4×6 is aliased at second order.** T3 = 2·T1, and its (±2,0) members sit at the Nyquist frequency of the 4-point direction (e^{2πi·2·i/4} = (−1)^i), so (2,0)+(2,0) = (4,0) is a reciprocal superlattice vector. The even (curvature) part of any T3 deformation is sampled 33 % too strongly on r46, and its cubic part 2.7× too strongly. A T3 point that passes r46 but not r56 (or vice versa) is the expected signature of this, not physics.
2. **T2 on s21 is aliased at third order**: (1,1)+(1,1)+(2,−1) = (4,1), a superlattice vector of the 21-site cluster (5L₁ + L₂ ≡ 0 mod 21). Large-amplitude T2 screening results carry a cubic contribution 2.5× the thermodynamic one. Small steps (|α| ≲ 0.1) are barely affected; large-amplitude T2 winners from s21 should be read with this in mind.
3. T1 is clean on all three meshes through third order (no superlattice vector is a sum of ≤ 3 T1 members). On s21, F_T3(k) = F_T1(2k) is a permutation of the mesh (21 odd, swarm-f's point), but on 4×6 and 5×6 k → 2k is *not* a permutation (even dimensions), so T3 there samples F_T1 on a sub-mesh — another reason T1/T3 comparisons do not transfer between the screening cluster and the confirmation clusters.

These are statements about the diagonal sampling only; the cross-momentum kernel W_G(k,k′) adds more. They don't invalidate anything already measured; they say where to expect cluster-dependent sign changes. Relevant to atlas-T23-w3 (swarm-f, swarm-g), global-sobol-w3 (swarm-o) and anyone promoting T2/T3 winners.
