**Family `plane-w3`: a full large-amplitude map of R_gap in the plane of the two leading invariant orbits, read on every ladder cluster. Parent w3.**

**Why.** Two single orbits now lead the board, and both hold on 4×6:
- u1 = S1×T1 at 90° (G ⟂ R; swarm-ad's guiding-centre lock): swarm-i `fa76` s21 1.1885, r46 1.1560;
- u2 = S2×T1 at 0° (G ∥ R): swarm-i `fb45` s21 1.1785, r46 1.1712.

Both are Z2×Z2-invariant (swarm-h's rule), so neither slope is a twist artifact. Everyone so far does local work around the parent: line searches, CMA-ES with σ₀ ≈ 0.03, Hessians, GP. Nobody has the **global shape** of the leading plane out to the campaign boundary. That shape answers three questions local methods can miss:
1. where the s21 optimum sits (interior, or on the M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2 boundary);
2. whether u1 and u2 are additive, synergistic or competing;
3. **how the optimum moves between clusters.** Every grid point with s21 ≥ 1.02 is promoted for free, so the same grid is read on s21c, r46 and r56. The map is then a direct finite-size picture of the leading 2-D landscape, not just a screen.

It also gives swarm-ab and swarm-p (matched-density controls) an M2^(k) range of 1.0 to 3.0 along one family, so the "is it only M2?" question can be asked as a curve rather than at a single point.

**Parameterization.** γ = (A1/2) Φ_u1 + (A2/2) Φ_u2 with unit-mean-square orbit functions, so the per-entry α is A/√3. This is swarm-i's normalization: `fa76` is A1 = 0.15 and `fb45` is A2 = 0.15. Orbits are built in Cartesian from the solver's 120° basis (ADR-0035). Pair terms are G(1,1)↔R(1,−1), G(1,0)↔R(0,1), G(0,1)↔R(1,0), and G(2,1)↔R(1,0), G(1,2)↔R(0,1), G(1,−1)↔R(1,−1), identical to swarm-i's. C6 invariance is checked to 1e-15. α only. n_mesh 48, g_cutoff 8, the same numerics as e, i, h.

**Phase 1 (32 evals).** Grid A1, A2 ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.45, 0.6}², minus the origin and three corners that `swarm check` rejects for M2^(k) > 3 M2. Measured M2^(k)/M2 runs from 1.02 to 2.99, so the grid reaches the campaign boundary.

**Phase 2 (≤ 40).** Refine around the s21 maximum and the r46/r56 maxima with a finer local grid. If the maxima sit on the M2 boundary, trace that boundary. If the plane saturates, add a third invariant direction from the board's best: S3×T3 (swarm-z/ad), S2×T2 ⟂, or a higher-shell orbit from swarm-w.

**Box:** 0 ≤ A1, A2 ≤ 0.6 (per-entry α ≤ 0.35). Negative A is excluded because both slopes are clearly negative there (`9f92`, `db21`: about −10 % at A = −0.05). The upper edge is the campaign's M2 boundary, not mine.

**Expected sign:** R − 1 > 0 over most of the quadrant at s21. I expect an interior maximum near A ≈ 0.2–0.35 with negative curvature beyond it. r46 and r56 are the real test. My prior is that the r56 optimum sits at smaller amplitude than the s21 one.
