**Family `cplx-dress-w3`: amplitude and phase together. Phase (β) dressing of the leading amplitude winner, the S1×T1 ⟂ (guiding-centre-locked) orbit. Parent w3.**

**Why this and not another atlas.** The low-shell α space is covered several times over (b, c, d, e, f, g, i, l, o, s, ad), and the high-shell invariant α orbits now have two agents (w, ah). Phase is covered only at α = 0 (j: chiral β; atlases: ±β on full stars). The brief lists "amplitude and phase together" explicitly, and no one measures how phase behaves *at* the winner. By swarm-j's M·T rule, the winner (achiral, Z2-invariant α) keeps C6, M·T and w3's half-cell Z2. So at the winner:
- β on the **same** ⟂ orbit is M·T-odd, so R(β) = R(−β) in the thermodynamic limit. It is pure curvature, and I expect it to be negative.
- β on a **mirror-odd (chiral-difference), Z2-invariant** joint orbit is M·T-even, so its linear term is allowed. Its slope at the winner is j's α = 0 slope plus a bilinear α_⟂·β_chiral cross term, which nobody measures. If the cross term is positive, phase adds a beyond-M2 piece on top of the amplitude winner.

**Directions (enumerated in `work/orbits.py`, 120° basis, ADR-0035).** 126 C6 joint (G,R) orbits in the box, 31 of them Z2-invariant (m ≡ R2, n ≡ R1 mod 2, as in swarm-h). Every invariant chiral mirror pair gives one mirror-odd direction (orbit_a − orbit_b)/√2: S3×T3 (30°), S4×T1 (49.1°), S4×T2 (40.9°), S6×T3 (60°), S7×T1 (16.1°), S7×T2 (73.9°), S8×T3 (30°), S9×T1 (66.6°), S9×T2 (23.4°), S10×T1 (40.9°), S10×T2 (49.1°). That is 11 directions. Each orbit has unit mean square (c/√3 per pair term, the normalization swarm-i uses). The base point is α_⟂ = 0.15, the same envelope as `20261008T050312.367Z_swarm-i_req_fa76` (s21 1.1885).

**Phase 1 (25 evals, s21 screen):** β_⟂ ∈ {+0.05, −0.05, +0.10}, and the 11 chiral directions at β = ±0.10. The odd part gives the slope at the winner and the even part the curvature. I take α = 0 slopes from j and w where they exist, and fill the gaps (≤ 11 evals) only for directions that show a signal.
**Phase 2 (≤ 40):** joint line search in (α_⟂, β_best). If i's line search moves the α optimum, I rebase there.
**Box:** |β| per direction ≤ 0.3, α_⟂ ≤ 0.6 (normalized). Well inside the campaign box.
**Expected sign:** β_⟂ negative (curvature). Chiral: one sign positive at first order, probably small (≤ 1–2 %), largest for S3×T3 and S4.
