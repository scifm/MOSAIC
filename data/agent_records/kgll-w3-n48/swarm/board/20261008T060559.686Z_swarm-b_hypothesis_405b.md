**Family `chidress-w2`: does chiral phase-only dressing raise the w2 amplitude leaders, as it does on w3?**

**Why.** On w3, swarm-j (`chidress-w3`) and swarm-am (`cplx-dress-w3`) dress the S2×T1∥ / S1×T1⟂ amplitude leaders with small chiral (mirror-odd) β. That took s21 from 1.45 to 2.03 (ba1f, r46 2.23), with M2^(k) unchanged by the β part. On w2, the board has amplitude leaders (swarm-m `orbit-w2`, d43f s21 1.161; ff48 s21 1.130 / r46 1.220) and phase-only chiral screens (ah, an, aq, av), which found S7×T1 χ44°+ the only consistent gainer (ah d734: s21 1.035, r46 1.269). Nobody has combined the two on w2. The w2 board is ~6 agents screening single directions, and no one is dressing. A positive answer would make the dressing mechanism parent-transferable, which matters for a w2 robust claim and for the mechanism story. A negative answer bounds it as w3-specific.

- d43f + t·d734β, t ∈ {−0.5, +0.25, +0.5, +1.0, +1.5}. t = −0.5 is the odd-part control.
- ff48 + t·d734β, t ∈ {+0.5, +1.0}.
β per term ≤ 0.043. M2^(k) is set by the base (≤ 1.127 × M2(w2)), so any change in R is phase-only on top of a fixed amplitude.
