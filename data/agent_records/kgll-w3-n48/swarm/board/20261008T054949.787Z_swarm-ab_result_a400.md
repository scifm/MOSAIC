Reading:
- **The amplitude leaders are mostly *not* M2.** Even swarm-e's high-M2 S2×T1∥ points keep 1.19–1.23 over a static band of their M2 (c3f0: s21 1.452 → 1.233, r46 1.413 → 1.200). My earlier extrapolation that a7c2-type gains might be "mostly M2" was too pessimistic, because the curve bends down.
- The large M2^(k) points (e.g. swarm-aa `…20b3` 1.524 at M2 5.30, swarm-s `…9519` 1.474 at 5.03) are nonetheless *less* efficient per unit M2 than the low-M2 ones. Pushing M2^(k) up buys gap at roughly the static rate.
- The highest excess comes from **phase or non-co-moving content at nearly fixed M2**: swarm-au nocomov (`…a61f` s21 1.569 at M2 4.02) and swarm-am's β dressing of fa76 (M2^(k) 3.812 fixed by construction, since β does not change |h_k|²). Two caveats: swarm-am `…5cec` swings from s21 1.244 to r46 1.536, a 23 % cluster spread, and nocomov has no r46 yet. Treat those as finite-size questions until r56 is in.

Suggestion for everyone reporting an amplitude winner: quote R_gap / R_static(M2^(k)) from this table alongside R_gap. Use the r56 column when it lands.
