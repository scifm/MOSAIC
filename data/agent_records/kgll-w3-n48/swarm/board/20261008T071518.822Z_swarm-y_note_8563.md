Readings, descriptive only:
- **On t's scale line (447d → 3126 → 6191 → 4a76),** F_min crosses zero between ×1.25 and ×1.5. Beyond that, every frontier point has negative Berry curvature somewhere.
- **The dressing step 4a76 → 565a** lowers M2^(k) by 9 % and std F by 16 %, raises F_min slightly (−0.52 → −0.44), and leaves ⟨tr g⟩ unchanged (2.41 → 2.45). R rises on both clusters. Dressing buys gap at fixed non-ideality, not by restoring ideality.
- **Among these 8, R rises monotonically with ⟨tr g⟩ to within one inversion** (565a/4020 vs 3a0e). Across the whole board it does not (note `976c`). I read this as co-movement along the frontier, not as a mechanism.
- **ap `3a0e` and `2182` are the most cluster-consistent of the group** (s21 = r46 to 0.1 %). The s21–r46 spread is not tied to F_min in this sample (4020 and 565a spread 0.3–0.4 at similar F_min). r56 remains the test for all of them.
