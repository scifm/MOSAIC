**Family `cyc-mech`: is the leading ⟂-orbit gain a cyclotron-orbital (pseudopotential, LL-mixing-type) effect? Uniform-LL0 controls of the top orbits, plus the exact algebra behind swarm-p's co-moving note.**

**Algebra (exact, one flux quantum per moiré cell; notes App. A conventions).** Write r = X + η (guiding centre + cyclotron). Then e^{iG·r} = e^{iG·X} D_G(η), and for flux 1 per cell e^{iG·X} is the magnetic translation by the *lattice vector* R_G = l_B² ẑ×G, which acts on a Bloch state as a phase: e^{iG·X} ψ_k = ±e^{ik·R_G} ψ_k. Hence

    e^{iG·r} ψ^LL0_k = ± e^{ik·R_G} D_G(η) ψ^LL0_k ,

and the **co-moving harmonic e^{i(G·r − R_G·k)} acting on LL0 is the k-independent cyclotron operator D_G(η)**. It doesn't touch the guiding centre, so it commutes with every magnetic translation. That's why swarm-p found it numerically invariant (`20261008T051407.373Z_swarm-p_note_c84d`, R_G = (−n, m) in ADR-0035 labels). Consequences:
1. A co-moving envelope gives every k the same orbital φ = F(η)|0⟩: a **k-independent Landau-level mixing**. A C6-symmetric star sum projects onto cyclotron angular momentum ≡ 0 mod 6, so it mixes in |6⟩, |12⟩, …; with |G1|²l_B² = 4π/√3 the coherent-state weight is not small. Its density is uniform on a uniform base, and its only many-body effect is a C6-anisotropic change of the pair form factor |⟨φ|D_q|φ⟩|², i.e. of the pseudopotentials.
2. The S1×T1 ⟂ orbit (fa76, 56da, be47) is half co-moving, half anti-moving. The anti-moving half is e^{2ik·R_G} D_G(η), a k-dependent orbital.
3. More generally, on a uniform base any envelope is a k-dependent cyclotron orbital Σ_G c_G(k) e^{ik·R_G} D_G(η)|0⟩. The static w3 parent is the k-dependent case. The co-moving sector is the one piece that is pure orbital/pseudopotential physics with no density or Berry-curvature modulation.

**Why it matters for claim 2 (beyond-M2).** If the ⟂-orbit gain is mostly the co-moving half, then the mechanism is "LL mixing into n ≡ 0 mod 6 via the envelope". The brief pins LL mixing (a_n = (1,0,0)), so that is a scope question for the human (separate `request`). Matched-density static controls (swarm-p, swarm-ab) cannot detect this: the co-moving half changes no density at all.
