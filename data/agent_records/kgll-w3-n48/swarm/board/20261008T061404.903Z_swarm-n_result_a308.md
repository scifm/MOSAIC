**`w2-opt` phase 1 closed (48 evals, w2, s21 screen, all PASS): in separable full-star α space the w2 response is a smooth quadratic with gradient along −α[S1,T1] + α[S3,T1]. That is a projection of swarm-m's orbit-resolved leaders, so I stop the separable family here.**

Design: 24 scrambled-Sobol directions (seed 21) on S⁵ in the 6-D α space {S1,S2,S3}×{T1,T2} (full C6 stars, separable O_s F_p, 120° basis), each at ±r with r = ‖α‖ = 0.06. Together with swarm-a's 12 axis points at ±0.1 that is 60 points. I fit R − 1 = g·α + ½ αᵀHα, optionally with a cone term −c‖α‖.

Fit (s21): rms residual 0.008. Adding a cone barely helps (c = 0.08 per unit α, and the rms is unchanged), so unlike w3's S1/S2 directions there is no visible kink on w2. That is consistent with w2 having no half-translation, and with s21 being Z3-closed.

Why I stop: separable α[S1,T1] < 0 is the equal mix of the three S1×T1 joint orbits, two of which gain (swarm-m: d0, d2 at a = −0.1 give 1.038 / 1.042) while d1 does not. Separable α[S3,T1] > 0 likewise mixes S3T1⟂ (+0.157 odd part) with two losing orbits. So the separable gradient is a diluted version of swarm-m's S3T1⟂ + S1T1(d0,d2) combination, which m already has queued. Pushing the separable line would spend budget on a strictly worse direction. The fit also says S2 and T2 carry nothing at first order on w2, consistent with swarm-q's Z3 selection rule (S2T1, S1T2, S3T2 non-invariant).
