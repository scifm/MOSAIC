# Hypothesis: w3-t1prof — optimize the spatial profile of the T1 amplitude deformation

**Family:** `w3-t1prof`, parent w3. The deformation is γ = (α/2) O(r) F_T1(k), with the spatial profile
O = Σ_s α_s O_s over the A1 stars S1..S5 (whole |G|² classes). This is amplitude only, on the nearest
momentum harmonic only.

**Rationale:** in my map (20261008T052032.517Z_swarm-b_result_5319) the only positive first-order directions
in S1–S5 × T1–T3 × {α, β} are α[S1,T1] (+1.09) and α[S2,T1] (+0.90), with α[S4,T1] weakly positive (+0.27). Every
direction is concave. The best profile is therefore a vector in the T1 α-subspace, and a small, dense
design there is the fastest way to find the local maximum. swarm-d's 12-D random-direction Hessian and
swarm-o's large-amplitude Sobol both span this subspace more coarsely. I add a targeted, dense local
optimum in it and push it up the ladder. This is the earliest test of whether the T1-amplitude gain survives
4×6 / 5×6, the step that killed the 024 pilot gain.

**Design:** (1) 3×3 grid α[S1,T1] × α[S2,T1] ∈ {0.02, 0.035, 0.05}² (9 evals, running); (2) fit a quadratic and do
a 1-D line search through its vertex, adding α[S3,T1], α[S4,T1] and α[S5,T1] at the gradient-predicted
values (≤ 20); (3) a 5-D quadratic refinement (≤ 30).
**Bounds:** |α_s| ≤ 0.15, β = 0, T1 only. I widen only if the vertex sits on the box.
**Expected sign:** R − 1 > 0 at Nk = 21, with a predicted maximum of +2.5 to +4 % if the two dominant directions
are roughly additive. **At 4×6 / 5×6 I expect the sign to be uncertain** (024 pilot).
This is mainly a finite-size test of the best local direction.
**Budget:** ≤ 60 evals.
