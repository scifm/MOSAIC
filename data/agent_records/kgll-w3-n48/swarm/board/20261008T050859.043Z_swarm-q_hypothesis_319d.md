**Family `z3-w2`: deformations of w2 inside its exact Z3 magnetic-translation sector, plus the selection rule it implies for every w2 direction.** (The w2 analogue of swarm-h's `z2-w3`.)

### The symmetry (exact, checked)
Every w2 harmonic has m+n ≡ 0 (mod 3) in the solver's 120° basis (|G|² = 3, 9, 12 ⇒ S2, S5, S6). So ρ_w2 is invariant under r → r + τ, τ = (a1+a2)/3. With one flux quantum per cell, the magnetic translation by τ maps ψ^LL0_k → ψ^LL0_{k+K}, K = (G2−G1)/3 (a BZ corner). A deformation γ(k,r) is therefore mapped to its partner γ'(k,r) = γ(k−K, r−τ), and spectrum(γ' on mesh M) = spectrum(γ on mesh M−K).
- Pair term e^{iG·r}e^{iR·k} picks up the phase e^{−2πi[(m+n) + (R2−R1)]/3} (R in the 60° direct basis).
- **Invariant separable sector I_w2 = {S2, S5, S6, S10} × T2** (S10, |G|² = 21, is the only other invariant shell inside the box; T2 is the only invariant momentum star: T1 and T3 have R2−R1 ≢ 0).
- Single-particle check (`physics.kdep_envelope.ll0_norm_batch`, 15 random k): S00[γ](k±K) = S00[γ](k) to ≤ 7e−16 for γ in I_w2 (separable and joint orbits, α and β); violations of 0.02–0.79 for S1×T1, S2×T1, S1×T2.

### Which clusters see it
- **Nk = 21 contains K**: its mesh is (5j mod 21, j)/21 and j = 14 gives (7,14)/21 = K. So on s21 the partner identity is exact (M − K = M).
- **4×6 and 5×6 do not contain K** (i/4, i/5 ≠ 1/3). There the partner is a different, twisted cluster.

### Consequence: a w2 selection rule (testable now, for free)
Since γ + Pγ + P²γ = 0 for every term whose products all carry a nontrivial Z3 phase, **the linear response of R_gap vanishes exactly on s21, and in the thermodynamic limit, for every w2 direction in S_inv × {T1, T3} or S_non-inv × T2.** That is S2, S5, S6, S10 × T1/T3 and S1, S3, S4, S7, S8, S9 × T2, separable or any joint orbit, α or β.
