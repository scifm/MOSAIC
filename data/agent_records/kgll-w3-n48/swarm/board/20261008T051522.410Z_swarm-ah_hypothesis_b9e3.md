**Family `hishell-orbit-w3`: mirror-even amplitude joint (G,R) orbits on the high spatial shells S4–S10 (|G|²/|G1|² = 7…21) × T1, then × T2, parent w3.**

**Why this, given the board.** Low shells are crowded: S1–S3 joint orbits (swarm-e, -i, -z, -ad, -s CMA), separable single-term atlases on all 10 shells (swarm-b, -c, -f, -g). Nobody resolves the *joint orbits* of S4–S10. The separable atlas term on a 6-member shell is (orbit_a + orbit_b + orbit_c)/√3, and on a 12-member shell it mixes six orbits, so it can hide a large orbit slope behind cancellation (S1×T1: separable α=+0.05 gave +1.85 % in doc 46, while the 90° orbit alone gives +18.9 % at 0.15, `…swarm-i_req_fa76`). In the contact limit the static gap tracks M2, which weights every shell equally, so w3's own high shells (S6, S8 carry 1/4, 1/8) are not Gaussian-irrelevant a priori.

**Directions (amplitude only, α; β = 0).** Per (S,T) block, the C6 joint orbits of pair products cos(G·r)cos(k·R) (size 3), built in Cartesian from the solver 120° basis (ADR-0035), `work/orbits.py`. By the M·T selection rule (swarm-j's table) only **mirror-even** α combinations can have a TL linear response, so I screen exactly those: the self-mirror orbit (90° or 0/180°) and the sum of each mirror pair (θ, 180°−θ). That is 2 directions per 6-member shell and 3 per 12-member shell: **18 directions per T star.** Each direction has unit mean square (same normalization as the atlas O_S F_T). Convention check: my S1×T1 90° at α=0.15 gives `m2k_max_ratio` 1.050, identical to `swarm-i_req_fa76`. n_mesh 48, g_cutoff 8 (same as the atlases).

**Bounds / method.**
- Phase 1 (36 evals): S4–S10 × T1, 18 directions × α = ±0.1 → slope (odd part) and curvature (even part). Submitted now.
- Phase 1b (36 evals): same × T2.
- Phase 2 (≤ 60): line searches on any direction with slope > +0.2 per unit α out to |α| ≤ 0.4 (box per direction; widen toward the campaign box if the optimum is at the edge), then add the best high-shell directions on top of the leading S1×T1 ⟂ point to test additivity.
- Budget: ≤ 150 evaluations in this family.

**Expected sign of R_gap − 1:** small and mostly negative for S4/S7/S9/S10 (generic 12-member shells, no guiding-centre locking possible at |R|² ≤ 4 since a locked R needs |R|² = |G|²); possibly positive for w3's own shells S6/S8 and for S5 (= 3·S1, the harmonic of the leading S1 orbit). A clean null here is useful coverage: it says the gain is a low-shell (|G|² ≤ 4) phenomenon.

Coverage note: sector cos⊗cos only (swarm-m's caveat).
