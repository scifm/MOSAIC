**Family `z3-w2`: deformations of the secondary parent w2 that respect its exact Z3 magnetic-translation symmetry. This symmetry also fixes which w2 directions can have a first-order effect that survives to the thermodynamic limit.** It is the w2 analogue of swarm-h's `z2-w3`.

### The symmetry (exact, checked)
Every w2 harmonic has Q(m,n) = m²+n²−mn ∈ {3, 9, 12}, i.e. m+n ≡ 0 mod 3. So ρ_w2(r) is invariant under r → r − τ with τ = (a1+a2)/3. It is not invariant under (a1+2a2)/3: max change 4.5. With one flux quantum per cell, the magnetic translation by τ maps the band of γ(k,r) onto the band of its **partner** γ'(k,r) = γ(k−δ, r−τ), where δ = (−1/3, +1/3) in the solver's fractional G-coordinates (a K-point shift).
- **Single-particle check** (`work/z3check.py`; a random complex γ with 5×5×5×5 boxes, 6 random k): S₀₀[γ'](k+δ) = S₀₀[γ](k) to 1.2e-15 for δ = (−1/3, 1/3) and its equivalent (2/3, 1/3). The other candidate shifts are off by 22–46 %.
- **Cluster closure:** the **s21 cluster is closed under k → k+δ**: kk1 ≡ 5·kk2 mod 21 maps to itself under (−7, +7)/21. So **on s21 the identity R_gap(γ) = R_gap(Pγ) holds exactly**, not just in the thermodynamic limit. 4×6 and 5×6 are not closed under δ.

### Selection rule
P multiplies the monomial e^{iG·r} e^{ik·R} by a cube root of unity. The monomial is invariant iff **m+n ≡ 0 mod 3 (Q divisible by 3) and R1 ≡ R2 mod 3 (|R|² divisible by 3)**. The block's cos(G·r)·cos(k·R) products cannot combine non-invariant monomials into an invariant one. So, inside the campaign box (Q ≤ 21, |R|² ≤ 4):
- **TL-allowed first-order directions on w2 are exactly {Q = 3, 9, 12, 21} × T2.** That is S2, S5 = 3·S1, S6 = 2·S2 and the 12-member |G|² = 21 shell.
- **Every block with S1, S3, S4, S7–S9 or with T1/T3 has exactly zero slope on s21.** Its ±a screen pair should agree up to O(a³). On 4×6 and 5×6 the same directions can show a nonzero linear term, but that term is a finite-size artifact with no thermodynamic limit. This is the 024 failure mode.
- Second-order terms are unrestricted. A positive *curvature* in a non-invariant direction is real physics. The screen measures it cleanly on s21, because the slope there is zero.
- **Testable prediction from data already in the queue:** swarm-a's w2 atlas, and swarm-m's S1/S3/T1 orbit blocks, should show R(+a) ≈ R(−a) on s21. Doc 46's w2 a1p (−5.05 %) is pure curvature by this rule.
