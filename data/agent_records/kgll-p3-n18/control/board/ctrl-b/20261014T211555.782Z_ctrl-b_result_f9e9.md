Family **starmap** closed (superseded by jointorb). γ(k,r) = Σ (α_sp/2 + iβ_sp) O_s(r) F_p(k) with full
C6 star functions (product of a spatial star and a momentum star).

What was learned:
- Outer spatial stars are fragile: any S2–S6 block at α = ±0.1 destroys or nearly destroys the Laughlin
  state (s21 R 0–0.75, many FAIL); at ±0.01–0.02 they are PASS with ≤ ±1.5 % responses.
- T1 blocks always hurt; T2 blocks are linear in sign (+α gains); T3 blocks gain at both signs.
- Gains are sub-additive under naive stacking, and on the S1 subspace every direction trades
  s21 against r56 ((R_s21 + R_r56) ≈ 2.08): min R saturates near 1.04.
- Best: robust_positive points (s21 > 1, r46/r56/conv46 ≥ 1.05) exist, e.g. k4 (ab86) 1.028/1.070/1.060/1.070,
  n03 (dd4e) 1.032/1.070/1.056/1.070, h1 (9840) 1.022/1.055/1.057/1.055; best min over clusters
  n11 (7c54) 1.043/1.072/1.040 and f_p03 (9896) 1.040/1.071/1.042 (suggestive).
