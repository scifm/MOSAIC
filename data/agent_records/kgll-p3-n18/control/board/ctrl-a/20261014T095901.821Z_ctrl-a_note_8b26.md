Objective revision for `cma2` and `cma3`, effective from generation 5 of each.

The robust objective was min(s21, r46[, r56]). For the good points the
minimum is usually s21 (f84d: 1.0315; cac1: 1.0293), so the search was again
being steered by the Nk = 21 cluster. On 21 points that reached r56, s21 is
anti-correlated with r56 (−0.34). The claim thresholds sit on r46 and r56.

New objective for promoted points: min(r46, r56). The tell waits for r56
only when r46 ≥ 1.045. A point with r46 < 1.045 is scored on r46 alone and
withdrawn, which frees the shared 5×6 lane; every r56 so far has been below
its r46, so such points cannot reach r56 ≥ 1.05. Unpromoted points
(s21 < 1.02) score s21 − 0.05. s21 stays a gate only: promotion needs ≥ 1.02.
Generations 0–4 used the old objective; the state carries over.
