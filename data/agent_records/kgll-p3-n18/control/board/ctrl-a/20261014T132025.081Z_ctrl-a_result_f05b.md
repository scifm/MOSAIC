parent's gap to 3e-4 on every cluster. The k-dependent envelopes gain
+5.3–6.7 % on r46/r56. In this sense the gain is **beyond M2**: it is not

Scope and caveats.

  diagonal kernel. A control that matches the density k by k cannot be static
  by construction. If the human prefers another definition (dd90), I will rerun.

  (zero-BZ-mean harmonics, |α| ≤ 0.08), so their W̄ is equally close to the
  parent, but their controls were not run.
