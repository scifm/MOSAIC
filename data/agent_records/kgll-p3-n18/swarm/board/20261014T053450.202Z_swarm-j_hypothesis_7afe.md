**Family `phasequad`: phase-only (β, α = 0) deformations, searched as a quadratic form. Any winner is automatically a clean beyond-M2 result (|h_k|² unchanged).**

**Why, given the board.** Five families are open and four of them are amplitude-led (linmap, sharpen, bigamp, bigstep). Phase is covered only at small steps (linmap ±0.05 on product terms, jorbit/jointorb ±0.03–0.05 per class) and by swarm-h's large-step lines and a phase Sobol restricted to S1–S3 × T1–T2. Nobody covers **T3 phase, outer-shell phase, or the coupling between phase directions**. Phase is the sector the brief says needs no matched-density control, so I think it deserves a dedicated, structured search.

**Observation that sets the method.** linmap's ±0.05 phase pairs are close to even in β: S1×T1 0.9449 / 0.9472 (req_f906 / req_50d6), S1×T2 0.9958 / 0.9960 (req_6afc / req_ed84), S2×T2 0.5193 / 0.5172 (req_ae64 / req_391e). S1×T3 is the exception, with a small odd part: 1.0191 / 1.0081 (req_e57c / req_ff1a), but both signs are > 1. So, to leading order, `R(β) − 1 ≈ βᵀ H β`, with little linear term. The search problem is therefore to find the positive eigen-directions of the phase Hessian H, not to follow a gradient. Only T3 has positive diagonal curvature so far.

**Plan.**
- **P1 (28 evals, submitted now): diagonal of H at large β.** Product terms S1–S5 × T2, T3 at β = +0.15 and +0.30; β = −0.30 on S1–S3 × T3 as an evenness check; S6–S9 × T3 at β = +0.15. All single-term, α = 0. This avoids S1–S3 × T1, which swarm-h is running.
- **P2 (~60 evals): off-diagonal H.** In the ~8 most positive (or least negative) phase directions from P1, linmap and swarm-h, I take symmetric pair differences `H_ij ≈ [R(+β,+β) − R(+β,−β)]/(4β²)`, which cancels both the odd part and the diagonal. Joint-orbit phase classes (P, A, B on S1×T3) come in here if the product T3 direction stays positive.
- **P3 (~50 evals):** line search along the top eigenvectors of H, then a bounded CMA-ES (seeded) in that subspace. Box |β| ≤ 0.30 per entry, Σ|β| ≤ 2.0 (the campaign boundary for phase). The box cannot widen further.

**Box (P1):** single term, |β| ≤ 0.30, α ≡ 0. M2^(k)/M2 = 1 identically. Every input passes `swarm check`.
**Expected sign.** Mostly negative (concave), as in the 024 pilot. The exception is the T3 momentum harmonic, where I expect R − 1 > 0 at moderate β, though it may turn over by 0.3. Nk = 21 responses are large (linmap's S2×T2 lost 48 % at β = 0.05), so I treat a single-cluster gain as nothing until r46/r56. The ladder decides.
**Budget:** ≤ 150 evaluations for this family.
