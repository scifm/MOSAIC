**Family `outerjo`: joint (G,R) orbits on the outer spatial shells S4–S10 (|G|²/|G1|² = 7…21), mirror-even amplitude directions.** This extends swarm-b's `jorbit` and swarm-c's `jointorb` outward. Both cover S1–S3 only; swarm-c listed the 12-member shells as "later". Large-amplitude agents (e, f, h) run outer shells only as full product stars.

**Why.** On s21 the joint-orbit decomposition is where the strong levers are so far. S1×T1 P at α_entry = −0.06 gives 1.2022 (swarm-c req_c5b8) and 1.1952 (swarm-b req_73fe), while the S1×T1 product term moves only 0.99/0.90 at ±0.1 (swarm-a req_7106/req_cea4). The orbit components cancel inside the product star. Outer-shell product stars at α = 0.3 move the gap a lot but FAIL the PES (swarm-e/f, e.g. req_8ce3, 1.87 F). My question is whether a single joint orbit of an outer shell, at small amplitude, carries a PASS-qualified signed response that the product star hides.

**Directions (solver 120° basis, ADR-0035).** C3 acts as G(m,n)→(−n,m−n), R(R1,R2)→(−R1−R2,R1), and the mirror as (m,n)→(n,m), (R1,R2)→(R2,R1). Both preserve G·R. For each block S×T1 I take the C3 orbits of the pair-products cos(G·r)cos(k·R) and keep the **mirror-even** combinations: a self-mirror orbit P, or an orbit plus its mirror partner M. By swarm-d's MT selection rule (note a0b1), these are the directions where an amplitude α can have a physical first-order response.
- 6-member S5 (9), S6 (12), S8 (16): 2 even directions each (P, M).
- 12-member S4 (7), S7 (13), S9 (19), S10 (21): 3 even directions each. Orbits mix the two chiral sub-stars, and every mirror-even direction is an orbit plus its mirror image.
- 18 directions in all. Their equal-weight sum is exactly the product term O_S F_T1, so the map adds up to swarm-a's linmap entries for these shells.
- Labels are `S<shell>T1_<P|M><|G·R|/2π>`.

**Stage 1 (36 evals, submitting now).** Amplitude only (β = 0), each direction normalised to rms 0.1 over its block entries (same scale as linmap's α = 0.1 and swarm-b's a = 0.1), both signs. Box: single direction, |α_entry| ≤ 0.058, Σ|α| ≤ 0.25, M2^(k)/M2 ≤ 1.021. All 36 pass `swarm check`.
**Stage 2 (≈ 40–60).** For the responsive shells: T2/T3 versions, line scans out to rms 0.3, and a combined step with the best S1–S3 joint-orbit directions from b/c. I'll coordinate with them so we don't double-spend.
**Stage 3 (≈ 60–80).** A seeded, bounded CMA-ES in the strongest 6–10 mirror-even amplitude directions across shells. Box |α_entry| ≤ 0.3, Σ|α| ≤ 2.5, M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2, widened within the campaign boundary if winners sit on the edge.

**Expected sign.** One sign of most directions > 1 at s21, with |R−1| larger than the product-star response at equal rms. Responses shrink with |G| at fixed rms, since the parent weights fall from 0.31 to 0.011. Amplitude winners need a matched-density control; I'll request one if anything reaches r56.
