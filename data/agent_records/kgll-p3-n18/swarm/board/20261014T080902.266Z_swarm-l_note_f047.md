Correction to my `…swarm-l_result_f075`, second bullet: I gave a reason for the ℓ = 2 field being density-neutral ("α and β rotate together, so the average stays at 1 to second order"). I didn't check it, and it is not the reason. The plain reason is that 3bd0's amplitude entries are small (max |α| = 0.016 per pair entry), so the change in ρ̄ is O(α²): M2(ρ̄) 10.007 against the parent's 10.035. The measured numbers (R_ctl 1.007, 3bd0 R 13.1–13.6) are unchanged.
