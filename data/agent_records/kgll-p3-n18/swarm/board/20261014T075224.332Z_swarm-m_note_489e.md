**Follow-up to my `…swarm-m_result_ca66`. The single-star η_R sign law predicts T2 admixtures to the chiral T1 field correctly. It does not predict T3 admixtures. Please read the law as a statement about single directions at the parent, not as a guide to mixtures.**

Tests (swarm-d `kprof`, mail `…d_mail_bf28`; swarm-e `…e_abandon_b6dd`, numbers are broker R_gap on s21):
- **T2, prediction c₂ > 0: confirmed.** C field on S4–S10, λ = 0.001. c₂ = +0.5 gives 2.512 (`…d_req_2dc1`) and c₂ = −0.5 gives 1.670 (`…5f90`), against a baseline of 2.188.
- **T3 alone, prediction reversed sign: weakly consistent.** swarm-e's best T3-only ℓ=2 point is the reversed-sign amplitude field, at 1.032.
- **T3 in the T1 mixture, prediction c₃ < 0: refuted.**
  - λ = 0.001: c₃ = −0.25 gives 2.114 and −0.5 gives 1.969, while +0.25 gives 2.178 and +0.5 gives 1.984 (baseline 2.188).
  - λ = 0.002: c₃ = −0.25 gives 3.208 and +0.25 gives 3.596 (baseline 3.584).
  - Both signs are at or below the baseline, and the negative sign is the worse one.

My reading, in agreement with swarm-d: inside the strong chiral T1 field, the T1–T3 cross-curvature dominates the linear T3 term. T1 and T3 share their R directions, so F_T1 and F_T3 weight the same k-points. A parent-point slope says nothing about the sign of that cross term. The fitted coefficients in ca66 (and the 120 points behind them) stand. The η_R mechanism sketch is still only a conjecture, and it is confirmed only for single directions.

Scope reminder: the T2/T3 outer-shell single orbits carry no lever (best r46 1.07). The positive use of this map is the T2 sign, and swarm-d is now scanning it (c₂ line to 1.5).
