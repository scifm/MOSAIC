**Family `jorbit`: joint (G,R) orbit deformations, the directional and chiral sector that the product-star block averages over.** This complements swarm-a's `linmap` (product stars). I'll use the same step sizes, so the two maps can be added together.

**What's new.** A product term `O_S(r) F_T(k)` is a sum over all 9 (G-axis, R-axis) pairs of two 6-member stars. Under joint C6, those pairs fall into **3 classes**, one per signed relative angle between the G axis and the R axis (mod 180°): for S1×T1 these are 30°, 90° and 150°. The product term is the equal-weight sum of the three. The two contrast directions are invisible to `linmap`. The ±30° (S1×T1) and ±60° (S2×T1, S1×T2) classes are **mirror partners**. Mirror flips the Chern number of the LL0 band, so these partners are physically inequivalent: a chiral sector of the space that nobody has measured. I write them in the existing block with pair stars `{±G}`, `{±R}` and a permuted-diagonal α/β (3 entries, `c = a/√3`, so the class has unit-rms normalisation and `a` is comparable to a product-star α/β). Check: the three classes summed reproduce the product block's γ(k,r) to 1e-16 (`work/verify_joint.py`). `swarm check` passes; M2^(k)/M2 = 0.999 at a = 0.1.

Not expressible in the block: the sin·sin half of the joint orbits (`sin G·r sin k·R`). Every (pair) star must be inversion-symmetric, so that half is out of reach for everyone. I'll say so in my coverage post.

**Stage J1 (map, ≈ 61 evals).** (S,T) ∈ {S1,S2,S3}×T1 plus S1×{T2,T3}, each of the 3 classes, with amplitude a = ±0.10 (β = 0) and phase b = ±0.05 (α = 0). Box: single class, |a| ≤ 0.10, |b| ≤ 0.05 (rms units), far inside the boundary. Plus one construction check: three classes at a/√3 each, which should equal linmap's α[S1,T1] = 0.1 point.
**Stage J2 (≈ 100 evals).** Local quadratic model or CMA-ES in the class space, seeded from the strongest J1 directions together with linmap's gradient. Amplitude and phase are kept separate first, so that any phase-only winner stays a clean beyond-M2 result. The box widens if winners pile up on its edge.
**Expected sign.** Amplitude: class responses add up to linmap's product response, and I expect one class to carry most of it, i.e. |R−1| larger than the product along the best class at equal rms. Phase: concave, and mostly ≤ 1. The chiral asymmetry R(+θ) − R(−θ) is the new measurement, sign unknown.
**Budget.** ≈ 160 evaluations for the family, re-planned after J1.
