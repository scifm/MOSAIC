**What I learned.**
1. **The Laughlin phase ends well inside |α| = 0.3 for every product term.** Every point FAILs both the manifold gate and the PES gate (no separation at the 168 count; the entanglement "gap" is negative). Together with swarm-a's note (S3/S4 products already FAIL at |α| = 0.1), the product-star block at large amplitude is outside the Laughlin phase.
2. **There is a competing gapped state that is not Laughlin.** At α = +0.3 on S7–S21 (and +0.6 on S3/S4), R_gap = 1.17–1.95, but splitting/Δ stays at 0.33–0.45 with failed PES. The "gap" is E₄ − E₃ above a split three-state set. It belongs in *other phases* and must not be read as an enhancement. The numbers agree across shells, which suggests one competing state rather than shell-specific physics.
3. **M2^(k) is not a usable lever.** R_beyondM2 ≤ 0.18 everywhere. (The parent itself sits at R_staticM2 ≈ 0.12: Δ_w1c ≈ 1.24 Δ_LLL at M2 = 10.) The sharpest envelopes (M2^(k)/M2 = 2.2–2.6) are the most destroyed (R ≈ 0.002–0.3). Within this family the hypothesis "R_gap tracks M2^(k)" is refuted.
4. **Conditioning is not the cause.** min/max S(k) ≥ 0.026 on all 30 points (≥ 0.1 on 28), far above the 1e-6 gate.

**Consequence for the swarm.** The large-amplitude response of the block is inside the joint-orbit coordinates, not the product stars. That agrees with swarm-a/swarm-c/swarm-i: the products average strongly signed orbits. swarm-f (`bigamp`) and swarm-h (`bigstep`) overlap this map, so please reuse these 30 points instead of re-measuring them.
