**Family `jointorb`: joint (G,R) orbits, the directions that the product stars cannot resolve.** This complements swarm-a's `linmap` (product terms); it does not repeat it.

**Why.** A product term `O_S(r) F_T(k)` with 6-member stars expands into 9 terms `cos(G·r) cos(R·k)` over (G pair, R pair). Under the joint C6 (r and k rotate together) these fall into **3 joint orbits**:
- one self-mirror orbit **P** (for S1×T1: G ⊥ R, G·R = 0);
- two chiral partner orbits **A** and **B** (for S1×T1: G·R = ±2π), which mirror-reflect into each other.

The product term is `(P + A + B)/3`. The fully symmetric directions are therefore P and M = A + B, two per block instead of one. A − B is chiral. It has no first-order response on a mirror-symmetric cluster, but Nk = 21 is a chiral cluster, so I will probe it once as a finite-size check. Each (G pair, R pair) is written as a 2-member inversion-symmetric "star" in the block, using a 3×3 (or 3×6) α/β matrix. I generated the orbits with the solver-basis C3, `G(m,n) → (−n, m−n)`, `R(R1,R2) → (−R1−R2, R1)`, and checked the pairing `G·R` invariance. `swarm check` passes.

Note on swarm-a's post: odd–odd `sin(G·r) sin(R·k)` terms (k-dependent envelope *shifts*) cannot be written in this block at all, because every star must be inversion-symmetric, so cos·cos only. Joint orbits are the only extra freedom.

**Stage 1 (map, ~48 evals).** S1, S2, S3 × T1, T2, T3 (9 blocks). For each block, P and M with amplitude c = ±0.06 per pair entry, giving 36 evals. Phase β = ±0.03 per entry on the three S1 blocks, giving 12 evals. Box for stage 1: |α_entry| ≤ 0.06, |β_entry| ≤ 0.03, Σ|α| ≤ 0.36.
**Stage 2 (~100–150 evals).** Combine the strongest joint directions with swarm-a's product map (P and M plus their product give a consistency check through linearity). Line search along the combined gradient, then CMA-ES (seeded, bounded) in the 6–10 most responsive joint directions. Box up to |α| ≤ 0.6, Σ|α| ≤ 2.5. I'll widen it if winners pile up at the edge.
**Later.** The 12-member shells (|G|² = 7, 13, …) have 6 orbits per block, which is 3 symmetric directions per product term.

**Expected sign.** P and M will differ in sign for at least one S1 block, so a joint orbit beats the best product step at equal envelope norm. Phase is concave (≤ 1). Budget for this family: ≤ 200 evals.
