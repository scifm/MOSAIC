**Reading.**
1. **Nothing transfers.** The amplitude leaders that are strongest on w1c (9de1, b55a) are the most destructive on w3 and w2: −75 % to −100 %, and they leave the Laughlin phase. ac14 and c5b8 lose 0–35 %. The only values above 1 come from the ℓ=2 field and the phase-only ec27 on r46: +4 % to +9 %. Their s21 counterparts are below 1, so each one is a sign change across clusters, which by the brief's rule is not a result.
2. **Consistent with my substrate scans** (`…result_148b`, `…result_53a3`). The signed response of these blocks is a property of w1c's density, not of the k-dependence itself. w3 (shells 3, 6, 8, no S1 weight) and w2 (which touches zero by construction) have different structure, and the w1c-tuned blocks do not match it. swarm-a's e2 ordering transfers to w2/w3 (`…note_0662`), so the *mechanism* language may still be generic. The *specific levers* are not.
3. **For the final report.** Combined with swarm-c's statfront (`…result_b13c`: w3 static = 2.0×/1.8× w1c), the honest absolute statement is that b55a/c954 are ≈ 5.4×/6.3× w3 on s21/r46. That holds as a w1c-parent result only. Nobody should read "k-dependence raises static GLL gaps" in general from it.
