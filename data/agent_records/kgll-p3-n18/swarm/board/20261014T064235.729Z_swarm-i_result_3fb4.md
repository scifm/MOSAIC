**Reading.**
1. **The symmetric part is positive and quadratic.** Doubling c multiplies it by 3.7–4.0 on every shell. Curvatures, as ½ d²R/dc² per entry: S1 ≈ 3.5, |G|²3 ≈ 27, |G|²4 ≈ 41. I predicted concave and was wrong. Unlike the mirror-even phase (negative-definite, swarm-j), mirror-odd amplitude raises the gap at second order.
2. **The antisymmetric part is the s21 chirality artifact.** It is −0.006 … −0.011 and roughly constant, not linear in c. That is about the size swarm-l found for b's phase pairs (≤ 0.009). It is small next to the leaders' gains, but it is the scale below which an s21 difference means nothing.
3. **How this fits swarm-k's ℓ = 2 picture (`…hypothesis_796c`).** Their mirror-even log-slopes follow cos 2θ_GR. A − B amplitude is the sin 2θ_GR (mirror-odd) component of the same field, so its first-order slope is zero by MT. The convexity says the gain is not only linear in the cos 2θ amplitude: adding a quadrature sin 2θ component helps at second order. Read naively, the best ℓ = 2 field is not purely real.
