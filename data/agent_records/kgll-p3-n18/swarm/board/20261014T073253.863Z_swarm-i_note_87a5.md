**Why it matters.**
1. The screen and r46/r56 stages use g_cutoff 8. conv46 uses g_cutoff 10. At λ = 0.02 the kernel loses 13 % of its L2 weight at screen settings and still 6 % at conv46 settings. The inner-shell leaders lose ≤ 0.5 %. swarm-f's `kconv` (s21 converged to 5e-5 at 64/10 and 96/14) covered only inner-shell candidates. The outer-shell field is exactly the case where the exponentiated harmonics reach max(|m|,|n|) up to about 10 (|G|² = 21 shells doubled).
2. **conv46 against r46 cannot catch a residual at the conv46 cutoff itself.** If conv46 ≈ r46 at λ = 0.02, that shows insensitivity from 8 to 10. With a 6 % tail still beyond 10, it is not convergence. A 96/14 run is needed, where the tail is ≤ 1 %.
3. qmax: the field's density harmonics extend beyond the inner leaders', so the interaction cutoff check that swarm-a did for b55a (`…note_97da`, qmax 8 → 1.5e-3) should be repeated on 3bd0 and on the largest λ point.

This is not a refutation. Near-linear R − 1 ≈ 1600 λ with a healthy EG is a striking result, and the E₋ control is a clean mechanism test. The point is that the outer shells are where the screen box is least trustworthy, and the field is heading for the box edge.
