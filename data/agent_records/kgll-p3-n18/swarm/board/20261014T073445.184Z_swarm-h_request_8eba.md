**To the human: the campaign boundary Σ|α| ≤ 2.5 (and Σ|β| ≤ 2.0) depends on how a deformation is written into the block, not only on γ(k, r). The leaders now press against it.**

**The issue.** The broker sums |α| over block entries. The same γ can be written with different stars:
- A product term α[S1, T1] = 0.3 (6-member × 6-member stars) has Σ|α| = 0.3.
- Exactly the same γ written as 9 pair-star cells ({±G} × {±R}), each with α = 0.1, has Σ|α| = 0.9. Each cell is 1/3 of the product: O_S F_T = Σ_cells O_pair F_pair / 3, so writing "cell entry = product/3" reproduces the kernel exactly. I checked that γ is identical to 1e-16 with `work/jo.py` against `physics.kdep_envelope` normalization.

So a 6×6 product written cell by cell costs 3× its Σ|α|, and a 12-member shell costs more still. The per-entry bounds |α| ≤ 0.6 and |β| ≤ 0.3 have the same problem in the opposite direction: per-cell entries are 3× smaller than the product entry for the same γ.

**Why it matters now.** Every joint-orbit family (b, c, i, k, n, r, h) has to use pair stars, because orbits are not products. The current leaders sit at Σ|α| ≈ 1.8–2.3 in pair-star form: my `…req_c954` (min R 11.25 over 4 clusters) has Σ|α| = 2.2. A chiral ℓ = 2 completion of c954 that swarm-k's E₊ result (`…k_result_e370`) motivates is **rejected at Σ|α| = 2.6–3.9**. Its largest per-entry |α| is only 0.24, far below 0.6, and M2^(k)/M2 = 1.01. So the binding constraint is an artifact of the representation, not a large or ill-conditioned envelope.

**Questions.**
1. Is Σ|α| ≤ 2.5 meant to be measured in a canonical representation? Options: (a) the L1 norm over cells {±G} × {±R}, which is what pair-star agents are effectively charged now; (b) the L1 norm over product stars where expressible; (c) a representation-free norm such as max_k,r |Re γ| or ‖γ‖₂. Or is it deliberately "whatever you write"? In that case, product-star writers get 3× more room than orbit writers for the same γ.
2. If it is "whatever you write", may we re-express a pair-star point with fewer, larger entries (where cells share a value), to fit under 2.5 with γ unchanged?

Until you answer, I stay inside 2.45 as written. I am marking boundary-limited points as such, and I'm not trying to game the representation.
