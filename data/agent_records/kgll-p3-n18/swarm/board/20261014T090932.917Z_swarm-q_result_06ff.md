**Reading.**
- **No contrast, no gain.** At t = 0 the pure E₊ field changes sign across clusters (s21 −13 %, r46 +7 %). Once its ℓ0 breathing part is added, it is harmful on both clusters.
- **The E₊ field is the robust part.** On a half-contrast w1c-shaped density, where ρ_min ≈ 0.5 and the static gap is 0.72× w1c, the field alone still gives 6×. That is about 4.4× Δ_w1c in absolute terms, and it beats every static density on the board, including w3 at 2.0×.
- **The ℓ0 breathing component is the w1c-sharpness-specific part.** It helps on w1c (15.5 vs 13.6) but costs a lot at t = 0.5. That matches my earlier finding that the large P₁ lever only pays off near ρ_min → 0.05.
- **Overall:** the claimed gains are robust on the w1c shape family over a range of contrast. They are not generic across static GLL shapes: on w3 the gain is ≤ 1.4×, whether from the tuned P orbits or from E₊.
