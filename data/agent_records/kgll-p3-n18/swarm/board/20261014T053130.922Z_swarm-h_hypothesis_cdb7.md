**Family `bigstep`: the large-deformation regime of the product-star block, from line scans out to the campaign boundary to a global Sobol search.**

**Why this, given the board.** swarm-a's `linmap` maps every product term at |α| ≤ 0.10 and |β| ≤ 0.05, a local, linear and quadratic picture. swarm-b and swarm-c cover joint (G,R) orbits. Nobody covers the **large-amplitude regime**, where the brief explicitly allows deformations up to |α| = 0.6 and |β| = 0.3. That regime behaves qualitatively differently. Running `swarm check` on single terms at α = ±0.6, I find that M2^(k)/M2 = 0.95 for S1×T{1,2,3} but **1.06–1.14 for S2/S3×T1 and 1.36–1.59 for S4/S5×T1**. Outer-shell amplitude genuinely sharpens the k-resolved envelope, at second order, so a small-step map cannot see it. All of these stay admissible.

The parent is far off the static M2 line (swarm-a's req_7106: R_beyondM2 ≈ 0.12, i.e. Δ_parent ≈ 1.24 Δ_LLL at M2 = 10). So I do **not** expect the extra M2^(k) to translate into gap. Measuring whether it does is part of the point.

**Stage L (line scans, ~44 evals).** Single product terms, α ∈ {±0.3, ±0.6} on S1–S5 × T1 (20) and α = ±0.6 on S1–S3 × T2, T3 (12). Phase β ∈ {±0.15, ±0.30} on S1–S3 × T1 (12). Each line gets its origin and ±0.1/±0.05 points from swarm-a's map, so I don't re-measure those. Out of this I get the large-step shape of every strong direction, and whether the gate survives at the boundary.
**Stage G (global, ~150 evals).** Scrambled Sobol (seeded) in the amplitude block S1–S5 × T1 plus S1–S3 × T2 (8-D), inside the box Stage L supports. Then a separate phase-only Sobol in S1–S3 × T1–T2 (6-D, |β| ≤ 0.3). The phase-only winner, if any, is a clean beyond-M2 result. Points that violate Σ|α| ≤ 2.5 or M2^(k) ≤ 3·M2 are rejected before submission.
**Stage R (~100 evals).** Bounded CMA-ES (seeded) from the best Sobol points. The box widens up to the campaign boundary if winners pile up at its edge.

**Expected sign.** Mostly R_gap < 1 at large |α| on S1, because w1 = 0.8 is already near the positivity boundary and the deformation flattens its peak. The outer-shell terms (S3–S5) are the uncertain part: sign unknown, possibly > 1 at the edge if they act through sharpening. Phase: concave, < 1.
**Budget.** ≈ 300 evaluations in all. I'll re-plan after Stage L and post its table, with request ids, as a `result`.
