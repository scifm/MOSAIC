**Family `orthant`: a sign-orthant factorial over the six inner T1 levers. Is the ac14 orthant the only basin near the parent, and does log R stay additive *across* signs?**

Why: every exploiting family (r xsector, h josobol, n trgrad, p poolbo) is centred on the ac14 "good orthant". h's Sobol is explicitly confined to it. The log-additivity that the surrogates assume (swarm-r X1) has only been tested inside that orthant. Nobody has asked whether a mixed-sign combination opens a different Laughlin basin, or how strongly the levers interact once a sign flips. This is a herding check, and it costs 32 evaluations.

**Design.** A 2^(6−1) half-fraction, resolution VI (defining relation I = P1·P2·P3·u1·u2·u3), so all 6 main effects and all 15 two-factor interactions in log R are estimable without aliasing. The levers are in my class coordinates (unit-rms class, `work/joint.py`), each at a single step where its good sign PASSes alone:
- P1 = S1×T1 90° class, α = −0.10·s (73fe, 1.195)
- P2 = |G|²3×T1 0° class, α = +0.10·s (6e96, 1.193)
- P3 = |G|²4×T1 90° class, α = −0.10·s (aba6, 1.370)
- u1 = S1×T1 30°/150°, β = ±0.05·s (ba6c, 1.331)
- u2 = |G|²3×T1 60°/120°, β = ±0.025·s (c6d3, 1.420)
- u3 = |G|²4×T1 30°/150°, β = ±0.025·s (f506, 1.506)

s = +1 is the good sign. The (+…+) corner is an ac14-like point in my coordinates.

**Box:** |α_entry| ≤ 0.058, |β_entry| ≤ 0.029, far inside the boundary. All 32 pass `swarm check`.
**Expected:** the good corner is the best (product of singles ≈ 4.4, with s21 splitting possibly binding). Corners with P2− or P3+ fall off the cliffs (the singles are 0.43 and a FAIL), and no other corner exceeds about 1.5. If a corner other than (+…+) PASSes with R > 2, that is a second basin and is worth a follow-up. Two-factor interactions: I expect |β_ij| ≪ the main effects, except P2×P3 and u-pairs, where the s21 splitting couples them.
**Budget:** 32, plus the complementary half-fraction (32) only if interactions turn out large.
