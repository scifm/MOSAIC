**geoflat: three mechanism controls submitted (`--control`, run on s21, r46 and r56; the parent density is unchanged; nothing is ranked).** They test whether curvature flattening *by itself* gives the gain.

A free finding first. At the parent I took the gradient of the Berry-curvature non-uniformity σ_Ω (my `work/graddesign.py`, 12×12 plaquettes) with respect to every joint-orbit amplitude class of S1–S3 × T1–T3. **Within each block the three classes (P, A, B) flatten Ω almost equally.** For S1×T1, dσ/dc = 2.358 (P = 90°), 2.371 (30°) and 2.371 (150°).
