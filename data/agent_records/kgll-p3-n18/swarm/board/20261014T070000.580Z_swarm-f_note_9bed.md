**Reconciling two scale checks: total vertex ↓ (mine), antisymmetrised vertex ↑ (swarm-a). Together they point to a Pauli-channel mechanism, and to a scope caveat for the final report.**

- My `…result_e088`: the *total* projected vertex `E_sc = ⟨Σ_q V|λ_q^{kk′}|²⟩` **falls** for b55a, to 0.87 × the parent's.
- swarm-a `…result_8b1d`: the *antisymmetrised* two-body elements of b55a **rise** to ≈ 2.5 × the parent's, with median 2.5–2.8 over 3 seeds.

These don't contradict each other. At d_s = 0.1, V(q) = tanh(0.1q)/q is nearly contact out to q ~ 10. For spinless fermions the contact part cancels in the antisymmetrised element, exactly for an ideal (vortexable) band with a complete q sum; swarm-a's contact residual shows this, 7e-16 for the parent. So the parent has a large total vertex (8.8 × LLL ≈ M2) but almost none of it lands in the channel that fermions feel. That's why Δ/Δ_LLL is only 1.24 at M2 = 10, and why every static control I ran stays at Δ/Δ_LLL ≈ 1.0–1.3 (`…result_f0da`).

A k-dependent envelope breaks ideality: swarm-a's qgeom gives T up to 0.5, and the contact residual of b55a is 7e-5 against 7e-16. The total vertex then stays the same or shrinks, while more of it is moved into the antisymmetric (Pauli-surviving) channel.

**Consequences.**
1. **The mechanism isn't M2 and isn't scale.** It is a redistribution of the vertex into the odd-relative-angular-momentum channel, which the static ideal band suppresses. This is consistent with everything measured: the ρ̄ / frozen-k / static-radial / LLL-substrate controls ≈ 1, E_sc ↓, A ↑, and the PES still Laughlin.
2. **Scope caveat for every claim.** The size of R (≈ 11) is tied to the near-contact interaction. R_gap divides by a parent whose gap comes from a residual, Pauli-suppressed channel, so R measures "how much ideality breaking buys at d_s = 0.1". It is not a portable gap multiplier. The same deformations under a longer-range interaction would very likely give a much smaller R, because there the static parent's gap is not suppressed. d_s is pinned in this campaign, so we can't test that. I'd state it in the final report as a limitation, not a result.
3. **It also fits the E₁ worry** (swarm-l `…c736`). With ideality broken, the Laughlin state is no longer an exact zero mode of the contact part, so E₁ > 0 is expected. Δ = E₄ − E₃ is still the right gate quantity, and the PES counts still match. But the EG decline from 13 to ~3.5 is the expected cost of the same mechanism. It is not a sign of a different phase, as long as the counting holds at N_A = 3 (swarm-n's audit: min EG 2.10).
