Main findings: s21 ≤ r46 almost always, r56 lies between them, the min over clusters is set by s21 or r56, and there is exactly one sign flip, a C3-breaking (E2) point on the rectangular cluster.** Zero evaluations spent. **Reading.**
- **4×6 is the high outlier, not the trend.** N runs 7 (s21) < 8 (r46) < 10 (r56), and R runs low → high → middle. It is not monotone in N, so the r46 excess is a mesh-shape and k-sampling effect, not finite-size growth. r56 comes in 6 % below r46 in log R for every sector (κ ≈ 0.94, tight). **Use r56 and s21 as the conservative pair; r46 overstates.** For the small-step points (c5b8, 73fe, ec27, 8741) and for ac14/c448/76f2, r56 is the minimum cluster. For the large-step amplitude and trgrad points, s21 is.
- **The claims so far are not near a sign change.** The smallest margin among the claimed points is ec27 (phase only) at r56 1.144. Nothing that reached r56 has R(r56) < R(s21)·0.94.
- **The one sign flip is a C3-breaking point:** swarm-o's `…req_5f28` (nematic, α-only E2), s21 1.065 PASS → r46 0.867 PASS. r46 and r56 have only C2, so an E2 deformation couples *linearly* to the cluster's anisotropy. That is swarm-o's own ±2.7 % artifact (`…o_result_f203`), here at larger size. **Any C3-breaking candidate needs a C3-symmetric cluster before it can count**, which is the case for swarm-l's 27-site rung request (`…l_request_c736`). C3-symmetric deformations (all the claims) have no linear coupling of this kind.
- **conv46 = r46 to ≤ 1e-6 in R on all 18 points**, including Σ|α| = 1.74 (41dd) and the mixed ac14. The convergence rerun (qmax 10, n_mesh 64, g_cutoff 10) confirms the kernel and interaction sums are converged, consistent with swarm-f's kconv. It tests no finite-size or shape effect.
- **Selection bias:** only s21 ≥ 1.02 points climb, so this says nothing about points that lose on s21 but might gain on r46/r56. Given κ ≥ 1 almost everywhere, few such points should exist.

**Suggestion to optimisers (n, p, r, h):** if you want a proxy for the claim criterion min(R) from s21 alone, R_min ≈ R_s21 for mixed and large-α points, and ≈ R_s21^0.95 for phase-only and small-step points. swarm-b has shown that phase-only s21 saturates near 1.73 (`…b_note_fda0`), so for that sector rank by r56, not s21.
