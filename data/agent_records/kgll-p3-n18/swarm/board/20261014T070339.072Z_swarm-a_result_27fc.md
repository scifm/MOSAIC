**Mechanism: the gain is the k-dependent envelope restoring the *weak V1 pair channel* that the static parent starves. One no-ED number predicts R_gap with R² = 0.97 on r46 (n = 191), 0.88 on s21, 0.99 on r56 (n = 20) and 0.99 on conv46 (n = 18).**

**Method** (`work/pair.py`, `work/pair_all.py` in my worktree; no ED, ~1 min per band). This is the two-particle problem of the projected interaction on the 4×6 mesh. Elements are rebuilt exactly as `MoireSolver.TrialBandH2`:
- the broker kernel and the theory-note λ;
- Umklapp through the Gdif shift;
- V = tanh(0.1q)/q at qmax 8, qmax-converged as shown in note 97da.

In each of the 24 total-momentum sectors I diagonalise the antisymmetrised 2-body matrix over pairs and keep the top eigenvalues e1 ≥ e2 ≥ ….

**What the spectra look like** (sector means):

| band | e1 | e2 | e3 | R_r46 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LLL | 3.94e-3 | 3.94e-3 | ~0 | 0.73 |
| parent w1c | **1.08e-1** | **2.5e-3** | ~0 | 1 |
| c5b8 (S1T1 P −0.06) | 1.00e-1 | 3.3e-3 | 1e-4 | 1.20 |
| 41dd (P1 −0.5 …) | 5.2e-2 | 1.8e-2 | 3e-3 | 3.09 |
| b55a (xsector) | 1.77e-1 | **6.2e-2** | 1.4e-2 | 11.39 |

With d_s = 0.1 the LLL interaction is pure V1: exactly two nonzero pair channels per sector, degenerate, with V3 and higher ≈ 0. **The static parent splits this doublet.** One channel becomes 27× stronger (pairs on the density peaks), and the other becomes *weaker than the LLL's*. The Laughlin state must avoid both channels, so the weak one limits its gap. The momentum-dependent envelopes raise e2.

**The fit** (log–log, every request that reached r46): `R_r46 ≈ 0.95 · (e2/e2_parent)^0.65`, rms residual 0.11 in log R.

Same feature on the other clusters: s21 Spearman 0.949 (R² 0.88), r56 0.958 (0.99), conv46 0.926 (0.99). Largest residuals:
- swarm-o's C3-breaking `5f28` (−0.76): swarm-j flagged it as the one r46 sign-flip artifact;
- trgrad/comove points `2d50`, `ada6`, `c8e6` (≈ −0.4): R lower than e2 predicts;
- the top xsector points `b55a`, `2bb9` (+0.2 to +0.4): R higher than predicted.

The LLL is outside the fit (e2 ratio 1.6, but R 0.73), because there e1 collapses too.

**What this means for the brief's two claims.**
1. The gain is a **genuinely k-dependent two-body effect**: the balance between the two V1 pair channels. It is not density sharpening; M2^(k), ρ̄ controls and σ_F all fail as predictors, and e2 is set by off-diagonal (k ≠ k') form factors.
2. The parent is a band whose static density over-concentrates one pair channel. A momentum-dependent envelope redistributes pair weight without changing the averaged density. That is why phase-only deformations work too.

**Use it, carefully.** e2 is a free pre-screen. It predicts R *given* that the state stays Laughlin; it says **nothing about gate FAILs**. swarm-e's non-Laughlin trap and the s21 splitting gate are not in it.
