What I read from this:
1. **Linear and large at small c.** dR/dc ≈ −3.5 per unit c on both s21 and r46, and the two clusters agree to < 1 % up to c = −0.06. c5b8 already holds on r56 and conv46. So the small-c response is not an s21 artifact.
2. **At large c the two clusters part.** r46 keeps climbing roughly linearly (2.65 at −0.5); s21 flattens between −0.18 and −0.35 and then jumps. Over the same range the s21 manifold splitting grows to the 0.1 gate and shrinks again. On s21 the three Laughlin states sit in different momentum sectors, and a k-dependent envelope is allowed to split them. Something reorganises between −0.35 and −0.5, possibly in which state is E4. I've submitted c = −0.4, −0.45, −0.55, −0.6 (the boundary) to resolve it.
3. **Laughlin character weakens steadily.** The PES entanglement gap falls 10 → 2.8 (s21, N_A = 2), and s21c N_A = 3 at −0.5 is 2.37. That is still well above the 0.5 confidence line, but the trend is monotone. A gap that grows while the PES gap shrinks is the pattern to watch in r56 and conv46 for the large-c points.
4. **Density is not doing this.** M2^(k) falls (to 0.93 M2 at −0.5) while the gap rises, so R_beyondM2 rises. α ≠ 0, so the beyond-M2 claim still needs the matched-density control (swarm-a, -l and -q have c5b8 controls running).
5. **My co-moving prediction is partly refuted.** I predicted that only matched P_n = S_n×T_n would respond. swarm-c's map shows S2×T2-P at ±0.06 giving 0.760 / 0.974 (concave, no gain), while the *unmatched* S3×T1-P at −0.06 gives s21 1.368 and r46 1.620 (`…req_f721`). The rule that fits better is "G ⊥ R with R ∈ T1". I'll keep the S_n×T_n reading only for S1.
