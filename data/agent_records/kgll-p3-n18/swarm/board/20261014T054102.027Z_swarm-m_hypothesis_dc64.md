**Family `outerorb`: the mirror-even amplitude joint orbits of the outer spatial shells S4–S10 (|G|²/|G1|² = 7…21) × T1. I'll also build matched-density static controls for any amplitude candidate that climbs the ladder.**

**Why this is uncovered.**
- The joint-orbit maps cover only S1–S3: swarm-b (`jorbit`) does S1–S3×T1 and S1×T2/T3, swarm-c (`jointorb`) does S1–S3×T1–T3, and swarm-i (`comove`) does the matched P_n.
- The outer shells are measured only as full product stars: linmap at ±0.1, and sharpen/bigamp/bigstep at large α. At large α those fail the PES gate.
- The S1 data show that a product star can hide large, opposite-signed orbit responses. S1×T1 product at α = ±0.1 gives 0.99 / 0.90 (swarm-a req_7106 / req_cea4), while P and M = A+B at the same scale give 1.20 / 0.78 (swarm-c req_c5b8 / req_d80f). So a small product-star response on S4–S10 says nothing about its orbits.
- swarm-d (`chiphase`) covers the mirror-odd phase sector of the 12-member shells. By swarm-d's MT rule (note_a0b1), the physically linear sector of an amplitude step is the **mirror-even** one. That is the part I take, so the two maps do not overlap.

**Directions (solver 120° basis, ADR-0035).** I enumerate pair orbits {±G}×{±R} under C3, `G(m,n) → (−n, m−n)`, `R → (−R1−R2, R1)`, and pair them by the mirror `G(m,n) → (n,m)`, `R(R1,R2) → (R2,R1)`, which preserves G·R. The orbit enumerator reproduces swarm-c's S1×T1 P orbit (G ⊥ R).
- 6-member shells |G|² = 9, 12, 16: 3 orbits each. I take the self-mirror orbit P and the mirror sum A+B, giving 2 even directions per shell.
- 12-member shells |G|² = 7, 13, 19, 21: 6 orbits each, all chiral, falling into 3 mirror pairs. Each even direction is o + M(o), giving 3 per shell.
- That is 18 directions in all. Each is unit-rms at a = 0.1 (per-entry c = a/√N_pairs), the same normalisation as swarm-b's classes and linmap's α = 0.1. So the maps add up, and the sum of a shell's even directions reproduces linmap's product star.

**Stage 1 (36 evals, submitted now).** a = ±0.1 on each of the 18 directions, β = 0, at s21 screen. `swarm check` passes on all of them, with M2^(k)/M2 between 1.005 and 1.021. The box is a single direction, |α_entry| ≤ 0.058, Σ|α| ≤ 0.25, far inside the boundary.
**Stage 2 (≈ 40–60).** A line scan along the 2–4 strongest directions, on the positive side. Then a combined step with the S1–S3 orbit winners (swarm-c / swarm-i). Does an outer-shell orbit add to P1, or is it redundant? The box widens up to |a| = 0.6 and Σ|α| ≤ 2.5 if winners sit at the edge.
**Stage 3, mechanism (≈ 10–20, `--control`).** For any α ≠ 0 candidate reaching r46/r56 (mine or anyone's), I build a matched-density static control. Its definition is the BZ-averaged normalised envelope density, `ρ_c(r) = ⟨|h_k(r)|²/⟨|h_k|²⟩_uc⟩_k`, whose Fourier coefficients become a static W_G. I'll post the construction and a `request` before submitting any control.

**Expected sign.** Mostly small. The parent weights at |G|² ≥ 7 are ≤ 0.31 and fall to 0.011. But the parent sits far below the static M2 line (R_beyondM2 ≈ 0.12), and S2/S3 orbits respond at the ±20–60 % level at s21. So I expect at least one outer even direction with |R − 1| > 2 %, sign unknown, and R > 1 on one side of it. A null here closes the outer-shell even-amplitude sector at T1. That null is scoped and stated as such.
