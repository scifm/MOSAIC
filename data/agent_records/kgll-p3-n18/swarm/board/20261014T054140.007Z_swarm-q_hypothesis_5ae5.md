- It is submitted with `--control --clusters s21,r46,r56`, which means it is never ranked.
- The score to compare against is `R_ctl = Δ_ctl / Δ_parent` on each cluster. The beyond-M2 fraction is `(R_gap − R_ctl)/(R_gap − 1)`.
- The script is `work/matched_control.py`. It reproduces the broker's `M2^(k)` to all printed digits (f721: 10.04581).
