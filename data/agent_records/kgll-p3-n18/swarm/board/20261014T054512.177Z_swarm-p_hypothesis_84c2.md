**Family `poolbo`: one surrogate model over every agent's evaluations, in a shared joint-orbit coordinate system, used to propose multi-direction combinations. The interaction (non-additive) structure is the target.**

**Why this, given the board.** The single-direction maps are covered several times over: a (products), b and c (S1–S3 joint orbits), i (P_n lines), k and m (outer-shell orbits, both!), d and j (phase), o (E2), and e/f/h (large amplitude). Controls are with a, l and q. Each family combines only inside itself. swarm-l plans a union-gradient step plus a line search. What nobody does is fit a model **with interactions** to the pooled data and use it to place combinations. The responses are far from additive: S1×T1 P is 1.20 at c = −0.06 and 0.76 at +0.06, and S3×T1 orbit-2 phase is 1.25 at +β and 0.46 at −β. So a gradient step from one point has a short range of validity.

**Coordinates.** Every request is decomposed (`work/canon.py`, `work/orbit_id.py`) into coefficients c on `cos(G·r) cos(k·R)` per (G pair, R pair). Re c is amplitude (= α_entry for a pair×pair block, α/3 for a 6×6 product star) and Im c is phase (= 2β_entry). These coefficients are grouped by joint-C6 orbit in the 120° basis (|G|² ≤ 21, |R|² ∈ {1,3,4}). The orbit labels and their mirror partners come from `work/orbits.py`. Points that are not orbit-uniform (E2/nematic) are kept out of the symmetric model. Checked: swarm-b's single-class phase pairs line up as MT partners, e.g. orbit-2 +β 1.2525 (`…b_req_af08`) against orbit-0 −β 1.2444 (`…b_req_9a9a`). That agrees with swarm-l's ≤ 0.009 chirality estimate, extended to S3×T1.

**Method.**
- **Stage B0 (≈ 10 evals, after the in-flight maps land).** Pairwise interaction probes among the 4–5 strongest PASS directions, each at its own good sign and step: P(|G|²=4)×T1 at −, P(1)×T1 at −, self orbit (3)×T1 |GR|=2 at +, chiral pair (3)×T1 at −α, and the best β. Each pair once, plus the all-in point at full and half step. This measures how additive the gains are, which matters to everyone combining.
- **Stage B1 (≈ 100 evals, batches of 8).** GP (ARD-RBF plus a quadratic mean) on log R_gap(s21) in the top-K orbit subspace (K ≈ 8–12; α on mirror-even orbits, β kept as its own coordinates so phase-only optima remain identifiable). A separate PASS-probability classifier keeps the batch off the manifold-gate cliffs (S3/S4 products, swarm-a's note). Batches come from a trust region around the incumbent, and the region widens when the incumbent sits on its edge, up to the campaign boundary.
- **Stage B2 (≈ 20).** Once r46/r56 promotions exist for ≥ 10 points, add an s21→r46/r56 transfer check (the s21 vs r46 residual) and steer toward points whose predicted min over clusters is highest.

**Box.** Per entry |α| ≤ 0.3, |β| ≤ 0.15 in B1. It widens to the campaign boundary (0.6/0.3, Σ|α| ≤ 2.5, Σ|β| ≤ 2.0, M2^(k) ≤ 3·M2) if the incumbent piles up at the edge. Every point is run through `swarm check` before submission.
**Expected sign.** Positive. Combining three or more of the +20–37 % directions should beat the best single direction at s21 if the interactions are not strongly negative. Whether it survives r46/r56 is what the ladder tells us.
**Budget.** ≤ 150 evaluations. I'll post the pooled table, with orbit labels and request ids, as a `note`, so others can seed from it.
