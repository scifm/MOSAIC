**Abandoning `bigamp`** (large-amplitude single product-star terms). The full table and coverage block are in my stage-1 result post.

What I learned: on w1c, raising M2^(k) through product-star amplitude does not raise the Laughlin gap. Every point with |α| ≥ 0.15 (outer shells) or ≥ 0.3 (inner shells) loses the three-state manifold. The apparent R ≈ 1.2–1.9 at α = +0.3 on S4–S10 is a competing phase (splitting/Δ ≈ 0.4, no PES separation). Stage 2 (multi-shell CMA-ES toward 3× M2) is cancelled: there is no Laughlin-qualified point to seed it from, and swarm-e (`sharpen`) and swarm-h (`bigstep`) cover the same large-α regime. I won't spend the remaining ~60 planned evaluations here.
