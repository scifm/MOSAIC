**Family `sharpen`: large-amplitude α deformations, out to the campaign boundary, with outer spatial shells first.**

**Gap in coverage.** swarm-a (`linmap`) maps every single product term at |α| ≤ 0.10 and |β| ≤ 0.05. swarm-b and swarm-c map joint orbits at similar small steps. Nobody covers the non-perturbative region |α| = 0.3–0.6 that the campaign boundary allows. That is where the envelope can change qualitatively.

**Rationale, from a local single-particle proxy (no ED).** I measured M2^(k)/M2 for each single term S_s×T_p at α = ±0.6 (24² k grid, n_mesh 48, `physics.kdep_envelope.m2_k_batch`). `swarm check` reports the same numbers.
- S1: 0.95. S3, S4: ≈ 1.1. S7, S9, S12, S16: 1.35–1.7. S13, S19, S21: 1.9–2.6 (S21×T1 at +0.6 gives 2.63, close to the 3× cap).
- T1, T2 and T3 give the same ratio. The + sign is sharper than − on the 12-member shells.

The parent's static density is held near ρ_min = 0.05 by the positivity bound. Because the deformation is multiplicative (`e^{α O F}`), it can sharpen the density without breaking positivity. With near-contact interaction and the static M2 law, Δ ∝ M2. So if the gap follows M2^(k) even partly, large outer-shell amplitude could give R_gap well above 1. The counter-risk is that a k-dependent multiplier breaks ideality (vortexability), so the Laughlin state is no longer a near-zero mode. The ED decides between the two. That is the test.

**Plan.**
- Stage 1 (30 evals, submitted now): every spatial shell S1…S21 × T1, α ∈ {+0.3, +0.6, −0.6}, β = 0. Single term. All pass `swarm check`.
- Stage 2 (≈ 40–60): line scans and T2/T3 on the responsive shells. Two- and three-shell combinations, constrained by M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2 and Σ|α| ≤ 2.5.
- Stage 3 (≈ 60–100): bounded CMA-ES over α on {S7, S13, S19, S21, …} × T1–T3, box |α| ≤ 0.6, seeded from stage 2.

**Box.** |α| ≤ 0.6 (the campaign maximum), β = 0, Σ|α| ≤ 2.5, M2^(k) ≤ 3 M2. It is already at the campaign boundary, so it cannot be widened further.

**Expected sign.** Uncertain, and that is the point. If R_gap tracks M2^(k): positive, O(+10–50 %) on outer shells. If ideality loss dominates: negative. Either outcome is informative. It also gives R_beyondM2 across a wide M2^(k) range, which feeds the mechanism question.

**Caveat I will track.** Exponentiating outer shells at large α generates harmonics beyond the screen box (g_cutoff 8, n_mesh 48). Any winner has to survive the conv46 stage (g_cutoff 10, n_mesh 64, qmax 10). Before then I'll report the size of the truncated tail from local FFTs.

**Budget.** ≤ 200 evaluations for this family, re-planned after stage 1.
