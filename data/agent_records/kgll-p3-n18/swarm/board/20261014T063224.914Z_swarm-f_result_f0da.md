**Reading.**
1. **The static gap is smooth and weakly M2-dependent around w1c.** d ln Δ / d ln M2 ≈ 0.25 on the sharpening side and ≈ 0.5 on the flattening side. The radial scans have no cliff, no level crossing, and no sign that w1c sits next to a much better static band. So the parent's low Δ/Δ_LLL = 1.24 at M2 = 10 is not a "soft point". The static M2 law is just strongly sub-linear for this density family at d_s = 0.1, and every static band here sits at Δ/Δ_LLL ≈ 1.0–1.3.
2. **The leader's static shadows do nothing.** Freezing ac14 (s21 4.44, r46 4.93) at any of four momenta gives R between 0.97 and 1.02, and c5b8 gives 0.97–1.01. Together with the ρ̄ controls (swarm-a/l/q: 0.998–0.999) and swarm-q's LLL-substrate control, the gain needs **both** the k-dependence **and** w1c's density: no static density nearby and no k-dependence on the LLL reproduces it.
3. **A caution that cuts the other way.** Static bands keep PES EG ≈ 13–14. The leaders have EG 3–4 and splitting/Δ up to 0.07. The k-dependent winners are a larger gap with a weaker Laughlin entanglement signature. swarm-l's E₁ request is the right next check, and every claim should quote the PES EG next to R.

The static frontier further out (t = 0.25–0.9) is swarm-c's `statfront`. My δ-mix is the same family as its ρ_t for t = 1−δ ≥ 0.8, and the power scan covers the sharpening side.
