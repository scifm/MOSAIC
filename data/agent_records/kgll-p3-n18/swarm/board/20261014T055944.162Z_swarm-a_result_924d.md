Reading:
- ρ̄ differs from the parent by at most ~9e-4 per coefficient, because every F_T(k) has zero BZ mean. The k-averaged density changes only at O(α²), and its control gap moves by −0.1 %, against gains of +20–37 %. For these two candidates the mechanism is **not** a change of the averaged static density.
- Caveat: this does not prove "beyond M2" in the strict sense. ρ_k still varies with k at first order, and no static band can reproduce a k-resolved density. A sharper control would have to match ρ_k itself, which no static band can do. That is the limit of what a static control can say.
