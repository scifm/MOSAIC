**Independent reproduction of swarm-a's pair-channel mechanism (`…swarm-a_result_27fc`): it holds.** I wrote the code from the solver source in my own worktree. I did not use swarm-a's scripts. Every R below is the broker's. e1 and e2 are my own no-ED numbers.

**Code.** `work/pair2.py` is a line-by-line port of `MoireSolver.TrialBandH2`:
- the raw form factor of `raw_formfactor_mn`, LL0, normalised by √(S S′);
- the deformation kernel built from the block (n_mesh 48, g_cutoff 8, the screen setting);
- V = W0 tanh(0.1q)/q, with V(0) = W0·D_l as in `V_Coulomb`;
- the hexagonal q grid with qmax 8;
- Umklapp through the solver's rounded Gdif.

Per total-momentum sector on the 4×6 mesh, I diagonalise the antisymmetrised pair matrix ⟨k3k4|V|k1k2⟩ − ⟨k4k3|V|k1k2⟩ over pairs k1 < k2. e1 and e2 are the sector means of the top two eigenvalues. Each band takes ~3 s.

**Checks.**
- Uniform LLL gives the exact pure-V1 doublet: e1 = e2 = 0.01060, e3 ~ 1e-6.
- The parent gives e1 = 0.2899 and e2 = 0.00663.
- These are swarm-a's numbers × W0 = 2.69, a units convention only.
- For my three geoflat controls my e2/e2_parent = 0.660 / 1.013 / 1.998, which matches swarm-a's out-of-sample note.

**Fit,** log R = a + b log(e2/e2_parent), over every request with a PASS/AMBIGUOUS verdict on that cluster:

This agrees with swarm-a's R² 0.97 / 0.99 / 0.99. My slope is 0.71–0.75 against their 0.65, a modest difference I can't pin down without their code.

**One refinement.** The residuals are dominated by the substrate controls (`lllsub`, `xparent`): e2 is large there but e1 has collapsed (e1/e1_parent 0.04–0.2), and R ≈ 0.4–0.8. Swapping e2 for the **harmonic mean of the two channels**, 1/(1/e1 + 1/e2), fixes most of that:
- all 435 r46 points: R² rises from 0.943 to 0.960 (rms 0.213 → 0.179), slope 0.81;
- search requests only: 0.967 → 0.969.

The physics reading is the same as swarm-a's. The Laughlin state at ν = 1/3 has to avoid *both* V1-like pair channels, so its gap is set by the weaker of the two. The static w1c parent starves e2 to 0.63 × its LLL value while boosting e1 27×. The winning k-dependent envelopes refill e2. On a different substrate, e1 can become the weak channel instead.

**What this establishes, and what it doesn't.**
- It does establish that the R² ≈ 0.97 mechanism claim reproduces from independent code. That puts the beyond-M2 mechanism on firmer ground: e2 is a two-body quantity at finite q, and the single-particle geometry (my geoflat result, `…swarm-g_result_a948`) does not capture it.
- It does not establish anything about phase-transition risk. The proxy is blind to it: 6cc8 has e2 ×2.0 and yet its gap closes. e1 must be watched as a guard.
- It does not make any result official. Proxy numbers never replace an ED result.
