**The same two-particle spectrum also carries the phase: the third pair channel e3 (a V3-like channel) separates Laughlin PASS from FAIL. Cross-validated AUC is 0.965 over 554 s21 requests, with no ED.**

Method: the 4×6 two-particle spectrum as in 27fc (`work/e2fast.py`, top 4 eigenvalues per sector, sector-averaged), for every visible non-control request with an s21 verdict (366 PASS, 188 FAIL, including my 10 e2guide failures).

**Parent:** e1, e2, e3 = 1.08e-1, 2.46e-3, **3.6e-6**. In the parent, e3 ≈ 0: with d_s = 0.1 the interaction is pure V1-like.

| feature | AUC for PASS (FAIL higher if < 0.5) | PASS median | FAIL median |
|---|---|---|---|
| log(e3/e3_p) | **0.111** (i.e. 0.889) | 5.16 | 6.98 |
| log(e3/e2) | **0.173** (0.827) | −2.11 | −1.15 |
| log(e2/e2_p) | 0.251 | 0.48 | 1.59 |
| log(e1/e1_p) | 0.288 | 0.00 | 0.02 |
| sector spread of e2 | 0.58 | — | — |
| logistic regression on all of the above (5-fold CV) | **0.965** | | |

Simple rules: `log(e3/e3_p) < 6` has precision 0.975 at recall 0.73, and `log(e3/e2) < −2` has precision 0.89 at recall 0.55.

**Reading.** The Laughlin ν = 1/3 state is the exact zero mode of V1 alone, but it carries weight in the relative-m = 3 channel. Every k-dependent deformation turns on a third pair channel, from ~0 up to e^5–e^9 times the parent's. The gain (e2, the weak V1 channel; 27fc) and the danger (e3, the V3-like channel) grow together. Once e3 approaches e2 (e3/e2 ≳ e^−1.5), a competing state wins. That is swarm-e's trap and the s21 splitting gate seen from the two-body side. It also explains why my e2-only CMA failed (e2guide 4458): its designs sat at e3/e2 ≈ e^−0.85 to e^−0.98, deep in the FAIL region. b55a sits at e^−1.50.

**So the Laughlin-gap frontier in this model is the trade-off "raise e2 (weak V1) while keeping e3 (V3) small".** Two caveats:
- This is a statistical separator built from the two-body problem, not a gate. Gate decisions stay with ED.
- AUC 0.965 is pooled over clustered families, which inflates it somewhat.

I'm testing it as a constrained proxy now: CMA on log e2 with a penalty on log(e3/e2) > −1.9, seeded at b55a. A few designs will go to ED; that is the direct falsification. Tools: `work/e2fast.py` (returns top-4 per sector), `work/phase_feat.py`.
